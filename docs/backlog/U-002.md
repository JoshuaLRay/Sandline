### Observed before the fix

`packages/server/src/session/stanceOrigin.test.ts` on the unfixed code (only the rig's new `crouchEyeHeight` number added):

- A crouched shot's origin on the wire was **1.55 m** above the feet (standing eye); prone was already right (0.41 m after wire rounding).
- Crouched behind a 1.3 m wall 2 m ahead, a round aimed at a standing enemy's chest **cleared the wall and hit it** (target netId 2000). The body was hidden to the head, but the gun was not.
- A crouched throw left from **1.55 m**, above the wall's top. Throws used the standing eye in every stance, prone included (`throwEye`).

### Change

- `MuzzleRig.crouchEyeHeight` is 1.15 m: the first-person camera's crouched pivot, inside the crouched head capsule (top about 1.29 m) and the 1.2 m crouch volume.
- `eyePosition` takes a body stance (`'standing' | 'crouched' | 'prone'`), with `eyeStance(crouched, prone)` and `stanceEye(body)` helpers.
- **Server:** a player's shot traces from the stance it fired in (rewound, like prone). Every throw, human and AI, launches from `throwEye(body)`, the thrower's stance eye.
- **Client:** the page's throw origin and arc, and its aim convergence, use the same stance eye, so prediction matches the server.
- **View:** a test pins the camera's standing, crouched and prone pivots to the rig, so the first-person crosshair and the round leave from one point.
- **Unchanged on purpose:** enemy and bot sight, the spawner's human-sight check, and AI crouched fire keep their previous eyes. AI crouched fire uses the cover body's 0.95 m crouched eye (T-3.19/T-3.20). Moving those would retune AI behaviour, which is outside this card; recorded here as a possible follow-up if it ever shows in play.
