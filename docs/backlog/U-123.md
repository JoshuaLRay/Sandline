## Implementation

- `packages/shared/src/sim/orderFeet.ts`: a pick's feet are the support under a
  point 5 cm in front of the hit face: a top keeps its height; a wall gives its
  foot; a ceiling gives the floor beneath; never the storey above.
- `packages/client/src/ui/orderPick.ts`: desktop crosshair and mobile tap share
  it; a ray that meets nothing falls back 30 m along it, on the floor under that
  point no higher than the eye. Marks still show the hit itself.
- `packages/server/src/ai/floor.ts`: `within(a, b, r)` (across ≤ r, height ≤ 1 m)
  for every order, follow, revive, kit and escort arrival, band and reach;
  `near(a, b, r)` (across ≤ r and height ≤ r) only for radii that choose cover,
  whose reachability the U-122 complete-path check already decides. A body
  mid-vault is judged by its takeoff floor, as U-122's cover planning does;
  formation trails record that height too.
- `Session.orderGoal`, 3D `reachable`, escort goal/nearest member; formation trail
  height; `OrderMarkers` line for a bot beneath its goal.

### Mission simulations (CI's three `sim-run` steps, run locally)

The simulations are seeded and deterministic, so they were compared per seed with
`main` (`6d4bfd2`) on the same machine, not only against the floors.

- A first version made arrival *and* cover-selection radii floor-strict. It moved
  completions 40% → 35% (1-human) and 5% → 0% (6-human): tracing the first
  diverging tick showed a bot mid-vault (feet ≈ 1 m up) counted as "another
  floor", and under-fire refuges on a vaultable platform refused. Both were
  over-reach, not stacked-floor behaviour, and were corrected (above).
- Final: all 40 `mission-01` runs are **tick-for-tick identical** to `main` in
  outcome and time (40% / 5%, floors 25% / 0%); `--all-missions --seeds 3` matches
  `main` (greybox 0/6, mission-01 1/6, Qalat 0/6); Qalat 20 seeds 0/40, the same
  `soldier-dead` failures `main` shows in CI #1698. These Qalat losses predate this task.
- The AI-cost gate is wall-clock: on this container `main` itself measured
  21.8–27.7% over six benches (mean ≈ 24.4%) and this branch 20.5–28.7% (≈ 24.1%),
  so a local run can cross the 25% ceiling either way. CI #1698 measured 11.0% for
  `main`; the CI value for this branch is recorded with the PR checks.
