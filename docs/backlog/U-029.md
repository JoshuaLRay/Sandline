# U-029 — Enforce squad weapon exchanges and left-handed loot access

Epic: **Squad**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: owner direction, 2026-09-27; extends the completed weapon pickup work in [U-018](U-018.md).

## Problem / outcome

The roster needs weapon exchange between characters and enemy weapon drops, with one exception: the left-handed sniper cannot use right-handed weapons or acquire firearms from another character or enemy. His left-handed firearms come from authored loot, including mission rewards.

## Scope

Extend U-018's server-authoritative pickup/equip flow so characters other than the left-handed sniper can exchange/pick up eligible primaries from squadmates and existing enemy drops. Preserve the weapon and ammunition exactly once through each transfer. Keep the left-handed sniper out of all character/enemy exchange paths; allow him to equip only weapons marked left-handed, sourced from authored loot/reward pickups. Prevent bypass through direct weapon selection, client input, bot control, possession switches, reconnect or stale pickup state.

U-020 supplies validated weapon handedness and left-handed variants. U-021 binds the left-handed identity to its slot. U-022 owns dual-primary slot limits and active-primary replacement.

## Controls (owner decision, 2026-09-29)

- **E picks up** the gun in reach. **If the character already holds a gun in that place, E swaps:** the held gun is exchanged for the one picked up, and the displaced gun becomes a world pickup by U-017/U-018's lifecycle. (U-018 already does this for E and enemy drops; this task extends the same press to squadmates' drops and applies the left-handed exclusion.)
- **G drops** the held gun as a world pickup with its current ammo and reload state, through the same host-authoritative, no-duplication path. The client only sends the press; no message names a pickup or carries rounds.
- **Conflict to resolve:** `G` is currently the grenade throw key (`THROW_KEY` in `packages/client/src/input/LocalInput.ts`, and the onboarding hint "G throws a grenade"). The owner's choice takes G for dropping, so the grenade throw must move to another key. **Decided 2026-09-29:** grenades move to slot 4 with swap-to-use (U-044/U-045), so `G` is freed for drop and no new grenade key (`T`) is needed. The key change itself lands in U-045; this task adds the drop. The implementing PR must update the key, the hint text and any docs together; the owner has not chosen the new grenade key.
- Open edge: whether the left-handed sniper may drop his own gun and pick it back up. Proposal: he may pick up a left-handed gun from the world, including one he dropped, but never a squadmate's or enemy's gun.

**Equipment is droppable too** (owner, 2026-09-29): `G` drops the item in hand whether it is a gun or equipment, by the same path; the equipment pickup rules belong to U-048.

## Handoff / evidence

Implemented on `task/U-029-drop-exchange`; awaiting review. No owner playtest verdict is claimed.

**What changed.** G is a new input button (`INPUT_BUTTONS.drop`, on the one frame it was pressed; no protocol layout change). The host's `dropHeld` puts the held primary on the ground where the soldier stands with its current rounds (a reload is cancelled), then draws the other primary, else the class's pistol, else the knife. Only a primary drops: the pistol and knife stay. A soldier can now carry no primary: the Weapon component's `primary` is `NO_SECONDARY` (15) then, and the page reads it as `NetClient.NO_PRIMARY` (-1), draws no primary on key 1 and adopts the gun the host drew. A respawn or a retry gives the class's primary back in hand. E on a dropped gun works for anyone, through U-018/U-022's rules (a swap when the hands are full; dual-primary eligibility). The left-handed sniper takes only a left-handed gun off the ground (all enemy and squadmate guns are right-handed, so none), and his own put down again.

**Evidence.** `packages/client/src/weapons/dropGun.test.ts` (end to end over loopback: drop with rounds, a squadmate takes it and nothing doubles, respawn, two primaries then knife, downed refused) and `packages/server/src/session/leftHandedLoot.test.ts`. `pnpm verify`, `check:assets`, `check:packs`, mission sim: see the PR.

**Proposals (labelled, reversible).** The pistol and knife cannot be dropped; the last primary can be. The left-handed sniper may pick a left-handed gun up, his own included. The grenade key was already freed by U-045; the drop uses G.
