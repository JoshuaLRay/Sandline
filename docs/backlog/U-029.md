# U-029 — Enforce squad weapon exchanges and left-handed loot access

Epic: **Squad**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: owner direction, 2026-09-27; extends the completed weapon pickup work in [U-018](U-018.md).

## Problem / outcome

The roster needs weapon exchange between characters and enemy weapon drops, with one exception: the left-handed sniper cannot use right-handed weapons or acquire firearms from another character or enemy. His left-handed firearms come from authored loot, including mission rewards.

## Scope

Extend U-018's server-authoritative pickup/equip flow so characters other than the left-handed sniper can exchange/pick up eligible primaries from squadmates and existing enemy drops. Preserve the weapon and ammunition exactly once through each transfer. Keep the left-handed sniper out of all character/enemy exchange paths; allow him to equip only weapons marked left-handed, sourced from authored loot/reward pickups. Prevent bypass through direct weapon selection, client input, bot control, possession switches, reconnect or stale pickup state.

U-020 supplies validated weapon handedness and left-handed variants. U-021 binds the left-handed identity to its slot. U-022 owns dual-primary slot limits and active-primary replacement.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Any non-left-handed character can take an eligible primary from a squadmate or an existing enemy weapon drop; the displaced primary follows the documented pickup lifecycle.
- [ ] Slot 4's left-handed sniper cannot take a firearm from a squadmate/enemy or use a right-handed firearm, even through forged/stale inputs, firing, bot possession, reconnect or character switching.
- [ ] Authored loot/reward sources can supply a left-handed firearm to slot 4; the initial loadout is the left-handed bolt-action sniper defined by U-020.
- [ ] Transfer is host-authoritative, atomic and non-duplicating, with ammo/reload state preserved according to the existing pickup rules.
- [ ] Interactions respect U-022's dual-primary eligibility and replacement rules, without permitting a second primary for other characters.
