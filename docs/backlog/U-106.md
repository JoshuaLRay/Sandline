### Automated geometry and mission evidence

- Three lane-contained nav/controller paths, north and south. Dense bidirectional
  samples along both separators verify path transitions occur only at C12 or hubs.
  Closing declared junction regions disconnects the lanes in the test navmesh.
- All C12 stairs pass the actual controller/path follower without vaulting, and
  the live rescued-POW AI walks all six crossing directions. Standing, jumping,
  crouched and prone attempts fail at separators, firing slots and the perimeter.
- Six soldiers plus POW cross the flank bottleneck in both directions at Tight,
  Standard and Wide, regrouping within 8 m of the command point. A Standard
  southbound fixture leaves the POW about 2.8 m short of the exact marker while
  everyone is already across and grouped; precise command arrival/congestion
  remains a human review point. This is distinct from the full-route escort
  checks, which require the POW within 1.5 m of every authored waypoint.
- Ray tests measure O1/O2/O3 road support, flank screening, an O1 middle-road
  blind spot, the upper east-gate angle and west-gate reward.
- Existing rescue/radio timing, tank drive/destruction, checkpoint restoration
  and all-seven extraction regressions retain their gates.
- Road width is 21 m to protect tank turning clearance; middle 8 m holding pocket
  extends to z=116. Northern hub is z=169.8..200 to avoid compound-wall embedding
  and protect the tank's spawn footprint. These measured blockout adjustments
  are recorded in the map document and annotated overview.
