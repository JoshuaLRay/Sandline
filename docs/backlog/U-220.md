## Problem / outcome

There is no way to look far without a scoped rifle. Outcome: binoculars as an
equipment item any character can carry (zoom view, mark through the existing
mark system), usable by bots on a recon order; later candidates (thermal,
laser designator for air support CP-38) evaluated with the loot item catalogue.
