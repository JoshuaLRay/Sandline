## Problem / outcome

U-150's scoping found U-149's accepted road too straight for three separate fights:
from the A4 bend a standing soldier saw straight down the tank's own 12 m lane to
the D0 court (`(-9,197)`→`(37,66)`, 138.8 m). Nothing may stand in that lane, so no
rock or cover could ever block that view, and the A2 and A4 fights were only 74 m
apart. The owner chose to bend the road at the fight boundaries, as the insertion's
added bend (U-138) did. Players should get three distinct fights (toll bend A2,
frontage A4/A5, forecourt A8), each seen only on arrival.

## Scope

Move or add a few road points in `qalat-road-supports.json`, keeping A2, A5, A6,
A8, the gate, both ridge crossings (C12-L from A2, C12-U from A5) and the buried
vault where they are, and regenerate U-149's supports, plan and captures. Add a
plan-view sight-line checker and its proofs. Update the construction specification
(addendum, §5.1 table, references to moved nodes) and hand displaced cover,
landmarks and sockets to U-150. No cover, terrain, shells, AI, X ingress,
campaign activation, caches or checkpoints.

The road shape is the owner's decision, so no Conflict default is needed. Design
note: with 36 m turning aprons at every bend, a bend must swing about 40 m sideways
before it hides anything, so small nudges cannot separate the fights.
