# U-076 — Spike: feasibility of a three-lane map about ten times mission-01's area

Epic: **Campaign design**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: owner decisions on the campaign design, 2026-10-01 ([design doc](../design/CAMPAIGN.md)).

## Problem / outcome

The owner wants three-lane missions of 30–45 minutes on maps about ten times the area of mission-01 (about 200 x 100 m today). Nothing in the engine has been measured at that size.

## Scope

A time-boxed spike that **measures and recommends; it does not build a mission.** Make a throwaway large level (grey-box boxes plus ground) of about 630 x 320 m with three lanes and measure: navmesh bake time and size (mission-01's is about 230 KB), path-query cost for 6 bots plus a full enemy cap, snapshot size and the relevance filter at that range, the 64-enemy cap against a map this long, client draw and load time (instancing of the kit, shadows, fog), the lightmap/bake step, and the headless sim's speed. Report numbers, the first thing that breaks, and a recommended size, spawn-in strategy (enemies are spawned by trigger and zone, not all at once) and any engine changes as proposed cards. Keep the throwaway level out of `main` (or clearly marked) and do not change gameplay.

## Start here

`packages/server/src/ai/nav` (bake), `packages/shared/src/sim/world.ts` and `levels`, `tools/src/level-check.ts`, `tools/src/bake-light.ts`, `sim-run --scenario crowd`, the relevance filter and `Netgraph`, the encounter spawner caps in `data/director.json`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A written report in the card: each measurement with its number and method.
- [x] A recommended map size and lane layout, and what must change in the engine for it.
- [x] Proposed follow-up cards (not implemented here) for anything that breaks.
- [x] No behaviour change for mission-01.

## Handoff / evidence

Done on `task/U-076-large-map-spike`. The measuring tool is `pnpm spike:large-map` (`packages/tools/src/spike-large-map.ts`); it commits nothing but itself. No gameplay changed.

**Sizing, corrected.** mission-01's play area is about 65 x 90 m = 5,800 m2, not its 200 x 200 m floor. "Ten times" is therefore about 58,000 m2: three 40 m lanes by about 480 m. The test world is three lanes of 40 m (120 m wide) by 250 m, 480 m and 960 m (5x, 10x, 20x), with cover boxes to mission-01's density, walls between the lanes with a gap every 80 m, and a compound at the end. Load: a human, five bots and 40 riflemen (the AI cost bench's load), 300 ticks, spread along the map or packed round the squad. Node 22.

| Length | Play area | Boxes | Navmesh bake (bare, tight floor) | Navmesh size | Cover points | Path across | Server step, spread / packed | Same, no cover points |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| mission-01 | 5,800 m2 | 34 + kit | 5.0 s (two bakes) | 231 KB | 728 | — | about 7.9 ms | — |
| 250 m | 5.2x | 190 | 1.9 s | 341 KB | 3,608 | 181 us | **27.8 / 14.7 ms** | 2.5 / 2.5 ms |
| 480 m | 9.9x | 357 | 2.5 s | 601 KB | 6,768 | 372 us | **82.6 / 111.1 ms** | 2.8 / 3.3 ms |
| 960 m | 19.9x | 708 | 5.2 s | 1,181 KB | 13,510 | 387 us | **268.5 / 295.0 ms** | 2.0 / 4.0 ms |

(The budget is 33.3 ms a tick.) Bytes to one client are about 1.2 KB a tick (about 300 kbit/s) at every size, and less when the enemies are spread (119 kbit/s at 960 m): the relevance radius (120 m) holds the snapshot flat.

**What breaks, in order.**
1. **The cover search scales with the whole map, and blows the tick budget at about 4x.** It is the only thing that did. With the cover points removed the same sessions cost 2–4 ms at every size, so the cost is the cover search and not enemies, boxes, nav or snapshots. Cause (`server/src/ai/cover.ts`, `sightFrom`): for each distinct threat eye position (a moving soldier is a new one every tick) it traces `protects(...)` for **every cover point on the map** against every box before the search narrows to the points near the asker, so a tick costs points x boxes x threats (6,768 x 357 at 480 m). Proposed: compute `protects` lazily for the points that pass the distance prefilter, and index the points in a grid. Card **U-081**; this must land before any map above about 4x.
2. **The navmesh bake fails on a long map with the world's square floor.** A floor is a square of `floor.halfExtent`, centred on the origin, so a 120 x 480 m map needs a 1,000 x 1,000 m floor: 100 million voxels, and at 480 m the bake **fails** ("Failed to create Detour navmesh data"); at 250 m it works but takes 10 s (the bake is run twice, for the vault links). Baked over a floor cut to the lanes the same map is 6 million voxels, 2.5 s, a 601 KB mesh. Proposed: a rectangular floor (`halfWidth`/`halfDepth`) used by the bake, the collision floor and the renderer. Card **U-082**.
3. **The baked mesh grows with the map** (1.2 MB at 20x, about 1.6 MB as the base64 the in-page session carries): fine for a host, worth watching for the page's load budget (`check:load-time`).

**Fine as it is, to 20x:** path queries (under 0.4 ms across the whole map), the cover bake itself (under 0.15 s), snapshot size, the box count in the character controller (the no-cover step stays under 4 ms at 708 boxes), and the enemy caps for a 30–45 minute pace (64 alive at once, spawned by trigger and zone, not all at the start).

**Not measured here** (needs a browser; owed if the owner wants them): client draw and load time for a map this size (instancing the kit, shadows, fog, the lightmap bake in `bake-light.ts`), and play with real combat on a big map (this load is the bench's, enemies idle and the human walking).

**Recommendation.** A three-lane map of about 40 m x 3 x 480 m (10x) is feasible once U-081 and U-082 are done; the design doc's earlier estimate of 630 x 320 m was wrong (it took the floor, not the play area, as mission-01's size). Until U-081 lands, keep a map under about 4x (about 25,000 m2).
