# U-097 — Give the Qalat valley connected, playable elevation

Epic: Map design. Status, priority, dependencies and branch/PR live in BACKLOG.md.
Source: owner feedback, 2026-10-03: “The map needs more art, it still feels like walking on a grid. It needs more verticality as well, rather than being just a flat plain.”

## Problem / outcome

The owner finds the current map too flat. U-092 delivered a first playable valley with isolated stair/deck overlooks; that does not constitute acceptance of its terrain design. Exact camera position, URL and build in the feedback are UNKNOWN. Make elevation a continuous part of moving through the three lanes, with readable differences in cover and sight lines.

## Scope

One focused Qalat level pass using the existing kit and supported collision/nav primitives: a lower riverbed, raised road banks and connected stepped eastern terraces, with elevation through the compound approach. Preserve the approved footprint, three routes, mission triggers and tank return route. Heights and placement are implementation proposals to verify in play. Do not introduce a general heightfield system or change combat difficulty here; if existing primitives cannot deliver connected terrain, record and split that prerequisite before expanding scope. Final surface dressing is U-096.

## Start here

`packages/shared/src/data/levels/qalat-road.json`, U-092's level/nav tests and evidence, `docs/design/MISSION-01.md`, and `docs/COMMANDS.md` (Adding a mission). Check existing kit stairs, decks and collision support before choosing geometry.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each lane has traversable height changes integrated into its route; the riverbed is visibly lower and the eastern terraces provide connected higher ground rather than isolated platforms.
- [ ] Elevation changes cover or sight lines at the riverbed, road bank and terrace/compound approach, documented in actual gameplay views.
- [ ] Players, bots and the escorted POW can traverse all three lanes in both directions without floating, falling through geometry or getting stranded.
- [ ] Rescue, escort, tank arrival/destruction and all-seven extraction remain functional; tank route clearance is preserved.
- [ ] Regenerated nav, level checks, asset/pack budgets and required CI pass.
- [ ] Owner reviews actual gameplay screenshots and a walk-through; no visual verdict is inferred from CI.

## Verification

Targeted bidirectional route and raised-ground collision/nav tests, authored-level checks, regenerated navigation, asset/pack checks and shared delivery gates. Capture real client views at ground level and elevated viewpoints; manually traverse transitions and exercise escort and tank scenarios. Re-run the existing mission baseline and report changes honestly without weakening gates.

Not started. Follow-up to U-092 and U-065; owner feedback requests a new terrain pass, not acceptance of the previous map. Proposed execution order: U-097, then U-096.
