## Problem / outcome

Qalat's isolation and route proofs were written for its three separated
lanes. Future maps may have two, four or braided approaches and declare
separation only where they mean it. Outcome: a data-driven check that reads a
map's declared approaches (waypoints/regions), its declared separations and
crossings, and verifies: every approach is navigable end to end and back by a
bot and the escort; every declared separation has no undeclared adjacency;
every declared crossing exists. Qalat's and Kestrel's records become inputs to
the same check without changing their geometry.

## Scope

- A small approach-graph schema in the map data (or beside it), validated by
  zod; a tool and test that runs the nav queries.
- Port Qalat's existing isolation proofs to the declared-graph form (proofs
  stay equivalent; no geometry change).
- `docs/COMMANDS.md` row.
