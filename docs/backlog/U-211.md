# U-211 — Silent knife takedown on unaware enemies

Epic: Stealth and enemy awareness ([U-204](U-204.md)). Pillar: P4.
Size: S–M. Human verdict: batched in U-212.
Status, priority, dependencies and branch/PR live in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: parity CP-19 (series: silent knife kills on sentries), within the owner's 2026-10-16 stealth epic.

## Problem / outcome

The knife does 40 damage at 2 m and makes no shot sound, but cannot silently
remove a sentry. Outcome (Conflict default): a knife attack on an Unaware or
Suspicious enemy from outside its view cone kills it silently (no warning, an
impact-free stimulus); against an Alerted or Combat enemy, or from the front,
the knife does its normal damage.

## Scope

- Host rule in the melee path using U-205's state and the target's facing.
- Bots: an ordered attack on an unaware enemy with the knife (proposal; via
  U-198's equipment orders or later).
- Presentation: existing melee animation; no new art required.
