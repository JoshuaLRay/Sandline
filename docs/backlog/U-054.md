# U-054 — C4 for the Support: throw it, place it, detonate it

Epic: **Controls**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [U-048](U-048.md), owner design decisions 2026-09-30.

## Problem / outcome

The Support carries C4. Owner decisions (2026-09-29 and 2026-09-30): only the Support can **throw** it; everyone else must **place** it. Hold left click to aim and release to throw; right click detonates. Numbers "fine" (2026-09-30).

## Scope


A new server-authoritative **placed** explosive (`kind: "placed"` in `projectiles.json`) with these rules (the owner accepted them as proposed):

- **Carried:** 2 charges (Support only, from `classes.json` `equipment`).
- **Throw (Support):** flies like a grenade but does not bounce: it **sticks** to the first surface it touches and arms.
- **Place (anyone who has one):** aim at a surface within 2.5 m, left click; it arms at once. No timer.
- **Detonate:** right click detonates **all** the owner's armed charges at once, from anywhere, while the owner is alive and not downed.
- **Pick up:** E on your own unexploded charge takes it back into the pouch.
- **Blast:** radius 4 m, damage 250 at the centre falling to 30% at the edge (`blastMinFraction` 0.3), the same cover rules as the frag. Friendly fire is the same as the frag's: it hurts everyone including the owner.
- **Owner dies:** charges stay armed but cannot be detonated until the owner respawns; a checkpoint retry clears them all and refills the pouch; an uncollected charge expires after 5 minutes.
- **Dropped C4** (G) is a world pickup, not an armed charge.
- Enemies ignore C4 in this version; bots do not use equipment yet.

Reuses U-046's right-click press (`cook` input) as the detonate press and U-048's equipment item and drop lifecycle. A destructible world does not exist, so C4 damages soldiers only.

- [x] Right click detonates every armed charge of the owner, and only the owner's; a downed or dead owner cannot.
- [x] Damage, radius and friendly fire as above, with the owner's XP credit for enemy kills.
- [x] E picks up your own charge; retry clears charges; expiry works.
- [x] Host-authoritative, replicated to all pages (the placed charge is visible), tested headless.
