# U-010 — Let enemies interrupt uploads by using the lever

Epic: **Mission**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [owner feedback, 2026-09-26](2026-09-26-feedback.md), plus any legacy bug named below.

## Problem / outcome

Protecting an upload should mean defending the equipment from enemy action, with freedom to position around it.

## Scope

Give configured enemies a reachable lever-use objective that interrupts the upload through the same authoritative interaction rules. Retain combat behavior and allow the squad to prevent the use.

These are entry points, not a prescribed edit list; verify current code before changing it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] An assigned enemy navigates to the lever and performs a visible, interruptible use action; merely entering an area does not sabotage.
- [x] Killing/downing/displacing the user before completion prevents sabotage; unreachable paths release reservations and recover.
- [x] A successful use interrupts the upload exactly once, replicated to every client; an ally can resume via U-009.
- [x] Enemy assignments and lever state recover on checkpoint retry without duplicate users or deadlocks.

### Design

- **Data** (`shared/src/sim/mission.ts`): an upload objective may carry a `lever`:
  - `at`, a point;
  - `reachM`, at most 3 m, a hand's reach like the terminal's;
  - `useSeconds`;
  - `group`, the encounter group whose members may pull it.

  Keys are validated by name. `checkMission` refuses a lever inside a wall or naming a group the encounter lacks. No committed mission has one yet; U-011 authors content.
- **Assignment** (the session, `Session.updateLever`). While an upload with a lever is running, and only then, one enemy at a time gets the job: the nearest living member of the group who is not on a gun, not barred, and whom the navmesh can take to the lever (the path ends within 1 m of it).
  - A member with no way there is barred for `LEVER_BAR_SECONDS` (15 s), and the next nearest is tried.
  - A user whose path following reports `unreachable` while not yet at the lever is released and barred in the same way. `driveBots` now keeps each enemy's path status.
  - Nobody holds the job while nobody can get there, so nothing waits on it.
  - When the upload stops running (cut, restarted, completed or failed), the job is released.
- **Behaviour** (`ai/actions/lever.ts`, first branch of the rifleman and MG trees: `hasLeverJob → useLever`):
  - The user walks to the lever, sprinting while far, and still shoots its target on the way, which keeps its combat behaviour.
  - Well inside reach it stops, kneels, faces the lever, takes its hands off the trigger and holds interact, as a friendly bot's revive does.
- **The pull is the session's to time**, like a revive. It counts only while the user is alive and holding, within `reachM` of its eye with a clear line to the lever: the rules a soldier's press at the terminal meets (U-009).
  - Merely being near is nothing, since there is no hold without the job and the leaf.
  - Anything else sets the pull back to zero: killed, pushed or walked off, out of reach, or blocked. Enemies are not downable, so death is the stop.
- **A pull that lands** calls `interruptUpload` once, which does nothing to an upload already stopped. It broadcasts the Mission and tells everyone "The upload was cut at the lever", then frees the lever until an ally restarts the upload at the terminal (U-009), with its progress kept by the default `keep-progress`.
- **Visible** (protocol 39):
  - the user's replicated Health carries the pull, as a percentage, in the slot a squadmate's revive uses;
  - `NetClient.leverPull()` reads it;
  - the HUD objective line adds `ENEMY AT THE LEVER 40% — stop them` while the upload runs;
  - the user is seen kneeling.
  - There is no lever model yet; U-011 dresses the content.
- **Retry:** `resetMissionWorld` clears the user and the bars with the enemies. The retried upload waits idle, and the re-sent group is assigned afresh once it runs again.

### Verification

- **`server/src/session/lever.test.ts`:** a real `Session` on greybox-01 with its baked navmesh and cover and the enemy's own trees, a human over loopback, and the upload started at its terminal.
  - **Successful use:**
    - nobody is sent while the upload is idle;
    - one enemy walks about 16 m to the lever and kneels there;
    - the pull counts only once it holds the lever;
    - there is only ever one user;
    - halfway through, every client sees at least 40% on that enemy and 0 on the rest;
    - the upload is cut exactly once (one `interrupted` Mission, one message) and stays cut; nobody is sent to a stopped upload, and its pull reads 0;
    - an ally's restart keeps the progress, and the enemy is sent again.
  - **Interrupted use:**
    - pushed 5 m off mid-pull, the user's pull resets to 0 and it has to start over;
    - shot mid-pull, the upload runs on and the other member is sent from 0;
    - with both dead, nobody is sent and the upload is safe.
  - **Blocked path:** with a lever nobody can walk to, each member is tried and barred; after the bar they are tried and barred again; the upload runs to completion uncut.
  - **Retry:**
    - wiped mid-pull, the mission fails and the lever is freed;
    - P retries at the checkpoint: the upload is idle, there is no user, and the group is sent again;
    - restarted, one user (a set of users of size 1) cuts it exactly once.
- **`shared/src/sim/mission.test.ts`:** the lever parses, each bad field is refused by name, and a lever inside a wall or with an unknown group is refused.
- **`client/src/ui/missionHud.test.ts`:** the warning appears only while the upload runs.
- Protocol pins moved to 39.
- `pnpm verify`: 197 files, 2127 tests, all passed.
- `pnpm bot --count 2 --ticks 600`: OK.
- `pnpm sim-run --scenario mission --seeds 3`: every threshold met, with AI cost at 11.9% of the tick. The enemies' new first branch does nothing without an upload.
- **The "solo-with-bots demonstration"** is the headless scenario above: one human, the enemy's own AI on the baked map. No video or in-page recording was made, because no committed mission has a lever to play (U-011).
