## Problem / outcome

There is no explicit awareness state: an enemy has a threat (combat) or is at
ease. Outcome: every enemy brain carries one of five states, changed only by
the host from perception, memory and (later) communication, with data-driven
thresholds and timers, exposed for behaviour, debugging and the indicator
(U-209). Behaviour in this card: the minimum per state (Unaware keeps today's
postures; Suspicious stops and turns toward the stimulus; Alerted takes cover;
Combat is today's combat tree); investigation and search behaviour is U-207.

## Scope

- `packages/shared/src/ai/awareness.ts` (pure, deterministic): state, the
  transition function from the awareness meter, stimuli and memory, timers;
  `packages/shared/src/data/awareness.json` (zod) with per-archetype overrides
  (the tank and POW exempt or fixed).
- The sight meter's lower "suspicion" threshold (default 0.3) below today's
  detection (0.7); Wary flag after combat.
- Server: state held per enemy, updated in `perceive()`; checkpoint restore
  starts enemies Unaware or Wary as their group was (proposal).
- AiDebug report (T-3.09) includes the state.
- No wire field yet (U-209 adds it).
