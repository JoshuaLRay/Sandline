# U-202 — Synchronised orders: plan several moves and release them together

Epic: Squad command reliability ([U-203](U-203.md)). Pillar: P2.
Size: L (split UI and host at claim time). Human verdict: batched in U-203's verification.
Status, priority, dependencies and branch/PR live in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: owner, 2026-10-16 — "Research synchronized commands inspired by Conflict: Global Storm … players could prepare multiple orders and execute them together to coordinate an assault. Document and scope this as a future enhancement, but prioritize basic command reliability first." Parity CP-48, gap G-15.

## Problem / outcome

Global Storm let the player give squadmates sequenced "advanced orders" and
release them on a signal (*unverified* secondary sources, ADR-022). Sandline
orders apply at once and replace the previous one. Outcome: a commander can
**prepare** orders for several soldiers or fireteams (move here facing there,
hold, plant, attack this), see them as pending markers, and **execute** them
together with one input ("go"), so a breach, a pincer or a covered advance
starts on one beat; any soldier can also be given a short queue (move, then
hold, then attack).

## Scope

- Pending orders per soldier (a short queue, e.g. up to three steps), shown as
  dimmed markers; "execute" releases all pending orders of soldiers the
  commander commands; "cancel" clears them.
- Host-authoritative; pending orders survive switches/leaves per U-196.
- Desktop: a modifier on the order wheel to prepare instead of issue; mobile:
  a prepare toggle and a Go button.
- Bots and humans: only bots execute orders; a human in a slot is shown the
  plan but drives themselves.
