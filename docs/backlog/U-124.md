# U-124 — Bake the kit gallery's house and roof stair into its navmesh

Epic: Map implementation. Status, priority, dependencies and branch/PR live in BACKLOG.md.
Source: found while auditing U-123 on 2026-10-05 (agent observation, not an owner report).

## Problem / outcome

Observed on `main` at `6d4bfd2` with the committed `kit-gallery` bake: the house
the kit gallery builds "with a stair to its roof, all walkable" (`docs/COMMANDS.md`,
T-4.10) has no navmesh on its interior floor, its doorstep or its stair. The roof
top alone is baked (y 3.30 at x/z 1, 44). A path from `(0, 0, 36)` to the roof ends
at `(0, 0.05, 39.5)`; a path to the interior floor is null.

Observation: the level's floor is `halfExtent: 40`, and the house spans z ≈ 41.7–46.3,
outside that baked ground square. Humans can still walk there, because the controller's
ground plane is unbounded; bots cannot path there. Hypothesis (unverified): the floor
extent, not the stacked-floor bake, is the cause.

Expected: bots can enter the house and climb the stair to its roof, so the gallery is
a committed stacked-floor review place for U-123's commands (floor y0 versus roof y3.25).

## Scope

Fix the gallery content or floor extent so the house, doorway and stair are baked; regenerate
the kit-gallery nav and cover. Do not change the nav algorithm or other worlds.

## Start here

`packages/shared/src/data/levels/kit-gallery.json` (`floor`, `house-*` pieces);
`pnpm gen:nav`; `packages/server/src/ai/nav/baked/kit-gallery.ts`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A path from open ground reaches the house interior floor and, by the stair, the roof top.
- [ ] A real-session move order to the roof arrives at y ≈ 3.25; one to the interior floor beneath it arrives at y ≈ 0.
- [ ] Regenerated bakes are committed, and `pnpm verify` and latest-head CI pass.

## Verification

Focused nav/session regression on the committed gallery bake, then the owner can use
`?kit` to order a bot onto the roof and back inside (U-123 review steps).
