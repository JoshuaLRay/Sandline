# U-062 — Enemy capture behaviour: the 2 s rule, the 5 s channel, interruption

Epic: **Campaign**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [U-032](U-032.md), owner decisions 2026-09-30 (all the proposed defaults, except that capture is allowed after 2 s down rather than 10 s).

## Problem / outcome

An enemy with nothing to shoot can take a downed character prisoner instead of leaving them, per [U-032](U-032.md).

## Scope

- Eligibility (owner, 2026-09-30): the character has been downed at least **2 s**, no living squadmate is within **15 m**, and at least one squadmate is up anywhere (a wipe is a failure, not a capture). Tune values live in schema-validated data (`capture` in `damage.json` or its own file), not in code.
- Behaviour: an enemy with no living squad target walks to an eligible downed character (nearest first, one capturer per character) and channels for **5 s**. On completion it calls `captureCharacter` (U-061). The channel must finish inside the 30 s bleed-out; an eligibility check that could not finish in time is refused.
- Interruption: the capturer dying, being suppressed past a threshold, or losing the path cancels the channel; a revive (3 s) by a squadmate cancels it and restores the character normally. A cancelled channel starts over with no progress kept.
- Bots: a bot that sees a capture in progress treats the capturer as a priority target and a revive as a priority task.
- It must not re-enable shooting a downed character (U-031 stays).

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] An enemy captures an eligible downed character in 5 s and the character leaves the map.
- [x] Not eligible before 2 s down, with a squadmate within 15 m, when the capturer is being shot at to the threshold, or when the squad has no one up.
- [x] A kill, suppression, path loss or a revive cancels the channel; a revive restores the character normally.
- [x] Enemies still never fire at a downed character (U-031's tests still pass).
- [x] Deterministic AI tests cover eligibility, completion, each interruption, and the wipe case.

## Handoff / evidence

Implemented on `task/U-062-capture-rules`; awaiting review. No owner playtest verdict is claimed.

**What changed.**
- `damage.json` gains a schema-validated `capture` block (`downedMinSeconds` 2, `squadmateRadiusM` 15, `channelSeconds` 5, `reachM` 1.5, `suppression` 0.25, `retrySeconds` 6, `approachSpeedMps` 4); `parseDamageConfig` range-checks it.
- `Session.updateCaptures` (after the lever, before enemies step): every 0.5 s it sends the nearest able enemy — alive, not mounted, **no target**, not the lever's user, not barred — to each eligible downed character (one capturer each), when a nav path reaches them and `walk / approachSpeedMps + channelSeconds` fits in what is left of the bleed-out. The session times the hold (enemy within `reachM`, brain holding `interact`) and on completion calls `captureCharacter` (U-061).
- Eligibility, re-checked every tick: downed ≥ 2 s, no squadmate who is **up** within 15 m, someone up anywhere (a captured one does not count). A squadmate arriving therefore cancels the hold, and a revive (which needs one) lands exactly as before.
- Interruption drops the job and its progress: capturer dead or mounted, suppression ≥ threshold, it finds a target, path `unreachable`, sent to the lever, or the hold can no longer finish inside the bleed-out. An interrupted capturer is barred for `retrySeconds`.
- Brain: `hasCaptureJob` / `holdPrisoner` leaves (`ai/actions/capture.ts`, from the lever's shape) in the `rifleman` tree, after the lever and before `hasThreat`. It never fires at the downed character (U-031's rule is untouched); the walk shoots only at its existing target, and it has none.
- Bots: `botTarget` returns a capturer the bot knows of once a hold has begun; `downedNear` ranks a squadmate being held above any other and ignores `reviveSeekM` for them.

**Decisions to confirm.** (1) Only the `rifleman` tree captures; the MG gunner stays on his gun. (2) "Capture in progress" for the bots means the hold has begun (the capturer is at the character), not that one is merely walking there. (3) The 15 m test counts squadmates who are up, not downed ones. (4) `approachSpeedMps` is an addition so "could not finish in time" covers the walk as well as the hold. (5) A capture that has begun is not announced or shown: that is U-064.
