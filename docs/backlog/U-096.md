# U-096 — Give the Qalat map an authored art pass and remove its grid feel

Epic: Map art. Status, priority, dependencies and branch/PR live in BACKLOG.md.
Source: owner feedback, 2026-10-03: “The map needs more art, it still feels like walking on a grid. It needs more verticality as well, rather than being just a flat plain.”

## Problem / outcome

The owner reports too little map art and the feeling of walking on a grid. Exact camera position, URL and build are UNKNOWN. The client currently adds a GridHelper in `packages/client/src/main.ts`; whether that explains all of the reported feel is unverified. Removing an overlay alone does not satisfy the requested art pass. U-092's first playable kit layout needs a visually authored follow-up.

## Scope

One focused art pass over the terrain completed in U-097: distinguish dry riverbed, dirt road, field terraces and compound using ground colour/material variation, irregular edges, rocks, banks, sparse vegetation and appropriate environmental detail. Break up repeated straight rows and uniform bare surfaces while retaining readable routes and cover. Restrict diagnostic grid rendering to explicitly requested QA views. Use the existing procedural art pipeline and locked asset decisions; no commissioned assets, new runtime dependency or combat rebalance is implied.

## Start here

`packages/client/src/main.ts`, `packages/shared/src/data/levels/qalat-road.json`, `packages/tools/src/art`, `docs/design/MISSION-01.md`, U-092 and U-097; relevant procedural art decisions in ADR-018/ADR-020. Change source generators and regenerate outputs as needed.

## Handoff / evidence

Implemented on `task/U-096-qalat-art`, using U-097's merged terrain (#281, merge `7c22e84`). On 2026-10-03 the owner explicitly requested continuation after granting full access; this authorizes using the technical terrain baseline, not inventing U-097 or U-096 visual acceptance. Both owner quality verdicts remain pending.

The art generator is `packages/tools/src/art/pieces/qalat.ts`. A single terrain skin follows the existing floor and every U-097 supporting plane, with grey dry-channel gravel, a worn two-rut dirt road, olive-brown winter field stubble and compound paving. Seeded broad colour variation gives the channel and fields wandering edges. Earth retaining faces have baked darkening; large surfaces retain their palette through the client AO path. Qalat uses smooth texture filtering and winter haze. This replaces 248 repeated ground decals; all existing high/low cover and mission data are retained.

Sparse seeded stone/scrub clusters stay outside the central 26 m corridor, away from existing cover, and on one supporting plane. Cosmetic fragments reach at most 0.29 m; twelve faceted bank rocks have matching 0.4 m-high collision, below the 0.45 m character step. The existing tank road, terrain elevations, mission triggers, encounter tuning and campaign state are retained. Navigation is regenerated: 883,336 bytes and 5,469 cover points (1,386 low / 4,083 high).

`?grid` explicitly requests the diagnostic helper in any world; it is absent by default. `?review-map` remains the explicit capture camera and now reports actual renderer draw calls/grid state. The capture tool covers an overview, both compound views, all three lanes, upper/lower terraces and the north descent. It asserts default grid-off, explicit grid-on and the 300-call ceiling, and writes `measurements.json`. Streaming CI uploads these real-client images and measurements as `qalat-map-review`.
