# U-032 — Capture downed characters as prisoners for next-mission rescue

Epic: **Campaign**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [owner feedback, 2026-09-27](2026-09-27-feedback.md).

## Problem / outcome

The owner wants an enemy to be able to capture a downed player character as a prisoner of war (PoW), with that character requiring rescue in the next mission. This is a new campaign mechanic; capture conditions, rescue objective details and persistence rules have not been specified.

## Scope

Decompose and implement an enemy capture interaction for a downed player character, persist the captured status into the next mission, and make that character recoverable through a rescue. A captured character is not killed by the capture and must not be available as a normal squad character before rescue. Define interruption, mission retry/checkpoint, and rescue success/failure behavior before treating the work as READY. Do not implement capture by allowing enemies to fire on a downed character; see U-031.

## Owner decisions (2026-09-30)

The owner accepted every proposed default, with one change: a downed character can be captured after **2 seconds** down (not 10).

1. **Eligibility.** Only a downed character, downed at least **2 s**, with **no living squadmate within 15 m**. A living or a dead character cannot be captured.
2. **Capture.** An enemy with no living squad target reaches the downed character and **channels for 5 s** ("binding"). The character then leaves the map. The channel finishes inside the 30 s bleed-out, so they are captured, not dead.
3. **Interruption.** Killing or suppressing the capturer, or a revive (3 s), cancels the channel; a revive during it restores the character normally.
4. **Wipe.** If the whole squad is down or dead it is a mission failure as today; there is no prisoner. Capture only matters while at least one squadmate is up.
5. **Retry and restart.** A retry goes back to the last checkpoint (checkpoints are not saved while a character is downed, U-059), where nobody was captured. A full restart clears every prisoner. **Completing a mission keeps them** captured for the next.
6. **Unavailable while captured.** The captured character is greyed out in the slot picker and cannot be played; a bot or player takes another free slot. They keep their rank and XP.
7. **Rescue.** A new `rescue` objective in a following mission: reach the prisoner's location and hold E for **5 s** beside them, with the usual guards. It needs a mission to exist for it (U-065).
8. **Outcomes.** Success restores the character to the squad with rank and XP. Failure (mission failed or left) leaves them captured for the next attempt. No permanent loss, and no extra penalty for a second failed rescue.
9. **Persistence.** `captured` and a prisoner location per soldier in the campaign file, as an additive optional field (the U-060 approach).
