# U-157 — RPG gunner: data, brain and authoritative rockets

Epic: Enemy AI (Conflict parity). Pillar: P4 / P7. Parent: [U-155](U-155.md).
Size: M. Human verdict: batched in U-119 (Mission 1 review) once placed in a mission.
Status, priority, dependencies and branch/PR live in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).

## Problem / outcome

U-155's fighting half: an `rpg` archetype that the server spawns, thinks for
and fires rockets with, replicated so a client can tell what it holds and when
it is winding up. Drawing it (the RPG-7 in hand, the wind-up, the trail) is
[U-158](U-158.md).

## Scope

Conflict default (ADR-021): RPG troops are the enemy that punishes a squad
bunched up or dug in, and their rockets are slow and visible, so moving in time
is the counterplay. Adapted:

- `rpg` row in `enemies.json`: the enemy rifle (`carbine`) and its own
  perception and accuracy; the `launcher` block grows its tuning — rockets
  carried, medium range band, wind-up, long reload, how many soldiers make a
  group, safety margin, how far it moves after a shot.
- Its own tree (`trees/rpg.json`) and leaves (`server/src/ai/actions/rpg.ts`):
  inside the band's near edge (or out of rockets) it fights as a rifleman with
  its rifle; otherwise it holds the launcher, takes and keeps cover, and fires
  at a target it knows has been still, in the band, that is behind cover or
  has another squad member within the blast. The shot is searched through
  `projectileArc` from the launch the session will make, aiming high for the
  drop; it is refused when the burst or the flight passes a friend or itself.
  It stands for `tellSeconds` (replicated as `aiming`) at a locked point, checks
  friends again, launches, and then relocates to other cover. No rifle fire
  while the launcher is in hand.
- Session: rockets from the launcher row in its pouch; the launch goes through
  the throw path, then the long reload; a shot stimulus; the `Enemy` component
  gains a `launcher` bit (what is in its hands). `PROTOCOL_VERSION` 72.
- Groups never give it the suppressor or flanker role (its tree has neither).
- `?enemies=rpg` puts one on the QA range; encounters may name it.
- A seeded `sim-run --scenario rpg` (bots against riflemen and RPG gunners).

Out of scope: the drawn RPG-7, wind-up and trail (U-158); placement in missions
(U-117); bots dodging a locked rocket (they react as to any blast).

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] `rpg` validates, spawns from an encounter and replicates (archetype,
      `aiming` in the wind-up, `launcher` while the launcher is in hand).
- [x] It fires rockets only in its band, at still targets in cover or groups,
      after the wind-up, never with a friend in the blast or the flight path.
- [x] It reloads slowly, relocates after firing and uses its rifle up close.
- [x] Squad bots survive and fight it in a seeded scenario with asserted floors.
- [x] Existing mission scenario floors are unchanged (no RPG placed in them).
