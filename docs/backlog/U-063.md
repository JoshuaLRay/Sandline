# U-063 — The `rescue` objective type

Epic: **Campaign**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [U-032](U-032.md), owner decisions 2026-09-30 (all the proposed defaults, except that capture is allowed after 2 s down rather than 10 s).

## Problem / outcome

A mission can ask the squad to free a prisoner, per [U-032](U-032.md).

## Scope

- New objective `rescue` in the mission format (`MissionDef`/`ObjectiveDef`, schema-validated, parsed in `@sandline/shared`): `prisoner` (a slot's prisoner location from the campaign, or a named place), `holdSeconds` (default 5), `reachM`.
- Server: holding interact within `reachM` of the prisoner for `holdSeconds` completes it and calls `freeCharacter` (U-061); the progress is interrupted if the holder is downed or leaves; any living squadmate can do it; the usual guards are the mission's own groups.
- The character is placed at the prisoner location, then joins the squad there with rank and XP. A failed or abandoned mission leaves them captured.
- HUD: objective line and progress through the existing mission messages.
- Wire: only what the existing Mission message already carries (type, progress, goal); bump `PROTOCOL_VERSION` only if a field is added.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A `rescue` objective parses and validates; a bad one is refused with a clear error.
- [x] Holding interact beside the prisoner for the hold time frees them and completes the objective; letting go, leaving or being downed resets the hold.
- [x] A freed character is playable again with rank and XP; a failed mission leaves them captured.
- [x] Mission tests cover success, reset, failure and the no-prisoner case (the objective completes at once or is refused, decided in the card's PR).

## Handoff / evidence

Implemented on `task/U-063-rescue`; awaiting review. No owner playtest verdict is claimed.

**What changed.** A `rescue` objective type (`label`, `holdSeconds`, `reachM` up to 3 m, optional `slot` 0–5 to name the prisoner; without it, any one). The prisoner's place is their own, saved with the campaign (U-061), so the mission names no position. A standing squad soldier (alive, not downed, not a prisoner) within `reachM` of a held prisoner, with a clear line to them, holding interact for `holdSeconds` frees them; the nearest prisoner to the first such soldier in slot order is the one, and a change of prisoner, letting go, leaving reach or going down starts the hold over. The Support's shorter interaction time (U-049) applies (5 s becomes 4 s). On completion the session calls `freeCharacter`, so they stand at the place they were held, whole, with rank and XP untouched, and the checkpoint the objective earns already has them free. `MissionWorld` gains `rescue(slot, reachM)`. The Mission message is unchanged (the `rescue` type takes the seventh of the three-bit type codes; `progress`/`goal` are the hold in ticks, `satisfied` is someone holding), so `PROTOCOL_VERSION` stays 57. The HUD line and the briefing say what to do.

**Decided in this PR.** With nobody held (or the named slot not held) the objective is complete at once, so a campaign that lost no one is not stuck; U-065's mission can rely on a prisoner existing by carrying one in. No bot behaviour asks to rescue (a bot's interact is for reviving), so a human squadmate does the holding; a bot rescue is a follow-up if wanted.

**Evidence.** `packages/server/src/session/rescue.test.ts` (hold and free with kit and place; letting go and distance reset the hold; a downed soldier loses it; nobody held completes at once; a failed mission leaves the prisoner held through a retry), `mission.test.ts` (the run's hold, the Support's shorter hold, the no-prisoner case, parsing and refusals), `missionHud.test.ts`. `pnpm verify` and the CI jobs: see the PR.
