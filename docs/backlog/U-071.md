# U-071 — Campaign design: premise, structure and the mission list

Epic: **Campaign design**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: owner, 2026-10-01: "I'm ready to design the actual campaign. The missions, maps, etc. Let's add that epic to the docs."

## Problem / outcome

The game has one real mission. The owner wants to design the campaign proper: the missions, their maps and how they
connect. This is the umbrella for that work and the home of its decisions: [docs/design/CAMPAIGN.md](../design/CAMPAIGN.md).

## Scope

A design task: it is done when the owner has answered the ten decisions in section 2 of the design doc (each has a
proposed default) and the mission table in section 3 has a title, a new element and a map idea for every mission.
Then one design brief per mission (the section 4 template) and the leaf cards below exist.

Not in scope: building any level, encounter or script (those are per-mission cards that follow), and any art.

## Decomposition

- [U-072](U-072.md): campaign flow: which mission is next, mission select, the debrief.
- [U-073](U-073.md): register a mission in one place; checks and the mission sim cover every mission.
- [U-065](U-065.md): the rescue mission (mission 2 by default).
- Per-mission cards are written when each brief is approved. Bots versus armour is a separate card, written on the owner's go.

## Start here

`docs/design/CAMPAIGN.md` (what exists, the gaps, the decisions); `data/missions/mission-01.json`, `data/encounters/mission-01.json`,
`data/scripts/mission-01.json`, `data/levels/mission-01.json` for what a mission is today.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The owner has answered or changed D1 to D10, recorded in the design doc.
- [ ] The mission table names every mission with its new element and map idea.
- [ ] A brief exists for the next mission to build.

## Handoff / evidence

Design doc and cards written 2026-10-01; no decision has been made. Nothing is claimed.
