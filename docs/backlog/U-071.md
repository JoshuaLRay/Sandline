# U-071 — Campaign design: premise, structure and the mission list

Epic: **Campaign design**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: owner, 2026-10-01: "I'm ready to design the actual campaign. The missions, maps, etc. Let's add that epic to the docs."

## Problem / outcome

The game has one real mission. The owner wants to design the campaign proper: the missions, their maps and how they
connect. This is the umbrella for that work and the home of its decisions: [docs/design/CAMPAIGN.md](../design/CAMPAIGN.md).

## Scope

A design task. **D1 to D10 were answered on 2026-10-01** (recorded in section 2 of the design doc): a ten-mission season of 30–45 minute, three-lane missions, straight line with campaign and replay runs, objectives in any order, Afghanistan after 9/11. It is done when questions Q1 to Q4 in section 3a are answered and the mission table has a title and a brief for mission 1 (then the others as they come, one at a time).

Not in scope: building any level, encounter or script (those are per-mission cards that follow), and any art.
