# U-197 — Man a mounted gun: bots mount guns, and a move order onto a gun mans it

Epic: Squad command reliability ([U-203](U-203.md)). Pillar: P2, P7.
Size: M. Human verdict: none (engineering); feel batched in U-119.
Status, priority, dependencies and branch/PR live in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: owner, 2026-10-16 — "Operating mounted guns" and "AI-controlled soldiers should be capable of performing the same relevant interactions as human-controlled soldiers"; parity CP-11, gap G-4.

## Problem / outcome

Humans and enemies can mount the MG nests (U-034); friendly bots cannot — the
session dismounts any autonomous slot immediately — and no order puts a bot on
a gun. Outcome: a player orders a soldier (or a fireteam, the nearest member)
onto a free mounted gun and it mans it, engages within the gun's arc under its
fire discipline, and gets off when ordered elsewhere or the gun is threatened.

## Scope

- **Conflict default / precedent:** a contextual move order — a move whose
  destination is within 1 m of a free emplacement mans it on arrival — the
  same pattern Mission 2's design uses for charge points
  ([kestrel-dam.md §9.5](../design/maps/kestrel-dam.md)). No new wheel sector
  (the wheel's eight sectors are full).
- Bots mounted: aim and fire inside the traverse/elevation limits, respect
  Hold fire / Defensive / Aggressive (U-101), dismount on a new order, when
  downed, or when a flanking threat is outside the arc for N seconds.
- Mobile commander: the same move order from the touch commander works.
- One soldier per gun; enemies and squad cannot share a gun.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A move order onto a free gun makes the bot mount it; it fires on enemies in its arc per fire discipline; a new order dismounts it.
- [ ] A bot never mounts on its own initiative (unordered) — proposal, reversible.
- [ ] Works for one soldier, a fireteam (nearest member) and from mobile.
- [ ] Headless tests: mount, engage, dismount, gun taken by an enemy first, bot downed on the gun.
