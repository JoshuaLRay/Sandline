# U-067 — Tank movement: follow a path, stop at a firing position

Epic: **Mission / vehicle combat**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [U-035](U-035.md), owner decisions 2026-09-30 (all the proposed defaults).

## Problem / outcome

The tank drives down the dirt road from the squad's spawn toward the compound, as [U-035](U-035.md) asks, and stops where it can threaten the squad.

## Scope

- A `tank` brain that follows an **authored tank path** (waypoints in the mission data, not the nav mesh of soldiers), at a speed near a slow walk; it turns its hull toward the next waypoint at a limited rate and never reverses.
- Stops at the path's final **firing position** if the road does not reach into the compound; holds there.
- A blocked path (a wall on the road, a wreck) holds the tank until the script opens it (U-069); it is never allowed through geometry.
- Crowd/avoidance: a tank does not push soldiers; soldiers in its way are stopped-for, not run over (no crush damage in this card).
- Determinism: path following uses the shared approved math only.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A tank given a path drives it at the set speed and stops at the end.
- [ ] It turns at a limited rate and does not clip walls on the authored path.
- [ ] It waits at a blocked segment and proceeds when the blocker is removed.
- [ ] Tests cover path following, turning, stopping and blocking.
