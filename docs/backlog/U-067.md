# U-067 — Tank movement: follow a path, stop at a firing position

Epic: **Mission / vehicle combat**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [U-035](U-035.md), owner decisions 2026-09-30 (all the proposed defaults).

## Problem / outcome

The tank drives down the dirt road from the squad's spawn toward the compound, as [U-035](U-035.md) asks, and stops where it can threaten the squad.

## Scope

- A `tank` brain that follows an **authored tank path** (waypoints in the mission data, not the nav mesh of soldiers), at a speed near a slow walk; it turns its hull toward the next waypoint at a limited rate and never reverses.
- Stops at the path's final **firing position** if the road does not reach into the compound; holds there.
- A blocked path (a wall on the road, a wreck) holds the tank until the script opens it (U-069); it is never allowed through geometry.
- Crowd/avoidance: a tank does not push soldiers; soldiers in its way are stopped-for, not run over (no crush damage in this card).
- Determinism: path following uses the shared approved math only.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A tank given a path drives it at the set speed and stops at the end.
- [x] It turns at a limited rate and does not clip walls on the authored path.
- [x] It waits at a blocked segment and proceeds when the blocker is removed.
- [x] Tests cover path following, turning, stopping and blocking.

## Handoff / evidence

Implemented on `task/U-067-tank-movement`; awaiting review. No owner playtest verdict is claimed.

**What changed.** `packages/shared/src/sim/vehicle.ts` is the drive: `createDrive(path, yaw)`, `stepDrive(...)`, `driveYawWire`. It follows waypoints in order, turns the hull at `turnDegPerSec` (30) in whole table units, pivots on the spot until it points within about 20 degrees of the waypoint, then goes forward at `speedMps` (1.6); it never reverses, and stops at the last waypoint (`arrived`, inside `arriveM`). Table trigonometry only, steering from the cross and dot of heading and bearing (no arctangent). `enemies.json`'s `vehicle` block gains `speedMps`, `turnDegPerSec` and `arriveM`. On the server a tank given a `path` (`EnemySpawn.path`) drives instead of walking on a soldier's controller (`driveTank`); it may stand only where three footprints along its hull clear every wall and every active script blocker, and not on a living soldier, else it holds (`blocked`) and tries again each tick, so a wall opening lets it go on; it does not push or run anyone over. Its replicated hull yaw and its hitbox follow the heading; the turret rides the hull until U-068. A dead tank stops. A tank given no path stands still.

**Limits, stated.** The hull is tested as three squares along its length, not as a true rectangle, so a tight bend can clip a corner a real hull would; the ground is taken as flat (it keeps its spawn height); and the path is handed to the spawn (`EnemySpawn.path`): reading it from mission data is U-069's, as is restoring its drive state (next waypoint, heading) from a checkpoint.

**Evidence.** `packages/shared/src/sim/vehicle.test.ts` (8: validation, steady speed, stop at the end, turn rate and pivot, no reversing, waypoint order, waiting and resuming, determinism) and `packages/server/src/session/tankDrive.test.ts` (8: speed on a session, no path, turning and the replicated heading, the wall and its opening, a soldier in the way, death, the hitbox turning with the heading and its control). `pnpm verify` and the CI jobs: see the PR.
