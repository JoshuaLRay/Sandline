# U-125 — Remove walkable nav islands baked inside solid boxes

Parent: [U-109](U-109.md). Epic: Map implementation. Status, priority, dependencies and branch/PR live in BACKLOG.md.
Source: found while auditing U-123 on 2026-10-05 (agent observation, not an owner report).
Design: [Mission 1 construction specification](../design/maps/qalat-road.md), §14.2 item 7.

## Problem / outcome

Observed on `main` at `6d4bfd2`:

- A fresh bake of a fixture holding one solid block (10 × 10 × 6 m on the ground)
  has a walkable polygon at y 0.05 at the block's centre, inside the solid, as well
  as the expected one on its top (y 6.05).
- The committed `qalat-road` bake has y 0.05 polygons inside `terrace-upper`
  `(33, 0, 150)`, `terrace-middle` `(33, 0, 104)`, `bank-middle-b` `(18, 0, 134)` and
  `terrace-outer-bank` `(51, 0, 100)`. Paths from them to open ground are partial,
  so the islands appear disconnected.

Expected (§14.2.7): no nav inside solid volumes; no walking under the map.

Hypothesis (unverified): Recast rasterizes only the boxes' faces, so a solid box
whose underside coincides with the ground leaves an open "room" between its
bottom and top faces with full headroom. U-120's closed undersides would not change that.

Why it matters: nearest-point queries near a box's base (spawns, order goals,
cover, follower goal search out to 64 m) can snap into an island and then find no
way out, and validation cannot tell an enclosed void from a playable floor.

## Scope

Exclude solid box interiors from the walkable bake (for example by marking their
volume unwalkable) without removing legitimate tops, basements under raised slabs,
or doorways. Bump the bake hash, regenerate every world, and record count changes.

## Start here

`packages/tools/src/nav/bake.ts` (box soup, area marking), `packages/tools/src/nav/stacked.test.ts`,
`packages/tools/src/nav/layers.test.ts`; `pnpm gen:nav`.

- [ ] Regenerated bakes are committed with hash/count changes recorded; `pnpm verify`, mission sims and latest-head CI pass.
