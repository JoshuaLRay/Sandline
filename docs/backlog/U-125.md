# U-125 — Remove walkable nav islands baked inside solid boxes

Parent: [U-109](U-109.md). Epic: Map implementation. Status, priority, dependencies and branch/PR live in BACKLOG.md.
Source: found while auditing U-123 on 2026-10-05 (agent observation, not an owner report).
Design: [Mission 1 construction specification](../design/maps/qalat-road.md), §14.2 item 7.

## Problem / outcome

Observed on `main` at `6d4bfd2`:

- A fresh bake of a fixture holding one solid block (10 × 10 × 6 m on the ground)
  has a walkable polygon at y 0.05 at the block's centre, inside the solid, as well
  as the expected one on its top (y 6.05).
- The committed `qalat-road` bake has y 0.05 polygons inside `terrace-upper`
  `(33, 0, 150)`, `terrace-middle` `(33, 0, 104)`, `bank-middle-b` `(18, 0, 134)` and
  `terrace-outer-bank` `(51, 0, 100)`. Paths from them to open ground are partial,
  so the islands appear disconnected.

Expected (§14.2.7): no nav inside solid volumes; no walking under the map.

Hypothesis (unverified): Recast rasterizes only the boxes' faces, so a solid box
whose underside coincides with the ground leaves an open "room" between its
bottom and top faces with full headroom. U-120's closed undersides would not change that.

Why it matters: nearest-point queries near a box's base (spawns, order goals,
cover, follower goal search out to 64 m) can snap into an island and then find no
way out, and validation cannot tell an enclosed void from a playable floor.

## Scope

Exclude solid box interiors from the walkable bake (for example by marking their
volume unwalkable) without removing legitimate tops, basements under raised slabs,
or doorways. Bump the bake hash, regenerate every world, and record count changes.

### Reproduction (on `main` at `63efd0c`)

`packages/tools/src/nav/solidInteriors.test.ts` failed on main:

- A 10 × 10 × 6 m block on the ground baked 17 polygons at y 0.05 inside it.
- A box sunk into a raised slab kept the slab top inside it as walkable islands.
- The committed-bake scan found polygons wholly inside boxes in greybox-01 (75,
  inside `spine`), mission-01 (75) and qalat-road (2,621, e.g. inside
  `valley-south-bound`, `terrace-upper` and the banks). A polygon only counts
  when every corner lies within a box's footprint, so a step's straddling edge
  polygon (an early false positive in kit-gallery) is not counted.

The hypothesis was confirmed: Recast rasterizes faces, not volumes, so the
floor inside a tall box sees only the box's top far overhead.

### Implementation

- `packages/tools/src/nav/solidBake.ts` runs Recast's solo pipeline step for
  step as `generateSoloNavMeshData` does. After erosion, the step the Recast
  sample leaves optional ("mark areas") marks every solid box's inside
  `RC_NULL_AREA`: inset half a cell across, from a cell below its base to two
  cells below its top. Erosion has already run, so nothing outside a box
  changes, and box tops stay walkable.
- `worldSolids` covers only boxes at least the agent's height (1.8 m) tall.
  Nothing shorter can hold a standing soldier, and a step or crate stays exactly
  as Recast bakes it. Both the bare link-check mesh and the final world mesh use
  it. The T-3.01 spike bake keeps the library generator.
- The bake hash adds `solidInteriors: 1`, and `pnpm gen:nav` regenerated every
  world.

| World | Mesh bytes before → after | Cover points |
|---|---|---|
| range | 169,900 → 169,900 (bytes identical; hash only) | 70 (unchanged) |
| kit-gallery | 85,432 → 85,432 (bytes identical; hash only) | 106 (unchanged) |
| greybox-01 | 224,648 → 213,204 | 712 (unchanged) |
| mission-01 | 231,452 → 220,008 | 728 (unchanged) |
| qalat-road | 983,520 → 594,236 | 2,250 (unchanged) |

One existing test changed its sampling, not its assertion. In
`packages/tools/src/qalat-road.test.ts`, `surface` sampled `supportUnder` with a
zero footprint, so a sample exactly on the seam between two abutting stair-step
boxes (x 33, z 130) found neither and returned ground y0 inside the solid steps.
That only ever connected through the old hidden island. It now uses a 1 cm
footprint and gets the step top. The lane-isolation assertions are unchanged.
