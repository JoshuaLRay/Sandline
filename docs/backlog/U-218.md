# U-218 — One drivable multi-crew vehicle (future epic)

Epic: Drivable vehicles. Pillar: P7.
Size: L+ (epic; new ADR). Human verdict: owner go-ahead and later playtests.
Status, priority, dependencies and branch/PR live in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: owner, 2026-10-16 — "Create a future epic or task family for introducing one well-designed, drivable, multi-occupant military vehicle … Do not implement drivable vehicles now." Parity CP-37, gap G-11 (series: Humvee, Bradley, Land Rover DPV; one drives, others shoot). ADR-015 cut drivable vehicles from the slice.

## Problem / outcome

The squad cannot drive. A single, well-made vehicle (a light utility vehicle
with a mounted gun, period-appropriate, original name) with driver, gunner and
passenger seats would bring back the series' combined-arms moments.

## Scope (when the owner gives the go-ahead)

1. **ADR** reopening ADR-015's cut: physics (Rapier vehicle controller vs a
   kinematic model, ADR-005 determinism), prediction for the driver
   (ADR-012), authority, snapshot budget (ADR-013).
2. **Seats:** driver, mounted gunner (reusing emplacement fire logic, U-034),
   passengers who can fire personal weapons (proposal); entering/exiting with
   E; switching seats.
3. **AI occupants:** bots drive on order (move order with the vehicle), gun on
   fire discipline, dismount on order; humans and bots share the path (ADR-001).
4. **Multiplayer:** seat ownership, drop-in/out while seated, possession and
   switching (U-026) inside a vehicle.
5. **Collisions and navigation:** vehicle navmesh/area costs, crushing rules,
   blocking soldiers.
6. **Cameras and controls:** third-person chase camera, gunner view, mobile
   commander view of a moving vehicle.
7. **Mission use:** one mission designed around it.

## Decision needed

**Should a drivable vehicle enter the plan now, and which one?**
A — after Mission 2 ships, one light utility vehicle with a gunner (largest
fun-per-cost);
B — later than Mission 3, or never in season one;
C — an armoured vehicle (more combined arms, much more cost).
**Recommended default: A**, because the series' vehicle moments were mostly a
fast car with a gunner, and the shared physics/seat work is the same for any
later vehicle. Reply "agree" or pick another.
