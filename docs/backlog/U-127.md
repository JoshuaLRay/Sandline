Epic: Map design. Status, priority and work link live in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: owner request, 2026-10-05: "Let's design mission 2 in full detail,
similar to how mission 1 is detailed, but even more so. Be creative with the new

## Scope and delivered design

Documentation only. This card delivers:

- a complete, coordinate-based [construction specification](../design/maps/kestrel-dam.md);
- a [mission brief](../design/MISSION-02.md);
- three construction diagrams: surface and route plan, dam complex on all levels,
  and sections A–C;
- reconciled campaign and map indexes.

No level, encounter, script, data or code changes. No owner approval of the
design or its play feel is claimed.

The design is **The Kestrel Dam** (`kestrel-dam`), a through-mission. The squad
inserts at a concealed gravel bar and takes the gorge road, the high irrigation
canal or the west bank to a Soviet-era dam. There it destroys two
anti-aircraft guns on opposite abutments with mission-supplied demolition
charges, frees carried-in prisoners, defeats two technicals sent across the
crest, and extracts by helicopter from the east abutment. Demolition is the new
element, taught by an optional silent training target before the defended guns.

- [x] Detail beyond Mission 1, including:
  - a boundary register with a shortcut-prevention construction for every edge;
  - computed support-ray tables for every embrasure;

  - fight-by-fight intent;
  - an outcome matrix;
  - four reference squad plans;
  - per-target demolition rules;
  - verified engine facts the geometry is built against.
- [x] Follows the shared standard:
  - three physically distinct routes (14 m assault, 6 m overlook, 4 m flank);
  - two listed road↔canal crossings, 58 m apart;
  - zero flank links;
  - bounded start and objective convergence;
  - geometric spawn concealment;
  - enemies on every route;
  - no hold-the-area objectives.
- [x] Engine prerequisites identified from source (N1–N8) and kept separate from
  Mission 1's capability cards; no unsupported field presented as existing.
- [x] Carry-over, prisoners, loot, failure, retry and owner decisions stated, with
  proposals marked as proposals (specification §20).
- [x] Diagrams generated from the same coordinates as the tables, rendered and
  reviewed.

## Verification and evidence

Branch `task/U-127-mission-02-design`; [PR #301](https://github.com/JoshuaLRay/Sandline/pull/301).
Latest-head CI results are recorded in the PR.

A single-source geometry model (a scratch design tool, not committed) produced
every coordinate and the three SVG diagrams, and ran these design checks, all
passing:

- **Counts:** 39 encounter entities, cap 42; unique IDs; every socket on an
  authored floor height.
- **Stairs:** all stair rise and run budgets.
- **Spawn screening:** 114,912 rays from every guard, reserve and technical-path
  eye to the insertion at three body heights, all blocked (smallest margin
  8.4 m).
- **Noise:** spawns beyond 150 m gunfire hearing; the training target 141.3 m
  from the nearest guard, beyond 120 m blast hearing.
- **Support rays:** all 12 through the specified embrasures.
- **Line of sight:** the tunnel bend blocks the roadblock MG; the west MG's
  field of view excludes Route 3's arrival.
- **Vehicles:** both swept corridors are clear; no socket is inside cover.

During the design these checks caught and fixed several defects:

- a straight insertion exit that leaked sight lines;
- a training target within blast-hearing range;
- embrasure sills clipping steep rays;
- an MG facing Route 3's arrival;
- a holding room narrower than the 4 m passage rule;
- huts and cover in the technicals' path;
- a door overlapping embrasures.

Source inspected for the engineering section:

- **Data:** enemy archetypes (`enemies.json`, `enemies.ts`), weapons and projectile
  numbers.
- **Movement and hearing:** controller movement limits, hearing radii.
- **Mission rules:** objective and event schemas (`mission.ts`, `events.ts`),
  area bounds (`world.ts`, `areas.ts`).
- **Server:** carried-in prisoner placement (`Session.ts`), bot armour logic.
- **Tooling and plans:** the headless mission scenario, and Mission 1's
  capability cards.

`CAMPAIGN.md`'s archetype list was corrected: RPG, sniper and officer are
reserved IDs, not built archetypes.

The diagrams are orientation aids; the specification's tables govern. Gameplay,
nav, render and performance results do not exist until implementation passes the
specification's §19 gates.
