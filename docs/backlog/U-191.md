# U-191 — Attachments and weapon customisation (if approved)

Epic: Loot and progression ([U-183](U-183.md)). Pillar: P8, P4.
Size: L (split by attachment family). Human verdict: batched in U-193.
Status, priority, dependencies and branch/PR live in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: owner, 2026-10-16 — required epic phase 8 ("Attachments and customization, if approved").

## Problem / outcome

If L-17 approves attachments: optics, suppressors, magazines, stocks, barrels,
grips/bipods and underbarrel launchers as items that mount on compatible
weapons (default: mount types), change function in bounded ways, are found,
equipped, removed and transferred under the approved rules (default: at the
loadout screen and supply caches), and are used by bots.

## Scope

- Attachment data and stat modifiers in shared code (deterministic); today's
  scoped weapon ids migrate to base weapon + optic.
- UI for equipping at the loadout screen and caches; viewmodel/third-person
  presentation of mounted attachments (art generators, ADR-018).
- Suppressor noise integrates with U-206/U-210.

## Read first

- U-185 specification; [LOOT-PROGRESSION.md §7](../design/LOOT-PROGRESSION.md); `packages/shared/src/data/weapons.json` (`scopeFovDeg`); `packages/tools/src/art`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Compatibility enforced by the host; incompatible mounts refused.
- [ ] Stat effects bounded per the specification and covered by unit tests.
- [ ] Scoped variants migrated without changing their feel (parity of zoom/spread).

## Verification

Unit tests, headless equip/transfer tests, art captures for owner review in U-193; CI.
