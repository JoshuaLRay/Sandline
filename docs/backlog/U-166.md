# U-166 — Preserve open-side blast exposure at a scenery impact face

Epic: Grounded combat. Pillar: P4.
Size: S (one shared blast boundary rule and regression coverage; scope to confirm).
Human verdict: none for the deterministic boundary regression.
Status, priority, dependencies and branch/PR live in BACKLOG.md.
Source: agent observation while verifying U-165, 2026-10-13; not an owner playtest report.

## Problem / outcome

Observed on main's ordinary blast rule: an actor on the open side of a wall
receives `blastExposure=0` when the explosion starts exactly on the wall face,
but exposure is 1 when that same origin moves 0.001 m outside the wall.
`stepProjectile` supplies an exact scenery-face detonation point on a world hit.
This can attenuate open-side blast damage as though the wall sheltered the actor.
Owner gameplay observation/frequency: UNKNOWN.

Independent reproduction: one cover box x93.4..94.6/y30..32.2/z236..240;
standing actor feet (91,30,238), height 1.8; explosion (93.4,30.9,238) gives
exposure 0, while (93.399,30.9,238) gives 1. U-165's actual cannon shell also
returns the exact west face x93.4. No runtime fix is included in U-165.

Hypothesis: `rayWorld` returns a distance-zero contact for blast rays departing
from the solid's boundary, and `blastExposure` counts that as obstruction.

## Scope

Confirm the ordinary Session world-hit path and scope the smallest boundary
correction. Preserve positive damage attenuation behind solid walls, roofs and
terrain; do not change projectile collision, weapon tuning or actor immunity.
Do not ignore unrelated blockers or a ray that enters the impact solid.
