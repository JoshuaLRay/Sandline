# U-223 — Screen the refuge escort hold from the east-gate approach

Epic: Map implementation. Pillar: P6 (rescue and protected return choices).
Size: S. Human verdict: batched whole-mission acceptance in U-119.
Status, priority, dependencies and branch/PR live in BACKLOG.md.
Source: U-222 shelter inspection, 2026-10-16 (agent observation, not a playtest).

## Problem / outcome

§10.3 says the refuge's walls and roof shelter the escorted POW "from every
external lane and tank ray". In the built outpost, a standing eye on R9's east
pad has a clear line to the escort hold at (16,8,380). The line passes through
the east gate (x56, z372..376) and the refuge's open east door (x22, z378..382).
Twenty-three probe rays reach the hold from (58..60, 9.6, 372..374), at heights
0.15 to 1.75 m (U-222 scratch probe). No tank-route position, the south
forecourt or the west pad has such a line. A real tank shell fired from the east
pad also did not reach the hold. The tank never drives onto the east pad, so this
is an infantry/camera lane, not a tank ray.

## Scope

Close the east-pad lane to the escort hold. Keep the approved refuge, east door
and east gate contracts (U-175/U-180), the 4 m door retrieval, the P-OUTPOST
cache and every U-221/U-222 proof.

## Owner decision — approved Option B (2026-10-16)

The owner explicitly approved Option B: add a roughly 4 m-long protective
high-cover screen east of the refuge door, sized to block weapon-eye and
production desktop/mobile camera rays from standing/crouched east-pad enemies.
Preserve the gate, doorway, holding point, escort route, 4 m passage and clear
landing. No further design approval is required. U-223 was promoted from
BLOCKED to READY before implementation. The original options are retained below.

- **A: move the escort hold inside the refuge.** Move it out of the doorway
  wedge to the south-west corner, about (12,8,377). Nothing is built; only the
  hold point and the brief move. Shells that go through the door can still
  burst inside the room.
- **B (APPROVED): add a protective court screen.** A 4 m high-cover piece east of the refuge
  door, on the line from the east gate, like O-C02. It must keep the 4 m court
  lane and the door landing.
- **C: accept the lane.** Record that the shelter is from the tank, not from
  infantry on the east pad.

**Recommended default: B**, because it closes the lane for every point in the
refuge without moving an approved door or the escort leg.
