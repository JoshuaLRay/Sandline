# U-003 — Align AR muzzle flash and tracers with the visible gun

Epic: **Weapon feel**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [owner feedback, 2026-09-26](2026-09-26-feedback.md), plus any legacy bug named below.

## Problem / outcome

In FPS ADS the AR appears to show multiple tracers; shots look disconnected from the gun crouched/prone, and in hipfire they never appear to originate at the gun. B-01/B-02 are prior fixes to inspect, not proof this report is already fixed.

## Scope

Use the rendered muzzle transform for local presentation, and reconcile predicted/confirmed effects without drawing a second local tracer. Preserve authoritative hit endpoints and legitimate shotgun pellets. Begin with the AR; smoke-check other weapons.

## Handoff / evidence

### What was wrong (reproduced on `main`, e00104c)

- **The tracer never came from the gun.**
  - The local streak started at the shared rig's abstract muzzle (`muzzlePosition`), not the drawn weapon.
  - At ADS that point is the **standing** eye in every stance. Crouched it sat 0.4 m above the camera, and prone 1.15 m above it. Each round's streak came down from the top of the screen into the sight picture, and with recoil successive streaks fanned. Captured on `main`: crouched and prone ADS show a vertical line from above the frame.
  - At the hip it started 1.05 m up and 0.26 m right, and ran **parallel** to the aim instead of converging on it. It was never on screen next to the gun, and never ended at the crosshair.
  - In third person it started at standing shoulder height, 1.42 m, whatever the stance.
- **The flash was not at the gun either.**
  - First-person ADS: it sat 8 cm ahead of the eye, inside the 0.1 m near plane, so it was always clipped.
  - Third person: a flat untextured square hung at standing shoulder height, visibly floating above a crouched or prone soldier.
- **The streak's end** came from a ray cast from that abstract muzzle, not from the eye the server traces from. It could stop on something the real round never meets.
- **Server confirmations never added a second local streak** (`drawServerShot` draws none), and that is unchanged.

### Design as built

- **Barrel tips as data.**
  - `weaponMuzzle(id, side)` (`weaponModels.ts`) gives each weapon's barrel tip in aim space, per side, measured on the generated models.
  - `weapons.test.ts` in tools checks each gun's point: within 15 mm of its mesh, and nothing along the bore reaching more than 5 mm further forward.
- **First person** (`drawnMuzzle.ts`, `ViewModel.muzzle`):
  - The viewmodel's barrel tip, as placed this frame (hip or ADS blend, bob, kick, reload dip), is carried from the viewmodel camera to the world camera at the same screen spot and depth. It is scaled by the ratio of FOV tangents, because ADS narrows the world's FOV but not the gun's.
  - Crouch, prone and the camera's interpolated pose come with the camera, since the viewmodel is attached to it.
  - `viewModel.update` now runs before the effects are placed, so they use this frame's pose.
- **Third person:** the tip on the body's weapon attachment (`playerRig.aim`), in its drawn pose for every stance.
- **Fallback:** downed, vaulting or on a mounted gun, the old rig point is kept.
- **Near walls:** the point is pulled back along the eye's ray to 4 cm short of any scenery between the eye and it, and never nearer than 0.15 m. That is the same spot on screen, still drawn in front of the wall and the near plane.
- **Tracers** (`CombatQA`):
  - `predictShot(eye, directions)` now takes the **stance eye**, the origin the server traces. Each pellet's end is found from there, and the streak is queued.
  - `drawPredicted(barrel)` runs once per frame. It draws the queued streaks from the drawn barrel and moves each live own streak's start onto the barrel for its 0.11 s life. Ends stay where the round stops.
  - One streak per pellet: one for the AR, one per pellet for the shotgun.
  - Server confirmations still draw none. Remote shots still draw one along the server's segment, which never follows our barrel.
- **Flash** (`effects.ts`):
  - Drawn at the drawn barrel and follows it (B-02 unchanged in spirit).
  - Now has a soft four-spiked texture instead of a flat square.
  - In first person it is `FIRST_PERSON_FLASH_SCALE` (0.4) of its third-person size.
  - The shell still ejects from the rig point beside the eye or shoulder (`ejectFrom`), so brass does not cross a first-person view.
- **Impacts:** unchanged, still at the server's point.
- **Audio, hits and the authoritative origin:** untouched. U-002's stance eyes remain the trace origin.
