# U-009 — Add an interaction-driven upload objective

Epic: **Mission**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [owner feedback, 2026-09-26](2026-09-26-feedback.md), plus any legacy bug named below.

## Problem / outcome

The owner rejects standing inside a box for a timer. They want an interaction, such as protecting a file upload from enemies using a lever.

## Scope

Add one reusable objective: a living ally interacts with a terminal to start an upload; the upload continues without allies occupying an area. Scope this task to the objective state machine, validated interaction path, checkpoint data and HUD. U-010 adds hostile lever behavior; U-011 migrates mission content. Proposed reversible default: enemy lever use pauses progress; an ally restarts at the terminal without losing accumulated progress.

### Design

- **The data** (`shared/src/sim/mission.ts`): a sixth objective type, `upload`:
  - `terminal`: the panel, a point `{x, y, z}` in metres;
  - `reachM`: from the eye, at most `UPLOAD_REACH_MAX_M` (3 m), so a hand's reach and not a zone;
  - `seconds`;
  - `onInterrupt`: `keep-progress` (the owner's proposed reversible default) or `reset-progress`.

  Keys are validated by name like every mission field. `checkMission` refuses a terminal inside a world box, because it could never be seen and so never started. Every committed mission is unchanged; U-011 migrates content.
- **The state machine** (`server/src/session/mission.ts`, `MissionRun`). Every objective now has a phase, `idle | active | interrupted`; every type except upload is `active` from its first tick.
  - An upload is `idle` until `startUpload()`.
  - Once `active`, it gains a tick of progress each tick, whoever stands wherever, and completes at `ticksOf(seconds)`.
  - `interruptUpload()` makes it `interrupted`, keeping or zeroing progress as `onInterrupt` says, until a new `startUpload()`.
  - Each call is refused, returning false and changing nothing, unless the current objective is an upload in the phase it applies to and the mission is in progress. A repeated, late or misplaced call cannot advance it: progress comes only from ticks while it runs, capped at the goal. Once complete, the mission has moved on.
- **The interaction** (`Session.startUploadAt`). The interact press is judged on the host, after the revive's and the gun's claim on the same press (`updateMounts`). It starts the upload only if all of these hold:
  - the room is started and the mission is in progress;
  - the objective is an upload that is not already running;
  - the soldier is alive and not downed, not vaulting and not on a gun;
  - the panel is within `reachM` of the soldier's eye (`soldierEye`, by stance);
  - `lineOfSight` from the eye to the panel is clear through the world's collision boxes, blockers included.

  Only the rising edge of the newest real input counts, so a held key, a repeated old input or a key already down on arrival is not a press.
- **The interruption, data-driven now:** a new event-script action, `interrupt-upload`, on any trigger. U-010's enemy lever will call the same `interruptUpload`.
- **On the wire** (protocol 38): the Mission message carries the phase (2 bits) after the type. `upload` is objective type 5, which fits the existing 3 bits.
- **The HUD** (`client/src/ui/missionHud.ts`):
  - idle: `start the upload at <label> · E at the terminal`;
  - active: `uploading from <label> · 25% · 45 s left · no need to stay`;
  - interrupted: `upload interrupted at 50% · E at <label> to restart it`;
  - complete: the host tells every client `Upload complete: <label>` (a script message), and the line moves to the next objective or to "Mission complete".
  - The interact prompt shows `E  START THE UPLOAD` or `E  RESTART THE UPLOAD` within reach of the panel, from the world's committed mission data. It is a hint; the host judges the press.
  - The briefing lists the objective too.
- **Checkpoints:** a checkpoint is still an objective boundary, as for every type. A retry, a restart or a durable checkpoint in a new host starts the upload `idle` from 0 with the terminal usable. A player who joins or resumes is sent the current Mission, phase and progress included.

### Verification

- **`server/src/session/mission.test.ts`**, unit:
  - idle does not move whoever stands where;
  - a start runs it with nobody anywhere, and a second start is refused;
  - it completes once at the goal, and complete is final;
  - `keep-progress` and `reset-progress` both hold through time and a second interrupt, and complete after a restart;
  - another objective, a failed mission, and the next objective after a completion all refuse it;
  - retry, restart and `restoreCheckpoint` make it idle from 0;
  - clients are told of a start and a stop, then once a whole second.
- **`server/src/session/upload.test.ts`**, a real `Session` on greybox-01 over loopback, with a terminal on the face of `as-wall-1`. The test first asserts the geometry it relies on.
  - **Refused:** a press out of reach; one in reach but behind the wall; one while downed; and E held from before arriving.
  - **Accepted:** a fresh press at the panel.
  - **Running and interrupted:** it runs after the soldier walks away. The script's `interrupt-upload` stops it and progress is kept. A key already held at the panel does not restart it, and a fresh press does. It completes once; late presses leave the next objective alone.
  - **Joins and completion:** a player who joins mid-upload, and one who drops and comes back, are sent `active` with the progress. Every client gets `Upload complete: the relay`.
  - **Checkpoints:** a wipe then P goes back to the checkpoint, where the upload is idle from 0 and takes a press again. A new host restored from the saved checkpoint shows it idle to a joining player, and it takes a press.
- **`shared/src/sim/mission.test.ts`:**
  - every type × phase × state round-trips the wire;
  - an unknown phase is refused;
  - the upload's data parses, and each bad field is refused by name;
  - a terminal inside a wall is refused.
- **`client/src/ui/missionHud.test.ts`:** each phase's line, and the prompt only within reach and only while idle or interrupted.
- **`server/src/session/events.test.ts`:** the new action is dispatched.
- Protocol pins moved to 38, and fixtures gain `phase`.
- `pnpm verify`: 195 files, 2109 tests, all passed.
- `pnpm bot --count 2 --ticks 600`: OK, peak divergence 0.0116 m.
- `pnpm sim-run --scenario mission --seeds 3`: every threshold met.
- No browser check: no committed mission has an upload yet (U-011 authors one), so there is nothing to see in the page without authoring content in this PR.
