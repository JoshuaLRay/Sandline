## Problem / outcome

The approved roster requires a left-handed bolt-action sniper for slot 4 and a semi-automatic sniper for slot 5, alongside the remaining LMG, AR, scoped AR, SMG and shotgun loadouts. Do not treat a weapon's handedness or firing action as presentation-only.

## Scope

Audit weapon IDs against the approved roster. Supply or adapt weapon definitions so slot 4's starting sniper is explicitly left-handed and bolt-action, and slot 5's is semi-automatic. Include handedness as validated weapon data used for eligibility, with left-handed variants available to the authored loot/reward path. Reuse existing models, mechanics and pipelines where appropriate. If more than one substantial new weapon behavior is required, split this card into per-archetype leaves before making it READY.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Every approved roster firearm has a valid gameplay definition, model, sound mapping and ammo/reload behavior. *(U-040 data, U-041 wire, U-042 models, U-043 sounds; the AR is the carbine, the LMG, shotgun and pistol already existed.)*
- [x] Slot 4 has a left-handed bolt-action starting sniper (`sniper-bolt-left`); slot 5 a semi-automatic one (`sniper-semi`), confirmed in `weapons.json` and by tests.
- [x] Sniper and scoped-AR roles match the approved distinctions (`carbine-scoped` is a scoped full-auto rifle; the snipers out-range the marksman). The SMG and shotgun are usable from the hip. *(The Support being unable to ADS or use first person is enforced by U-021/U-022, per the owner's 2026-09-29 decision.)*
- [x] Left-handed weapon eligibility is machine-readable (`handedness`, `canWield`) and can be checked by U-029. **Authoring a left-handed gun as reward loot is not built here:** it is the acceptance criterion "Authored loot/reward sources can supply a left-handed firearm" of U-029, which owns the loot path.
- [x] Weapon tuning is schema-validated data and server-authoritative; no class-specific numeric magic constants.
- [x] Generated assets/audio and budgets are current (`gen:art`, `gen:assets`, `gen:audio`, `check:assets`, `check:packs`, `check:load-time` in CI); firing/cycle behavior has regression coverage. The first-load budget needed a fix on the way (U-043).
