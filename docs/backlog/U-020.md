# U-020 — Add the roster’s missing weapon archetypes

Epic: **Squad**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [owner feedback, 2026-09-26](2026-09-26-feedback.md), plus any legacy bug named below.

## Problem / outcome

The approved roster requires a left-handed bolt-action sniper for slot 4 and a semi-automatic sniper for slot 5, alongside the remaining LMG, AR, scoped AR, SMG and shotgun loadouts. Do not treat a weapon's handedness or firing action as presentation-only.

## Scope

Audit weapon IDs against the approved roster. Supply or adapt weapon definitions so slot 4's starting sniper is explicitly left-handed and bolt-action, and slot 5's is semi-automatic. Include handedness as validated weapon data used for eligibility, with left-handed variants available to the authored loot/reward path. Reuse existing models, mechanics and pipelines where appropriate. If more than one substantial new weapon behavior is required, split this card into per-archetype leaves before making it READY.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every approved roster firearm has a valid gameplay definition, model, sound mapping and ammo/reload behavior.
- [ ] Slot 4 has a left-handed bolt-action starting sniper; slot 5 has a semi-automatic starting sniper, with weapon IDs/behavior confirmed in data.
- [ ] Sniper and scoped-AR roles match the approved distinctions; SMG/shotgun support use works without ADS.
- [ ] Left-handed weapon eligibility is machine-readable and can be checked by U-029; reward loot can author left-handed firearms.
- [ ] Weapon tuning is schema-validated data and server-authoritative; no class-specific numeric magic constants.
- [ ] Generated assets/audio and budgets are current; individual firing/reload behavior has regression coverage.

Follow the shared [delivery workflow](../../.claude/commands/next-task.md), including `pnpm verify`. Record commands/results and observed acceptance evidence below. Visual/audio quality and owner decisions are not proven by a passing test.
