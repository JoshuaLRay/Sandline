## Decision needed

The standard 0.40 m tread recipe conflicts with the fixed C12 layout: the
C12-U (62,14,216)→(78,20,224) segment is 17.889 m long. Three ten-riser
flights need 14.8 m between first and last risers including two 2 m rests,
but even optimally oriented 4×4 m endpoint landings leave only 13.889 m.
C12-L's axis-aligned first climbing landings leave 8.050 m for 9.2 m of flights.

- **Recommended default: 0.30 m treads on constrained flights only.** Keep
  exact spines, heights, 4 m widths, 0.20 m risers, 60/110 counts, waypoint
  landings and 2 m rest landings; follows the existing service-bridge exception.
- Keep 0.40 m treads and revise the spines: preserves the ordinary stair recipe
  but moves the approved crossings and requires new coordinate decisions.
