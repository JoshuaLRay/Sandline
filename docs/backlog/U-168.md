## Owner-approved stair recipe — 2026-10-13

The standard 0.40 m tread recipe conflicts with the fixed C12 layout: the
C12-U (62,14,216)→(78,20,224) segment is 17.889 m long. Three ten-riser
flights need 14.8 m between first and last risers including two 2 m rests,
but even optimally oriented 4×4 m endpoint landings leave only 13.889 m.
C12-L's axis-aligned first climbing landings leave 8.050 m for 9.2 m of flights.

- **Recommended default: 0.30 m treads on constrained flights only.** Keep
  exact spines, heights, 4 m widths, 0.20 m risers, 60/110 counts, waypoint
  landings and 2 m rest landings; follows the existing service-bridge exception.
- Keep 0.40 m treads and revise the spines: preserves the ordinary stair recipe
  but moves the approved crossings and requires new coordinate decisions.

The owner replied **"Approve and continue"** on 2026-10-13, approving the
recommended 0.30 m tread exception on constrained flights. Apply it only where
needed to fit the landings or preserve the existing 8 m ridge route; record the
exact affected segments in the construction data and proof. All other stated
dimensions remain required.

## Construction interpretation

Conflict default / smallest reversible proposal (ADR-021 step 4): the fixed
4 m C12-L terminal ribbon meets the southeast corner of the existing V1/R2
floor before its last riser. That bounded shared corner is the open stair
mouth, supported by the exact lower stair columns rather than an overhead
ridge floor. The bay anchor, lip and canopy stay at their approved coordinates;
all listed rays and the full 8 m ridge lane remain required. Tests restrict the
mouth exception to the actual stair footprint and independently prove its floor.
