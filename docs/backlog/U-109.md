## Integrated evidence (2026-10-06)

`packages/tools/src/nav/layerCommands.test.ts` now runs **23 tests** on one
baked floor-0 / solid-slab-5.5–8 / bridge-14–16 fixture with real Sessions,
friendly brains and a loopback client. The fixture includes stairs to y8 and
one low cover crate on each floor; y16 intentionally has no upward connection.

- Navigation resolves each standing floor, leaves slab interiors unwalkable,
  and agrees with collision/headroom queries. Generated cover is usable on
  each floor; a real Session rejects a partial path to cover on the isolated
  bridge, with a same-floor positive control.
- Six cross-floor combinations (0↔8, 8↔16, 0↔16) have zero AI sight exposure.
  Actual encoded `Fire` messages resolve to the first slab face and leave the
  target's health unchanged. Three same-floor controls have full sight and
  damaging shots, proving that firing and perception are active.
- Actual Three.js screen rays, order picking and move/hold construction pass
  through the wire into Session order handling and back as replicated goals.
  Identical target x/z retain y0, y8 and y16. The pick's 5 cm backoff toward the
  eye is intentional; horizontal assertions allow that and wire quantization.
- Desktop and mobile camera solvers use the **same Three.js scene collider as
  `main.ts`**, extracted without changing its query. Twenty-one desktop and
  45 mobile pitch/zoom cases cover slabs from above and below, including 3×
  landscape and 6× portrait zoom. Each solved focus→camera segment is clear;
  cases that would intersect scenery retract. Rays may pass around a slab's
  edge, but never through it. The meshes use the page's normal front-face
  winding and invisible collision material, including downward undersides.
- The existing eleven command/traversal/checkpoint cases run on that same
  fixture: actual stair ascent/descent, refusal of the isolated bridge goal,
  group goals, following, revive reach, POW following/orders and retry/save
  restoration of y0/y8/y16 positions and the POW's elevated goal.
