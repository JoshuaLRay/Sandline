# U-056 — Concussion grenades for Preach

Epic: **Controls**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [U-048](U-048.md), owner design decisions 2026-09-30.

## Problem / outcome

Preach carries **4 concussion grenades** in slot 5 (owner, 2026-09-30; the item was proposed 2026-09-30).

## Scope


A **thrown** item on the existing grenade physics (`projectiles.json`, hold-LMB aim and release, right click cooks it like a frag, U-046): a blast that does **no damage** but heavily **suppresses** every enemy in its radius (proposal: radius 8 m, suppression well above the frag's `blastSuppression`, fuse 2.5 s, cooldown 1 s), so the assault team can revive or advance under it. Friendly soldiers in the radius are not suppressed (damage is already none). Enemies react through the existing suppression response; nothing in enemy AI changes.


## Start here

`packages/shared/src/data/projectiles.json`, `packages/server/src/session/Session.ts` (projectiles, pouch, detonation), the foundation from [U-048](U-048.md) and, for placed items, [U-054](U-054.md).

## Acceptance criteria


- [ ] Thrown and cooked like a frag; no damage to anyone.
- [ ] Every enemy within 8 m is suppressed above the frag's level, falling with distance.
- [ ] Preach carries 4; a checkpoint retry refills them; tested headless.

## Verification

Server tests for each rule, a hosted-session test, `pnpm verify` and the CI jobs. Numbers are placeholders until playtested.
