# U-056 — Concussion grenades for Preach

Epic: **Controls**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [U-048](U-048.md), owner design decisions 2026-09-30.

## Problem / outcome

Preach carries **4 concussion grenades** in slot 5 (owner, 2026-09-30; the item was proposed 2026-09-30).

## Scope


A **thrown** item on the existing grenade physics (`projectiles.json`, hold-LMB aim and release, right click cooks it like a frag, U-046): a blast that does **no damage** but heavily **suppresses** every enemy in its radius (proposal: radius 8 m, suppression well above the frag's `blastSuppression`, fuse 2.5 s, cooldown 1 s), so the assault team can revive or advance under it. Friendly soldiers in the radius are not suppressed (damage is already none). Enemies react through the existing suppression response; nothing in enemy AI changes.


## Start here

`packages/shared/src/data/projectiles.json`, `packages/server/src/session/Session.ts` (projectiles, pouch, detonation), the foundation from [U-048](U-048.md) and, for placed items, [U-054](U-054.md).

## Acceptance criteria


- [x] Thrown and cooked like a frag; no damage to anyone.
- [x] Every enemy within 8 m is suppressed above the frag's level, falling with distance.
- [x] Preach carries 4; a checkpoint retry refills them; tested headless.

## Verification

Server tests for each rule, a hosted-session test, `pnpm verify` and the CI jobs. Numbers are placeholders until playtested.

## Handoff / evidence

Implemented on `task/U-056-concussion`; awaiting review. Numbers are placeholders until playtested; no owner verdict is claimed.

**What changed.** `projectiles.json` gains `concussion` (kind `thrown`, 2.5 s fuse, 8 m, **0 damage**, 4 carried) and every projectile row a `suppression` field (0 keeps the shared blast curve in `suppression.json`; the concussion's 1.0 is its own amount at the centre, falling to nothing at 8 m). The host raises that amount on the hostile side inside the radius; the thrower's side is never suppressed (as for every blast). Preach carries 4 in slot 5 (`equipment: "concussion"`); thrown, aimed and cooked exactly like a frag (U-046); a retry refills them. `PROJECTILE_IDS` gains `concussion` (protocol 52); a placeholder held model.

**Evidence.** `packages/server/src/session/concussion.test.ts` (the data; an enemy at 1 m is suppressed to about 0.89, at 4 m about 0.5, at 15 m not at all, all above a frag's blast at those distances, and nobody's health moves; a frag still uses the shared curve; the squad is not suppressed), `equipment.test.ts` (Preach draws it), `ballistics.test.ts`.
