# U-080 — Realistic character models: a more grounded look

Epic: **Art**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: owner decision D1, 2026-10-01 ([design doc](../design/CAMPAIGN.md)).

## Problem / outcome

The owner finds the characters too cartoonish, even for PS2 standards, comparing them with *Conflict: Desert Storm*: ours are scrawny and look 'drawn'. The campaign is now grounded, post-9/11 Afghanistan.

## Scope

**Decided 2026-10-01 (owner's defaults):** improve the procedural/code-built pipeline (no authored-model import, so no new runtime dependency or ADR); re-proportion and re-texture the current rig; the reference is *Conflict: Desert Storm* as the owner described it. The owner supplied a Conflict: Desert Storm screenshot on 2026-10-02 and specifically identified boxy shoulders/hips and cartoon faces; continued procedural generation is explicitly approved. The characters are procedural/code-built humanoids (`packages/client/src/character`, `tools/src/art/characters`). Proposed first step: a short art-direction pass, then a re-proportion of the rig (heavier torso, shoulders and limbs, equipment volume, gear and uniform detail, flatter, less saturated palettes and texture) and a texture pass, judged against the owner's reference. Decisions needed: (1) the visual reference set; (2) whether to improve the procedural pipeline or commission/import authored models (a runtime dependency or asset format needs an ADR); (3) the polygon and texture budget for a six-plus-enemies scene. Enemy variants (Taliban/al-Qaeda dress) and the POW are part of it. No change to hitboxes or gameplay.

### Anatomical procedural rework — 2026-10-02

Owner decision: merge the intermediate improvement, then substantially rewrite
procedural modelling so AI/code continues to do all asset authoring.

- `anatomy.ts` supplies shared adult facial landmarks, diagonal rounded
  shoulder surfaces, and cloth profiles with asymmetric compression folds.
  Eyes sit in recessed sockets behind a projecting brow; the nose bridge/tip,
  cheekbones, muzzle, jaw and chin change the actual mesh. Skin painting uses
  narrowed subdued eyes, softer cheek colour and anatomical shadowing.
- `SkinBuilder` supports nonplanar cross-sections, angular skin weights,
  shared vertical UV frames and stitching boundaries. Joined surfaces retain
  their UVs while shared positions receive area-weighted smooth normals.
- The US trousers are **one connected Y-shaped shell**: waist halves join
  outside thigh semicircles and an inner groin gusset joins both branches.
  This replaces the separately capped pelvis slab. Lower waist sides blend
  to their corresponding thigh bones. Each leg uses a different atlas half.
- Sleeves travel diagonally from under the collar into rounded deltoids;
  exposed wide flat shoulder caps are gone. Trousers gather above the boots
  and bend around knees, with geometric folds rather than texture alone.
- Soldier and enemy share anatomy generation. Enemy headgear stays narrower
  than the US helmet, and its long shirt retains the distinct hanging cloth
  silhouette. Soldier armour uses rounded pectoral sections and a tapered
  waist; hands and helmet have more restrained proportions.
- The source GLBs, compressed web GLBs and manifest are regenerated together.
  The same 17-bone skeleton, joint positions, hit capsule, animation code,
  weapon attachment and gameplay remain unchanged. All assets stay within
  their existing character budgets: soldier 7,482 / 15,000 triangles and
  enemy 7,180 / 15,000; each retains one 1024² atlas.
- Evidence: [intermediate / rework, front and side](../art/u-080/squad-anatomical-comparison.png)
  and [production standing / crouched rigs holding the generated M4](../art/u-080/squad-anatomical-poses.png).
  Identical software-GL lighting is used for comparing source assets; posed
  evidence uses the production skin adapter, pose code and weapon attachment.
- Regression tests protect connected trouser topology, the hidden narrow
  shoulder start/rounded progression, vertical texture registration and real
  facial landmark geometry. Existing regenerated asset, rig, weighting,
  normals, hit-capsule and distinct enemy silhouette tests remain enabled.
- Validation: all 2,694 tests across 250 files pass; typecheck, lint,
  production build, asset budgets and pack coverage pass. A final enemy
  face-atlas registration correction also passes all 20 character checks
  and the three fighter pipeline/reproducibility checks.
- U-080 stays in REVIEW: merging this iteration does not claim owner visual
  acceptance or completion of the separate POW-specific model.

### Garment and equipment pass — 2026-10-02

The second comparison still showed smooth tubular clothing, a monolithic vest,
simple hands/boots and a symmetrical squat. Implemented:

- Shared fold landmarks and shading in metres, more densely sampled cloth,
  hanging/compression folds at elbows, knees and cuffs; inner leg clearance.
- A separate blouse under curved front/back armour, side closures, overlapping
  front opening and staggered soft pouches; rounded asymmetric pack pockets.
- Subdued woodland cloth on the armour over the DCU uniform; sleeve atlas region
  with height registration shared between painted and modelled folds.
- More mature face proportions/paint, distinct palm/fingers, boot tongues/laces.
- An asymmetric low crouch with rear knee lowered, inside the existing hit-volume
  tolerance and existing helmet clearance. No new gameplay pose or hitbox change.
- [Same-lighting front/side comparison](../art/u-080/squad-layered-comparison.png)
  and [standing/low-crouch gameplay-rig poses](../art/u-080/squad-layered-poses.png).
  Images use source GLBs and actual rig poses; they are staged reviews, not
  screenshots of a live match.

This is a substantial refinement, not a claimed quantitative match. The reference
has different lighting, uniform pattern, resolution and poses; U-080 stays REVIEW
for the owner's visual verdict. Everything remains generated from code.

Validation: typecheck and lint pass; all 2,696 tests across 251 files pass, followed
by targeted character and asset regeneration checks after fitting the armband to
the new sleeve. The production client builds and renders in a mission. Final
soldier: 12,358 triangles; fighter: 10,380; both below 15k, with unchanged 17-bone
skeletons and atlas dimensions. Asset budgets and pack coverage pass. Mission
capture: 177 draw calls (limit 300); timing from software GL is not a hardware FPS
claim. [Production mission frame](../art/u-080/squad-layered-gameplay.png).
