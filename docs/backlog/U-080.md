# U-080 — Realistic character models: a more grounded look

Epic: **Art**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: owner decision D1, 2026-10-01 ([design doc](../design/CAMPAIGN.md)).

## Problem / outcome

The owner finds the characters too cartoonish, even for PS2 standards, comparing them with *Conflict: Desert Storm*: ours are scrawny and look 'drawn'. The campaign is now grounded, post-9/11 Afghanistan.

## Scope

**Decided 2026-10-01 (owner's defaults):** improve the procedural/code-built pipeline (no authored-model import, so no new runtime dependency or ADR); re-proportion and re-texture the current rig; the reference is *Conflict: Desert Storm* as the owner described it. No reference images have been supplied yet, so the first pass is from that description and goes to the owner as screenshots for review; the owner can add images at any point. The characters are procedural/code-built humanoids (`packages/client/src/character`, `tools/src/art/characters`). Proposed first step: a short art-direction pass, then a re-proportion of the rig (heavier torso, shoulders and limbs, equipment volume, gear and uniform detail, flatter, less saturated palettes and texture) and a texture pass, judged against the owner's reference. Decisions needed: (1) the visual reference set; (2) whether to improve the procedural pipeline or commission/import authored models (a runtime dependency or asset format needs an ADR); (3) the polygon and texture budget for a six-plus-enemies scene. Enemy variants (Taliban/al-Qaeda dress) and the POW are part of it. No change to hitboxes or gameplay.

## Handoff / evidence

First pass done, in REVIEW: **awaiting the owner's look at the screenshots**; no verdict is claimed.

- What changed (`packages/tools/src/art/characters/soldier.ts`, regenerated with `pnpm gen:art` then `pnpm gen:assets`): the US soldier's body is heavier. `BULK` thickens each part's rings about its own axis (never its height, so every joint and pose is unchanged): arms ×1.2, legs ×1.1 across and ×1.26 front to back (the fighter's silhouette test needs daylight between the legs, so the thighs grow in depth), boots ×1.1, hands ×1.12, vest ×1.1. 3,948 triangles (the budget is 15k), same 17 bones, inside the hit capsule; hit boxes, animation and the rig are untouched (their tests pass, and `pnpm check:assets` is within budget).
- Screenshots of the squad in play, from the production client in headless Chromium (software GL): [front](../art/u-080/squad-first-pass-front.png), [side](../art/u-080/squad-first-pass-side.png).
- Not done yet, and what I would do next if this direction is right: the enemy fighter and the escorted prisoner get the same treatment (the prisoner is a palette on the same rig until then); a texture pass (flatter, less saturated palette, more fold, dirt and seam detail); a heavier, less symmetrical set of gear. A look is the owner's to judge; say what is wrong and I change the generator.

### Second pass — 2026-10-02

Owner feedback: the first-pass screenshots still look too cartoony. Continued
with the accepted procedural pipeline, using *Conflict: Desert Storm* as the
fidelity reference rather than claiming a visual match.

- Soldier: broader rounded shoulders, chest-to-waist taper, shaped calves and
  forearms, a slightly closer-fitting helmet with matched goggles and bands.
- Equipment: pouches moved onto the deeper vest surface; rounded pack body,
  external back and side pockets, and modelled shoulder webbing.
- Texture: muted DCU and olive-tan nylon, broad painted form shading, diagonal
  cloth creases, dust, compression webbing, stitching and buckles.
- Enemy: fuller shoulders and calves, stronger hanging cloth folds and form
  shading; existing pakol/turban variants retain their distinct silhouettes.
- Generated source assets, web assets and manifest updated together. Soldier:
  4,332 triangles, 17 bones, two materials, one 1024² atlas. Joint positions,
  hitboxes, animation code and gameplay are unchanged.
- Review image: [previous / revised, front and back](../art/u-080/squad-second-pass-comparison.png),
  rendered from the source GLBs under identical lighting in Chromium. This
  shows the bind pose for inspecting shape and texture, not a gameplay frame.
- Validation: typecheck, lint, all 2,691 tests across 249 files, asset budgets
  and pack coverage pass. The 183 character/animation tests include rig, skin
  weights, capsule bounds, normals and regenerated-source checks.
- U-080 remains in REVIEW; final visual acceptance and the POW-specific model
  are still outstanding.
