# U-058 — Smoke grenades for Ortiz

Epic: **Controls**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [U-048](U-048.md), owner design decisions 2026-09-30.

## Problem / outcome

Ortiz (slot 3, scoped AR, the second team's lead) carries **2 smoke grenades** (proposal 2026-09-30, not objected to by the owner; confirm when this card starts).

## Scope


A **thrown** item on the existing grenade physics: on its fuse (2 s) it releases a smoke cloud (proposal: 5 m radius, 20 s, fading in the last 4 s) that **blocks line of sight**: enemy perception and the bots' sight lines treat a point inside the cloud as blocked, and shots through it still land (smoke is concealment, not cover). The cloud is replicated so pages draw it. Large: it touches sight checks in `shared/ai/perception.ts` and rendering, so it is the last equipment card.


## Start here

`packages/shared/src/data/projectiles.json`, `packages/server/src/session/Session.ts` (projectiles, pouch, detonation), the foundation from [U-048](U-048.md) and, for placed items, [U-054](U-054.md).

## Acceptance criteria


- [x] The cloud appears where the grenade stops and lasts as above, on every page.
- [x] An enemy cannot see (and so cannot target) a soldier standing inside or seen through the cloud beyond its fade.
- [x] Shots and bullets are unaffected; tested headless, with the perception change covered by a unit test.

## Verification

Server tests for each rule, a hosted-session test, `pnpm verify` and the CI jobs. Numbers are placeholders until playtested.

## Handoff / evidence

Implemented on `task/U-058-smoke`; awaiting review. Numbers are placeholders until playtested; no owner verdict is claimed.

**What changed.** `projectiles.json` gains `smoke` (kind `thrown`, 2 s fuse, no damage, 2 carried, `releases: "smokecloud"`) and `smokecloud` (kind `placed`, 20 s, `smokeM: 5`); every row gets `releases` and `smokeM`. When the grenade goes off the host releases the cloud at that spot as a stuck, replicated projectile (so every page draws it as a translucent 5 m ball) that goes quietly at 20 s, its radius thinning linearly over the last 4 s. `sight()` in `shared/ai/perception.ts` takes the clouds and treats any probe line that passes through, starts in or ends in one as blocked, so an enemy neither sees nor targets a soldier in or behind smoke, and a bot cannot see through it either. Bullets and blasts are untouched (concealment, not cover). The pop is not a blast: no flash, boom or shake on the page, no stimulus for enemies to hear. Ortiz carries 2 (`equipment: "smoke"`); thrown and cooked like a frag. `PROJECTILE_IDS` gains `smoke` and `smokecloud` (protocol 55). The Possessed message's pouch length is now 4 bits (with eight kinds, the old 3 bits silently truncated it).

**Evidence.** `packages/shared/src/ai/perception.test.ts` (the segment/sphere test; a target seen through or standing in a cloud is hidden, one in front is seen, range and cone unchanged), `packages/server/src/session/smoke.test.ts` (the data and Ortiz's two; a pop leaves a cloud and hurts no one and is not offered back with E; lasts 20 s, thins over the last 4, then goes; a squad soldier is hidden from an enemy who saw them while the cloud is between, and seen again when it ends).
