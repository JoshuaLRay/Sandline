# U-208 — Warnings, alarms and reinforcements

Epic: Stealth and enemy awareness ([U-204](U-204.md)). Pillar: P4, P7.
Size: M–L. Human verdict: none (engineering); feel in U-212.
Status, priority, dependencies and branch/PR live in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: owner, 2026-10-16 — "Communicating threats between nearby enemies. Triggering alarms and reinforcements." Parity CP-42 (series: being seen raises a base alarm). Design §4.

## Problem / outcome

Groups share a target only among members who already know; there is no alarm.
Outcome: an Alerted enemy shouts a warning to comrades within a voice radius
(with what it knows, not the truth); missions may author alarm points that an
enemy runs to; a raised alarm alerts the alarm's area and fires an `alarm`
event-script trigger that can release staged reserves (U-131) and waves,
scaled by the director's human-count budget.

## Scope

- Voice radius and wall reduction as data; warned enemies become Alerted with
  the shouter's last-known position.
- Alarm points in level/encounter data; an `alarm` trigger in `events.ts`;
  the squad can stop the runner; the HUD message "Alarm raised" (text, no
  voice).
- Mission state records whether an alarm was raised (for loot bonus
  conditions, LOOT-PROGRESSION L-01).
- Qalat and Kestrel: optional alarm points proposed in their records, not
  required (their approved layouts stay).

## Read first

- U-205; `packages/server/src/ai/group.ts`, `director/spawner.ts` (staged reserves), `packages/shared/src/sim/events.ts`, `encounters.ts`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Warnings propagate only within voice range and carry approximate, not true, positions.
- [ ] An alarm point raised by an enemy fires the trigger once; killing the runner first prevents it.
- [ ] Reserves released by the alarm respect the director budget for 1–6 humans.
- [ ] Checkpoint retry restores the alarm state.
