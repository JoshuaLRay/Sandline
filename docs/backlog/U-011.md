### What changed in mission-01

| # | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | destroy the west-lane patrol | unchanged |
| 2 | destroy the east-lane position | unchanged |
| 3 | **clear-and-hold** the compound: stand in it for 60 s | **destroy the compound's garrison**: take it by fighting it |
| 4 | **defend** the compound: stand in it for 330 s | **upload** at the terminal on the compound's crate, 240 s, while the counterattack goes for the **lever** in the compound's north-east corner |
| 5 | reach the start line | unchanged |

- **Data** (`missions/mission-01.json`):
  - the terminal is on the south face of `objective-crate`, at (0, 1.0, 72.3), reach 2 m;
  - the lever is at (6.5, 1.0, 75.0), reach 1.8 m, pulled in 4 s by the `counterattack-push` group (U-010);
  - `keep-progress`: a cut keeps what has been sent.

  `checkMission` holds both points clear of the level's boxes. The navmesh paths to both from the start, the compound and the enemy's spawn zone, checked before placing them. Collision, nav and the level are untouched: the props are drawn and do not collide, so nothing needed re-baking.
- **Script** (`scripts/mission-01.json`): the messages now say what to do: "take the compound: put its garrison down"; "start the upload at the terminal on the crate, then keep them off the lever in the north-east corner"; "Upload complete — fall back". The counterattack is still sent when the fourth objective starts and stopped when it ends (U-001).
- **The page** (`client/src/assets/missionProps.ts`):
  - a grey-box console with a lit screen at the terminal, and a post with a red handle at the lever, from the mission data for the world the page is in;
  - the HUD's U-009/U-010 lines, the `E  START THE UPLOAD` prompt and the `ENEMY AT THE LEVER` warning;
  - the briefing reads "Start the upload at the relay and see it through 4 min — keep the enemy off its lever".
- **Bots can start the upload.** A friendly bot ordered to hold at a waiting terminal (`orderHold`, with `SquadView.terminal`) holds its hands on it. The session runs that bot's hold through the same `startUploadAt` checks as a player's press: alive, in reach of the eye, a clear line, and the upload waiting. It restarts the upload after every cut, and a revive's hands never count. In solo with bots, the player or a bot ordered there starts it.
- **The saboteur fights back** (U-010's tree, tuned here with the mission, because the scenario showed it failing):
  - **`leverCalm`:** it goes for the lever only when it has not been under fire for `backOffSeconds` (6 s). Fired on, it breaks off and fights from cover. Keeping it under fire keeps it off the lever.
  - **Cover near itself:** while it holds the lever job it takes cover near where it is (`coverNear` null), not back inside its garrison's area through the fire.
  - Without these, lever users were in cover 2–6% of their time under fire, and dragged the scenario's enemy-cover gate under its floor. With them: 71%.
- **Headless scenario** (`tools/src/scenarios`):
  - the leader walks the approach routes into the objective that destroys the compound's opening garrison, as it did into the old clear-and-hold;
  - during the upload its nearest standing bot holds at the terminal, and the rest hold between terminal and lever;
  - a running upload counts as a timed objective for the quiet-time ceiling.
- **Pressure tests** (`server/src/session/encounterPressure.test.ts`, U-001): the defend's checks now run on the upload's clock, started by hand. The counterattack is sent as it starts, never empty longer than a wave, stopped when it is won, and a retry inside it sends it again once.
