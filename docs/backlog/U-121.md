## Scope and acceptance

Audit nearest/resolve/path projection so an intended lower floor cannot silently project onto an upper storey. Respect vertical spans for dynamic blockers without changing legacy 2D callers. Audit vault and drop sampling at all supported elevations and validate collision/headroom along each link; no connection through a solid slab. Test y0, y8 and y16 with legitimate stairs/links, blocked undersides and same-x/z blockers. Update bake algorithm hashes and regenerate when link logic changes.

## Reproduction and implementation

On merged main, the new three-floor regressions showed:

- A y16 goal at x30 projected to y0 when the horizontal search widened.
- A basement-only blocker also disabled the y8 surface and y16 bridge.
- No vault links were generated across a waist-high obstacle on the y8 deck.

Resolution now keeps its vertical tolerance fixed (2 m by default, optionally
tighter) while widening only horizontally. Closest-point results must satisfy
the vertical bound; resolved results must also fit the horizontal radius.
Dynamic blockers intersect polygon height plus the baked agent's standing
height; omitted y bounds retain legacy all-floor blocking and reference counts.
Vault sampling considers each supported approach height. Vault/drop links use
a conservative swept body envelope to reject overhead/wall collisions, allowing
the deliberately traversed low obstacle or source support. Clearance envelopes
may reject marginal traversals; they never authorize clipping through ceilings.
Link algorithm revisions and vault lip are included in the bake hash.
