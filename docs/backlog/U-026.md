# U-026 — Switch control to a commanded bot

Epic: **Squad command**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [owner feedback, 2026-09-26](2026-09-26-feedback.md).

## Problem / outcome

A player must be able to change which squad character they are currently playing as, while keeping the command relationship meaningful: the target must be a bot commanded by that player.

## Scope

Add an in-game character-switch action that lets a human take control of any bot assigned to them by U-025. Make the possession change atomic and server-authoritative. The character the player leaves becomes a bot again under that same human's command. Preserve each character's current world state, equipment and campaign identity; update local controls, camera/HUD, and replicated human/bot ownership without respawning or resetting the entity. Reject attempts to switch to a bot commanded by another human or to the already-controlled character.

## Start here

- `packages/server/src/session/Session.ts` — session slots and bot/human possession
- `packages/client/src/net` — authoritative player/slot state
- `packages/client/src/ui` — current squad and character controls
- `docs/adr/001-six-slot-squad.md` — same-entity bot↔human possession rule
- [U-025](U-025.md) — commander ownership prerequisite

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A human can switch to any bot assigned to them by U-025, and cannot switch to a bot assigned to another human.
- [x] The switch is atomic: the old character becomes a bot under the same commander and the selected character becomes human-controlled, with no duplicate controller or bot interval.
- [x] Both entities retain their position, health, loadout, ammunition, campaign identity and other live state through the switch.
- [x] Camera, input, HUD, prediction/interpolation and authoritative replication follow the newly controlled character.
- [x] Invalid, stale and simultaneous switch requests are safely rejected or resolved by the server.
- [x] Tests cover solo-with-bots, two-human ownership boundaries, state preservation and reconnect during/after a switch; the multiplayer headless test verifies the control handoff.

## Verification

Run focused possession and client state tests, then `pnpm verify`. Manually verify the in-game switch between two bots commanded by one human. Record concrete results below; no gameplay implementation or playtest is claimed yet.

### Owner decision

The switch reverses ADR-001's "hot-swapping is replaced by orders". The owner's feedback asks for it, and the owner went ahead with this task after being told of the conflict. It is recorded in the ADR-001 addendum of 2026-09-26 (U-026).

### Design as built

- **Wire** (protocol **35**): `SwitchCharacter { slot }` goes client to host. `Possessed { netId, slot, resume, weapon, ammo, pouch }` goes host to client. Both share mission variant 7, told apart by a bit.
- **Server** (`Session.applySwitchCharacter`): one message and one atomic swap, between ticks, so no tick sees two controllers or none.
  - The soldier left behind becomes a bot, as on a leave (revive state cleared, dismounted, fresh brain, idle, no reconnect claim), but under the same human's command.
  - The soldier taken becomes the human's, as on a join (brain stopped, its order ended as "replaced"), and the client's input tick count carries over.
  - The connection, a new reconnect token and the local player identity (XP) move with the player. Every bot they commanded follows them to their new slot.
  - Nothing else about either soldier changes: position, health, weapon, magazine, pouch and class stay, and the campaign soldier (XP) stays with its slot.
  - Refused (nothing changes, nothing sent) when:
    - the session has not started, or is paused;
    - the target is not a bot this human commands (another's, a human's, their own soldier, no slot);
    - the target is a dropped player's seat still held (T-4.18).
- **Page** (`NetClient`, `main.ts`):
  - on `Possessed`, the page takes the new netId and slot and the new reconnect token, and drops the predictor so it rebuilds from the new soldier's first snapshot;
  - the new soldier's interpolation buffer goes, and the old soldier becomes a remote;
  - the page adopts the soldier's gun, magazine and pouch (`CombatQA.adopt`, `ThrowQA.setCounts`) and marks its class as already in hand, so nothing re-equips over it.
  - Camera, HUD ("you" row) and input follow because they read the NetClient.
- **UI:** the Esc menu's Squad command section shows a **Take control** button on each bot you command. It sends the request and returns to the game.

### Verification

- `session/switch.test.ts` (5 tests):
  - **Solo with bots:** one message leaves exactly one controller. Possessed carries netId, slot, weapon, magazine 7 and the pouch minus one. Both soldiers are field-for-field as they were (position, health, weapon, magazine, pouch, class), and command follows the player (`[3,3,3,-1,3,3]`). Inputs then move the new soldier more than 1 m while the old one stays put, and switching back works.
  - **Two humans:** a switch to the other player's bot is refused; a switch to a bot handed over is allowed. The other player's old soldier, a human's slot, one's own soldier and slot 7 are all refused, as is a stale request after a reassignment.
  - **Repeated and back-to-back requests** resolve in order, with never two controllers.
  - A lobby room refuses a switch before its start.
  - **Reconnect:** the pre-switch token no longer resumes, while the token from Possessed resumes into the switched-to soldier with the same netId. A dropped player's held seat cannot be taken.
- `LocalServer.test.ts`, in-page multiplayer handoff (two NetClients on one LocalServer):
  - after the switch, the page's slot and netId are the new soldier's, with two controllers on the host;
  - the page predicts within 5 cm of the new soldier's position, the old soldier is in the page's remotes and the new one is not;
  - the other client's roster shows slot 0 as a bot under slot 3;
  - walking moves the new soldier by more than 1 m, and the page's prediction stays within 0.5 m of the host's.
- `protocol.test.ts` round-trips both messages. `commandModel.test.ts` checks that only your own bots are switchable.
- `pnpm verify`: 187 files, 2025 tests, all passed.
- `pnpm bot`: OK, peak 0.0116 m.
- Mission scenario (3 seeds): OK, unchanged. `--all-missions`: OK.
- **Browser smoke** (`?squad`, the page with its sparring partner in slot 2):
  - before: the HUD marks "1 qa" as you;
  - the Esc menu offered Take control on slots 2–5 (index);
  - taking slot 4: the menu closed, the HUD marked "4 qa" as you with "1 Bot → you", the camera moved to slot 4's position, and the menu now offers slots 0, 2, 4 and 5;
  - no page errors.
  - This is an agent smoke check with screenshots, not a playtest.

### Not done / open

- Local input sent in the round trip before the host's answer lands on the new soldier (at most one round trip of movement). The page's prediction starts from the host's position, so it is not a visible correction.
- There is no key binding for switching yet; it is in the Esc menu only. A binding (for example cycling through your bots) is a small follow-up if wanted.
- Whether switching reads well in play is the owner's playtest.
