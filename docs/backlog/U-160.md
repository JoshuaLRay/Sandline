Epic: Map implementation. Pillar: P6 (objective missions; Mission 1's road route).
Size: L (terrain generator, its proofs and regenerated evidence).
Human verdict: batched in U-139 (the road's look and play, judged once the fights exist).
Status, priority, dependencies and branch/PR live in BACKLOG.md.
Parent: [U-150](U-150.md) (first leaf). Depends: U-159. Blocks: [U-161](U-161.md).
Canonical design: [Mission 1 construction specification](../design/maps/qalat-road.md) §§3.2, 5.1, 6.2 and the U-159/U-160 addenda.

## Problem / outcome

After U-159 the bent road was a bare y8 slab: walking off any edge dropped to the
backing plane, and nothing stood between the three fights except the bend
geometry on paper. Players should find every road edge closed by rock they
cannot climb, the A3 and A7 inside corners filled with rock above head height,
and the toll, frontage and forecourt fights hidden from one another in the
built scene, while the ridge bays keep the views §6.2 promises them.

## Scope

Extend U-149's isolated road build with rock everywhere off the walking surface
between the insertion (z84) and the south gate: solid from y5.5 (future basement
rooms stay open) to at least 2.2 m above the road, then the west T-W escarpment
(y26), the T-C inside-corner spurs (A3 y20, A5 y22, A7 y20) and the T-E foot (y20)
to the T-E edge. Build fins F1/F2. Cap the rock 0.3 m under the V1–V3 fans (§6.2)
and leave temporary caps where later leaves open the road (C12-L mouth: U-141;
south gate: U-116). No cover, landmarks, shells, sockets, X ingress, ridge,
bays, C12 stairs, campaign activation, AI, caches or checkpoints (U-161 and later).

Conflict default (ADR-021 step 3/4): with the bend, V2's view of the frontage MG
nest at (-18,200) crossed the new A3 inside corner only 0.9 m above the road, so
that corner could not be both solid and seen through. As in Desert Storm, the MG
sits at the head of the road it covers and the high ground flanks it: A-C06 moves
to (-22,8,222) and AF1 to (-23,8,224), at the A4 bend facing down the west leg;
V2 now sees it over the road and F1 still hides it from V1. Reversible: U-161
places the breastwork and can move it with its tables and sight tests.
