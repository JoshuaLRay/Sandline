# U-051 — A joining player may take any free slot

Epic: **Squad**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [U-021](U-021.md) follow-up, owner decisions 2026-09-29 (D-8 in the roster doc).

## Problem / outcome

Owner decision (2026-09-29, D-8): a joining player may take any free slot, not only the lowest. Today a join takes the lowest free (bot) slot, and U-026 can only switch into a bot after joining.

## Scope

A slot chosen at join (in the room lobby, showing each free slot's character), honoured by the host when it is free and otherwise falling back to the lowest free; a resume keeps its own seat. No wire change is assumed but the Join or a room command may need one (then a protocol bump). Once seated, U-026's switching is unchanged.

## Start here

`packages/server/src/session/Session.ts` (the join and seating path), `packages/client/src/ui/RoomLobby.ts`, `packages/shared/src/net/protocol.ts`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The lobby lists the six characters and lets a joining player pick a free one; a taken one cannot be picked.
- [ ] Two players choosing the same slot at once: one gets it, the other the lowest free, with no duplicate character.
- [ ] A resume returns to the player's own seat.

## Verification

Shared and server tests, then `pnpm verify` and the CI jobs.
