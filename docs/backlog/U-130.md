## Handoff

Implementation authorized by the owner’s next-task request on 2026-10-06.
Branch `task/U-130-patrol-regions`, based on merged U-129/PR #304. No open
implementation PR existed at claim.

Implemented in [PR #305](https://github.com/JoshuaLRay/Sandline/pull/305): optional
per-socket 3D routes and named 3D region unions. Each member keeps its own posture;
only assigned members patrol. Patrols reverse along interior points and pause
exactly 90 locomotion ticks at each endpoint by default, including the initial
post. Combat preempts patrol; its leg, direction and remaining pause resume when
idle. Durable saves and retry retain the phase, 3D route and region, without
process-clock deadlines. Legacy route/save shapes remain accepted.

Regions resolve to actual ground nav polygons at Session startup. Both patrol
directions must have complete legal paths. Every returned segment is checked
against the exact union (including tiny gaps), and temporary nav flags restore
active blockers exactly. Bounded pursuit, cover choice/reservations, priced and
refined group flanks, suppressor spots, capture, lever and gun paths use the
member's legal floor/corridor. A final controller/avoidance/spacing movement
check prevents boundary crossings. Members without regions retain legacy paths.

The real baked-nav fixture has basement, surface, disconnected bridge and a
physical stair connection. Actual Session locomotion proves floor separation,
both stair directions, reverse traversal, initial/end pauses and unassigned
members holding. Reactive combat preemption rejects a reachable other storey
and an insertion goal, with a legal pursuit control. Cover rejects an otherwise
reachable wrong-floor reservation; capture assignment and lever feet goals
require bounded routes. The real range group fixture proves an unbounded flank
exists outside the region while the bounded group refuses it. Retry and JSON
reload retain remaining endpoint ticks and reverse phase; malformed phase fails
the durable parser.

Authoring, corridor constraints and reproduction are in
[COMMANDS.md](../COMMANDS.md#individual-patrols-and-combat-regions-u-130).
A native path crossing a partial boundary polygon is conservatively rejected;
the engine does not clip it or search an alternative inside that polygon.
Authors must include complete traversable corridors, nav surface offsets and
explicit stair/vault transition volumes. No production map/nav bake inputs or
generator outputs changed, so no regeneration is needed. Replacement-map
placements/counts/screens and owner map/play quality remain in U-114–U-117.
