# U-084 — Mission-01: the squad dies in the garrison fight before the upload

Epic: **Enemy AI**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: found while doing U-079 (bots against armour), 2026-10-01.

## Problem / outcome

`sim-run --scenario mission --seeds 10` at the 1-human budget: five of ten seeds (1, 3, 4, 7, 9) fail between 29 and 58 s, with the upload not started and no tank in the game (identical traces with and without the tank script). The host's reason is `all-downed` or `soldier-dead`. In the traced seed (1), three bots run ahead of the rest to the compound entrance (about 8, 71) at 41 s, against the garrison's machine gun and riflemen, and are down within a second or two; the other three arrive later and are downed too. The squad splits and arrives piecemeal.

The mission sim only reports it; nothing asserts on it beyond the completion floor, which U-079 meets with the other five seeds. Outcome wanted: the squad arrives at the garrison together and the opening fight is not a coin-flip, so the completion floor measures the rest of the mission.

## Scope

Find why the bots separate (formation leads, the sim's leader's orders, or the garrison's trigger distance) and fix the cause, not the sim. Observations first: which bots, which orders, who killed them.

## Start here

`tools/src/scenarios/mission.ts` (`createMissionLeader`), `server/src/ai/friendly/formation.ts`, the `garrison` group in `data/encounters/mission-01.json`; run seed 1 and print slot positions and orders each second from 30 s.

## Handoff / evidence

**Observations (not hypotheses).**

- The mission rule changed, not the fight. Over 10 seeds at the 1-human budget the same scenario completes 100% at U-011 (5a38d45), 90% just before `dadd365` (mission fails on the first death or all six down), 40% at it, then 30% with the tank, and 10% over 20 seeds on 2026-10-01. The 6-human budget goes 100% → 50% → 10% → 0%. A mission-01 casualty used to be survivable; now the first death ends the run.
- Results swing with any AI change: U-083's bounds (60 m priced range, 48 candidates) took 10 seeds from 30%/10% to 0%/0%; loosening only one of them gave 0%/10% or 30%/0%. With 20 samples at these rates the standard error is about two completions, so a 10-seed completion rate cannot tell a small change from noise. CI runs 3 seeds and does not assert completion, so none of it was seen before.
- In 20 one-human seeds the first soldier down is mostly the assault fireteam (slots 3-5) at the compound entrance around 40 s, a rifleman within 2-7 m, while the overwatch team is 10-15 s behind (its route is longer). The scripted lead gave each fireteam its own route with no coordination.
- Bots wait 30 s or more at a stop hurt (10-33 HP) with three health kits each: orders pre-empt the heal branch, and a bot never healed itself.
- At 6 humans a group spawns at the east lane as the squad sprints between stops in the open, and four bots are down within four seconds; at the upload an extra wave arrives at the compound as the garrison falls. These are not changed here (U-085).

**What changed.**

- `tools/src/scenarios/missionLeader.ts` + `mission.json` `groupUpSeconds` (45): a fireteam does not start its last leg while another has not started its own, for up to that long. This is the scripted lead standing in for a human, not game code.
- Game AI: `kitSelf` / `healSelf` (`actions/friendly.ts`, `data/trees/friendly.json`, `SquadView.needsKit`): a bot hurt below `bot.kitBelowFraction` with a kit, under no order or a hold or a finished move, and not pressured, uses a kit on itself where it stands (the human's kit path; 10 s). A bot moving, attacking or reviving does not stop for it.
