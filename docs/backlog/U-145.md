# U-145 — Use supply caches through approved mobile controls

Epic: Map implementation. Status, dependencies and work live in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: U-134/U-112 mobile-use acceptance; split before claiming U-134 on 2026-10-07.

## Problem / outcome

U-134 requires mobile cache choice and use, but the
[approved mobile design](../design/mobile-spectator-commander.md) lets mobile
participants watch and command bots. U-133 rejects `SupplySelect` from spectators
and requires held soldier interaction input. A touch button alone cannot use a
cache in the production mobile path.

## Owner decision — 2026-10-08

The owner approved the recommended **commander resupply** design and requested
that this decision be recorded and merged in the documentation.

A phone participant selects one eligible bot they command and one compatible
supply type, then orders that bot to collect it from a cache while remaining in
the spectator/commander view. The host validates command ownership, bot
eligibility and cache use, and owns transfers, stock and progress. Watching a
human does not grant resupply authority. Mobile manual walking, aiming and firing
remain excluded under ADR-002. Supply-only temporary possession was not selected.

This approves the control design, not an implementation or a UI/play verdict.
U-134's desktop and physical-phone acceptance remain pending.

  orders the bot to collect it, can interrupt host use, and sees authoritative
  stock/progress through the commander interface.
- Full/incompatible/depleted choices consume no time/stock. Contention, changed
  permissions, human targets, focus loss, menu entry and reconnect remain safe.
- Reproducible browser and real Session coverage, `pnpm verify`, client build,
  applicable budgets/generators and all four latest-head CI jobs pass.
