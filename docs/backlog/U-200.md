# U-200 — Bots pick up, swap and hand over equipment on order

Epic: Squad command reliability ([U-203](U-203.md)). Pillar: P2, P8.
Size: M. Human verdict: none (engineering).
Status, priority, dependencies and branch/PR live in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: owner, 2026-10-16 — "Picking up, exchanging, and using equipment" for AI squadmates; loot ownership with bots (LOOT-PROGRESSION §9, L-15).

## Problem / outcome

Bots never pick up or drop anything (the session skips autonomous slots). With
equipment freedom and loot, a one-human squad must be able to have a bot take
the rocket launcher off the ground, swap to the sniper rifle the squad found,
or hand its weapon to the player. Outcome: ordered pick-up, swap and hand-over
for bots, with items owned per the loot design.

## Scope

- Orders (via U-198's chosen equipment UI): pick up the item at a point; give
  my held item to soldier X; drop my held item.
- The same host validation as a human (handedness, carry rule from U-194).
- No autonomous looting by bots (L-15 default).

## Read first

- `packages/server/src/session/Session.ts` (`takePickupAt`, `dropHeld`, autonomous skip in input handling); U-194 rules; U-198 decision; LOOT-PROGRESSION §9

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An ordered bot walks to and picks up an item it may carry; refusals are reported.
- [ ] A bot can hand its held item to a named squadmate; both inventories update atomically.
- [ ] No duplication under pick-up races with humans (headless test).
