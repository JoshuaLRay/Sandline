## Scope and acceptance

carriageway/4 m shoulders, turning aprons, all A-C cover and landmark shells,
road spurs and X0–X6 motor-pool/service ingress. Prove three distinct road fights,
≤90 m ordinary firing lanes, support/clearance and full rotating tank sweep from
X0 through every bend to A1. Reserve the exact bridge void/abutments for U-140;
prove screening of X0 without moving it or changing warning timing. Recheck
insertion occlusion with the new geometry. No new campaign integration, AI tuning,
cache or checkpoint implementation. Include actual controller/nav travel and
reproducible captures, affected generation, budgets, `pnpm verify` and all four
latest-head CI jobs. Human map/play quality remains pending. Split further before
claiming if this package exceeds one focused PR.
