# U-158 — RPG gunner: RPG-7 in hand, wind-up, launch and trail

Epic: Enemy AI (Conflict parity). Pillar: P4 / P7. Parent: [U-155](U-155.md).
Size: S. Human verdict: batched in U-119 (Mission 1 review) once placed in a mission.
Status, priority, dependencies and branch/PR live in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).

## Problem / outcome

U-155's presentation half. U-157 replicates the RPG gunner's archetype, its
`aiming` wind-up and a `launcher` bit for what is in its hands; the client
still draws it with its rifle and a rocket in flight as a bare cylinder.

## Scope

- The fighter drawn with the generated `rpg7` (`weaponModels.ts` already maps
  the enemy `rocket` to it) while `launcher` is set, its AK otherwise.
- The wind-up readable at range while `aiming` is set: the launcher shouldered
  and a cue of its own (the tank's lock only shows as a flash when it ends, in
  `tankLook.ts`), and the launch flash when it ends.
- A smoke trail behind every rocket in flight (the squad's and the enemy's),
  pooled like the other effects; the existing launch and flight sounds and the
  explosion are reused — add an audio recipe only if a distinct enemy launch
  is wanted.
- Reproducible capture steps (`?enemies=rpg`) for the U-119 batch.

## Read first

`client/src/character/remoteSoldiers.ts` (`remoteSoldierState`),
`client/src/weapons/weaponModels.ts`, `client/src/weapons/effects.ts`,
`client/src/main.ts` (projectile meshes, `playWorldEvents`),
`client/src/character/tankModel.ts` (how `aiming` is drawn).
