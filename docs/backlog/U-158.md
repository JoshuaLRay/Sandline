# U-158 — RPG gunner: RPG-7 in hand, wind-up, launch and trail

Epic: Enemy AI (Conflict parity). Pillar: P4 / P7. Parent: [U-155](U-155.md).
Size: S. Human verdict: batched in U-119 (Mission 1 review) once placed in a mission.
Status, priority, dependencies and branch/PR live in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).

## Problem / outcome

U-155's presentation half. U-157 replicates the RPG gunner's archetype, its
`aiming` wind-up and a `launcher` bit for what is in its hands; the client
still draws it with its rifle and a rocket in flight as a bare cylinder.

## Scope

- The fighter drawn with the generated `rpg7` (`weaponModels.ts` already maps
  the enemy `rocket` to it) while `launcher` is set, its AK otherwise.
- The wind-up readable at range while `aiming` is set: the launcher shouldered
  and a cue of its own (the tank's lock only shows as a flash when it ends, in
  `tankLook.ts`), and the launch flash when it ends.
- A smoke trail behind every rocket in flight (the squad's and the enemy's),
  pooled like the other effects; the existing launch and flight sounds and the
  explosion are reused — add an audio recipe only if a distinct enemy launch
  is wanted.
- Reproducible capture steps (`?enemies=rpg`) for the U-119 batch.

## Read first

`client/src/character/remoteSoldiers.ts` (`remoteSoldierState`),
`client/src/weapons/weaponModels.ts`, `client/src/weapons/effects.ts`,
`client/src/main.ts` (projectile meshes, `playWorldEvents`),
`client/src/character/tankModel.ts` (how `aiming` is drawn).

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] The RPG gunner holds an RPG-7 with `launcher` set and its rifle otherwise.
- [x] The wind-up is visible; the launch, trail and explosion are drawn and heard.
- [x] Client tests cover the held-item choice and the trail pool.

## Verification

Client unit tests; `pnpm verify`; capture steps; all four CI jobs. Look and
feel are reviewed with Mission 1's batched verdict (U-119).

## Handoff / evidence

[#328](https://github.com/JoshuaLRay/Sandline/pull/328). `remoteSoldierState`: the launcher's projectile (`rocket` → enemy `rpg7`) while `launcher` is set, the archetype's rifle otherwise; never for an archetype without a launcher. `launcherLook.ts`: carried low (the rig's new `lower` dip), up onto the shoulder over 0.3 s for `aiming`, with a pulsing warhead glint that is the same size on screen at any range. `rocketFx.ts`: a pooled trail (320 puffs in one points cloud, one draw call; per-puff size via a pinned shader line, per-puff alpha) behind every drawn rocket, the squad's ghost included, and a launch flash at the muzzle and open rear with backblast smoke when a new rocket appears beside a drawn launcher (`RemoteSoldiers.launcherNear`, 4 m / 0.6 s window for the last rocket). Existing launch/flight sounds and explosion reused; no new recipe, no `gen:audio`.
- Conflict default: the wind-up is the counterplay window (U-157), so it reads at range as the tube coming up plus a glint, like the tank's lock lamp.
- `pnpm verify`: pass (315 files / 3302 tests). New: `launcherLook.test.ts`, `rocketFx.test.ts`, RPG cases in `remoteSoldiers.test.ts`.
- Headless Chromium on the production build (`?enemies=rpg`): no shader/page errors; every launch matched to the gunner; glint visible at ~40 m; the trail draws as one plume.
- Capture steps: `pnpm host` is not needed — `pnpm --filter @sandline/client dev`, open `localhost:5173/?enemies=rpg`, choose Practise here, stay at the spawn line with the squad bunched. About every 10 s (four rockets, a fresh gunner after a kill) the gunner at ~46 m shoulders the RPG-7 with an orange glint (~1.2 s), fires (flash, backblast, trail, launch/flight sound, explosion). Walk ~25 m (W for 4–5 s) to see the RPG-7 in hand closer. The QA panel (H) shows `rockets … launches … wind-ups …`.
- Human verdict: look and feel pending, batched in U-119. Criteria: all three.
