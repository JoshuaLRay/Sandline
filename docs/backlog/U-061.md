# U-061 — The captured state: persistence, slot rules and retry behaviour

Epic: **Campaign**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [U-032](U-032.md), owner decisions 2026-09-30 (all the proposed defaults, except that capture is allowed after 2 s down rather than 10 s).

## Problem / outcome

A character can be a prisoner. The campaign remembers it across missions, the roster will not let anyone play it, and retry/restart treat it as decided in [U-032](U-032.md).

## Scope

- Campaign file: `captured: boolean` and `prisoner: {x, y, z} | null` per soldier (additive and optional, like U-060's `world`; absent means free). Validation on load; an older save loads with nobody captured.
- Session: a `captured` flag per slot. A captured slot cannot be claimed by a join, a possess or a class switch; its bot (if any) is stood down; the scoreboard and roster mark it. Rank and XP are untouched.
- `captureCharacter(slot, at)` and `freeCharacter(slot)` as the two calls U-062 and U-063 make; nothing here decides *when*.
- Retry: a retry restores the checkpoint, which has nobody captured (a capture made after the checkpoint is undone). Restart: clears every prisoner. Mission complete: the captured flag and location are written to the campaign file.
- Interaction with U-059: captures are not part of the checkpoint world; document that in the code.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `captured` and `prisoner` round-trip through the campaign file; a save without them loads as before; malformed values are refused with a fallback.
- [ ] A captured slot cannot be joined, possessed or switched into, and is reported on the roster and scoreboard.
- [ ] A retry undoes a capture made after the checkpoint; a full restart clears all; completing a mission keeps them.
- [ ] Rank and XP survive capture and freeing.
- [ ] Unit and session tests cover each rule.

## Handoff / evidence
