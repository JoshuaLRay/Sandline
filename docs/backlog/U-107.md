Epic: Map design. Status, priority and work link live in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).

rectangular lane revision is insufficient and requests a complete authored map:
spawn naturally out of sight of every enemy route, an obvious road to the prisoner

with enemies on all paths. Deliver highly specific Markdown and a new PR.

## Scope and delivered design

Documentation only: replace the current map brief with a comprehensive,
coordinate-based [construction specification](../design/maps/qalat-road.md),
update the mission summary/shared guidance and add three construction diagrams.
The playable U-106 map remains in place until follow-up implementation. Preserve
historical evidence and unfulfilled human/combat-completion acceptance honestly.

The specification defines Juniper Hollow insertion, two reveal bends, the supply
road, climbing ridge with three overlooks/two crossings, continuous underground
depot with large vaults, the outpost's rooms/doors, every guard socket/patrol,
cover, finite supplies, events/checkpoints, tank road and protected extraction.
It explicitly supersedes old coordinates, the surface riverbed flank, three C12
openings, immediate reserve release and tank parking inside the spawn.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Naturally screened spawn has specific terrain/overhang geometry and a
  whole-spawn, all-enemy-route occlusion acceptance test.
- [x] Irregular route spines, dimensions, heights, stairs, room bounds, doorways,
  connections and boundaries make the map reproducible from this specification.
- [x] Underground rooms form a fully covered route; floor/ceiling structure and
  overlapping road/vault/bridge behaviour are specified.
- [x] Enemies on all three paths have exact positions, counts, patrols, facing,
  alert bounds and reinforcement behaviour.
- [x] Outpost, rescue/radio, supplies, escort, tank and extraction are fully sequenced;
  saves, retries, early kills and route combinations have defined outcomes.
- [x] Engine prerequisites are identified from source; unsupported fields are not
  presented as existing JSON capabilities.
- [x] Surface plan, underground plan and vertical sections accompany the Markdown.
- [x] Mission/campaign/authoring guidance and historical task pointers are reconciled.
