# U-018 — Equip dropped guns into the primary slot

Epic: **Loot**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [owner feedback, 2026-09-26](2026-09-26-feedback.md), plus any legacy bug named below.

## Problem / outcome

Enemy guns should be usable as the player’s primary, not a cosmetic pickup or a pistol replacement.

## Scope

Implement a server-authoritative pickup interaction that atomically replaces the selected primary, preserves the pistol and handles ammo/reload state. Reversible default: the replaced primary becomes one world pickup with its current ammo, using U-017’s lifecycle.

### Design

- **The press is the host's to judge** (`Session.takePickupAt`), in the interact chain after a revive's claim and an emplacement. It succeeds only if all of these hold:
  - the soldier is alive (not downed or dead), not vaulting and not on a gun;
  - the nearest pickup is within `PICKUPS.reachM` (2.2 m, new in `pickups.json`) of the eye;
  - there is a clear line from the eye to it.

  The press is all the page sends. No message names a pickup or carries rounds, so a stale id or a client-supplied magazine has nothing to ride on.
- **Atomic, on one tick:**
  - the pickup leaves the ground;
  - its gun becomes the slot's `primary`, in hand with the pickup's rounds;
  - the primary it replaces goes down where the soldier stands, as a pickup of its own, through U-017's lifecycle (netId band, cap, 120 s, cleared by a retry). It keeps its rounds when it was in hand, or a full magazine when it was stowed, since a stowed gun is drawn fresh (the existing rule).
  - The class's pistol is untouched.
- **A race:** presses resolve in slot order within the tick. The first takes the gun, and the second finds nothing at that spot. There is one winner, one transfer, and no duplicate.
- **What a soldier carries** (`Session.carries`, used by both Equip and Fire): on a class loadout, the class's guns, with the first replaced by the picked-up primary; on a free loadout (the range), anything. A Fire naming a gun the soldier does not carry is now refused. Before this change it silently swapped the soldier to any gun it named, so a picked-up gun could otherwise be undone, or any gun used.
- **The page:**
  - the Weapon component carries the soldier's `primary` (protocol 41), read by `NetClient.primary`;
  - key 1 draws it and key 2 the pistol (`weaponIndexForKey`'s `primary`);
  - when the host's primary changes and the host has it in hand, the page adopts it with the host's rounds and plays the equip sound. The HUD (`combat.weapon`), the view and body models (`heldId`), firing, reload and gun sounds all read the gun in hand, so they follow;
  - the prompt reads `E  TAKE THE MARKSMAN RIFLE` within reach. It is a hint; the host judges the press.
- **Policies:**
  - **Respawn:** a picked-up gun does not come back. The class's primary returns, in hand if the primary was (`restorePrimary`). The death drops nothing.
  - **Checkpoint retry or restart:** the same, and the ground is cleared (U-017).
  - **Disconnect:** a held seat keeps what it picked up, as it keeps its class and pouch (U-024), and a resume within the grace returns with it. A bot filling the seat carries it too (ADR-001: a slot's state persists).
  - **A U-026 switch:** each soldier keeps its own primary.
  - **Two primaries** (U-022) is out of scope. Today there is one primary and it is replaced.

### Verification

- **`client/src/weapons/pickupEquip.test.ts`:** a real `Session` with class loadouts on greybox-01, and `NetClient`s pressing interact over loopback.
  - **Refused:** out of reach; in reach but behind `as-wall-1`; downed.
  - **Taken:** a Team Leader with 11 rounds in the carbine takes a marksman rifle with 7. It is the primary and in hand with 7, and a carbine with 11 lies where the soldier stands. The page reads primary = marksman and a magazine of marksman/7.
  - **Keys:** the pistol is still carried. The class's old carbine is refused by Equip, and by a Fire naming it, with no shot and no swap. The new primary equips.
  - **Race:** two Team Leaders press on the same tick. Exactly one has the marksman, the other keeps the carbine, and the ground holds one carbine with no marksman: a single transfer.
  - **Policies:**
    - a dropped player who resumes keeps the marksman;
    - killed and respawned, they have the class's carbine back, full, in hand;
    - taking another gun and then a restart gives the carbine back, with the ground cleared.
- **`client/src/weapons/weaponKey.test.ts`:** key 1 is the host's primary on a class loadout and on the range; 2 stays the pistol; 3–4 on the range are unchanged.
