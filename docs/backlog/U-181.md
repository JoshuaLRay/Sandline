# U-181 — A downed soldier bleeds out over two minutes

Epic: Every soldier matters (owner direction 2026-10-16). Pillar: P5.
Size: S. Human verdict: none (engineering criteria only; the feel is judged in the next batched mission playtest, U-119).
Status, priority, dependencies and branch/PR live in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: owner, 2026-10-16 — "Change the current bleed-out timer from 30 seconds to 120 seconds (2 minutes)… to create more opportunities for enemies to capture downed squad members and for teammates to attempt rescues or revives." Explicitly approved for implementation now. Decision record: [ADR-025](../adr/025-bleed-out-two-minutes.md).

## Problem / outcome

A downed soldier died after 30 s unless revived or healed; the series gave
about two minutes (CP-24). Outcome: 120 s, counted down honestly on every
client, with revive, health kits, capture, checkpoints and mission failure on
death all behaving as before over the longer window. No respawn.

## Scope

- `damage.json` `downed.bleedOutSeconds` 30 → 120 (the only authoritative value).
- The replicated Health `timer` was six bits and clamped at 63 s: widen it to
  seven (127 s) and take the bit from revive/lever progress (seven → six bits,
  sixty-thirds via `progressToWire`/`progressFromWire`), so the per-entity
  snapshot cost and its budget test are unchanged. `PROTOCOL_VERSION` 72 → 73.
- The parser rejects a bleed-out longer than the wire can show.
- Tests updated where they assumed a short window; parity CP-24 updated.

Out of scope: difficulty-dependent windows, respawning, capture tuning.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A soldier left downed dies exactly at 120 s; shots on the body still cut it (damage tests, Session bleed-out test).
- [x] Revive and health kits work throughout the window; a revive started after death does nothing (Session tests).
- [x] An enemy can still capture a character who has been down for longer than the old 30 s (capture test at 60 s down), and still refuses a capture it could not finish in time.
- [x] Every client receives the full countdown (120 s round-trips the Health timer; no 63 s clamp) and revive/lever progress reads back as a percent never ahead of the server.
- [x] Checkpoints are still deferred while anyone is downed and restore everyone up (existing U-059 tests).
- [x] A bleed-out death still fails the mission; no respawn path added (existing U-033 tests).
- [x] Snapshot per-entity budget unchanged; protocol pins at 73.
