# U-068 — Tank weapons: cannon and coaxial machine gun

Epic: **Mission / vehicle combat**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [U-035](U-035.md), owner decisions 2026-09-30 (all the proposed defaults).

## Problem / outcome

The tank threatens the squad, per [U-035](U-035.md), with counterplay for the squad.

## Scope

- **Cannon:** reuses the `rocket` projectile row with tank numbers (slow shell, big blast), fired at the squad's position every **6 s**, telegraphed with a muzzle flash and a short aim-lock tell so players can react. It never fires at a downed character (U-031).
- **Coaxial MG:** a burst weapon on sight within its range, using the existing LMG data.
- The turret tracks targets independently of the hull at a limited rate.
- Target choice: the nearest standing squad soldier it can see; it does not shoot through cover it could not hit.
- All numbers in schema-validated data; the tank fires only while holding a firing position or moving slowly.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] The cannon fires on its cadence with a visible tell and hurts by its blast rules; the MG fires bursts on sight.
- [x] The turret tracks independently of the hull within a turn-rate limit.
- [x] It never targets a downed or captured character.
- [x] Tests cover cadence, the tell, turret tracking and target choice.

## Handoff / evidence

Implemented on `task/U-068-tank-weapons`; awaiting review. No owner playtest verdict is claimed.

**What changed.** `fireTanks` runs each tick after the soldiers' fire. **Target:** the nearest standing squad soldier (alive, so never one who is down or held prisoner) within the cannon's range with a clear line from the muzzle to one of its aim points, so never through cover; it sees by line and range, not by the soldiers' stealth rules. **Turret:** `turretYaw` turns to the target's bearing at `turretTurnDegPerSec` (45, 4 wire units a tick), apart from the hull, and back to the hull's heading with nobody to shoot. **Gate:** nothing fires unless the tank is at or below `fireMaxSpeedMps` (0.5), so not while it drives, and only with the turret within `alignDeg` of the bearing. **Cannon:** after half an interval from spawn, when ready and aligned it LOCKS: it holds the target's aim point for `tellSeconds` (1 s; replicated as `aiming`, the fourth field of the `Enemy` component, for the flash and the warning), then fires a shell at the LOCKED point, not wherever the target has moved to, so a squad that moves in the tell is missed; then it waits `intervalSeconds` (6). A target that goes down mid-tell cancels it. **Shell:** the `rocket` projectile row with the tank's own numbers (28 m/s, 140 damage, 4.5 m blast; `cannon` in the `vehicle` block), laid high by the drop over the flight; it bursts on the first body it meets, and `bodyAlong` now uses each entity's own shape, so a rocket meets a tank's hull rather than the soldier-sized capsule at its middle. **Coaxial gun:** the tank's `weapon` (the LMG) in the archetype's bursts inside `machineGun.rangeM` (60) with the turret on the bearing, by the archetype's `accuracy`; it does not turn the hull. `PROTOCOL_VERSION` 60.

**Not here.** The muzzle flash, the tell's look and the HUD warning (U-070); the mission's tank path and trigger (U-069); bots choosing to take cover from a tank.

**Evidence.** `packages/server/src/session/tankWeapons.test.ts` (13: nearest-visible target and what it passes over, the turret's rate and independence from the hull, the cadence and the tell, the tell on the wire, the locked point against a target that moved, the splash and what is outside it, no fire while driving and fire once stopped, nothing at someone down, a tell dropped when the target goes down, the gun's range and bursts, no fire with the turret off the bearing), plus the new data tests. `pnpm verify` and the CI jobs: see the PR.
