# U-068 — Tank weapons: cannon and coaxial machine gun

Epic: **Mission / vehicle combat**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [U-035](U-035.md), owner decisions 2026-09-30 (all the proposed defaults).

## Problem / outcome

The tank threatens the squad, per [U-035](U-035.md), with counterplay for the squad.

## Scope

- **Cannon:** reuses the `rocket` projectile row with tank numbers (slow shell, big blast), fired at the squad's position every **6 s**, telegraphed with a muzzle flash and a short aim-lock tell so players can react. It never fires at a downed character (U-031).
- **Coaxial MG:** a burst weapon on sight within its range, using the existing LMG data.
- The turret tracks targets independently of the hull at a limited rate.
- Target choice: the nearest standing squad soldier it can see; it does not shoot through cover it could not hit.
- All numbers in schema-validated data; the tank fires only while holding a firing position or moving slowly.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The cannon fires on its cadence with a visible tell and hurts by its blast rules; the MG fires bursts on sight.
- [ ] The turret tracks independently of the hull within a turn-rate limit.
- [ ] It never targets a downed or captured character.
- [ ] Tests cover cadence, the tell, turret tracking and target choice.
