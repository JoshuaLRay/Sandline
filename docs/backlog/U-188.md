# U-188 — Multiplayer loot ownership and synchronisation

Epic: Loot and progression ([U-183](U-183.md)). Pillar: P8.
Size: L. Human verdict: none (engineering); feel in U-193.
Status, priority, dependencies and branch/PR live in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: owner, 2026-10-16 — required epic phase 5; "The design must work equally well with one human or six humans."

## Problem / outcome

Implement the ownership model chosen in U-184/U-185 (default E4: shared
physical items in missions, squad/campaign ownership for persistence): who can
take what, how the stash is equipped between missions, and no duplication or
theft, with any mix of humans and bots.

## Scope

- Authoritative pick-up races (two players, one item), bot finds (U-200),
  join/leave/switch/disconnect/capture behaviour, stash equip permissions
  (default: a soldier's occupant, host override), reliable inventory messages.
- Conservation invariant enforced and property-tested.

## Read first

- U-185 specification; U-186 ADR; [ADR-001](../adr/001-six-slot-squad.md) addenda (command, switching); [spectator command authority](../design/spectator-command-authority.md)
- `packages/server/src/session/Session.ts` (`takePickupAt`, `dropHeld`, `releaseSlot`, `applySwitchCharacter`)

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Headless tests: simultaneous pick-up, join mid-mission, leave with items, switch, disconnect and resume, capture and rescue, checkpoint retry — no item duplicated or lost.
- [ ] Stash equip permissions enforced by the host.
- [ ] One human + five bots and six humans produce the same item outcomes.
