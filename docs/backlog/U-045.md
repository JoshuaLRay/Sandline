# U-045 — Weapon and device slots 1–6 with swap-to-use

Epic: **Controls**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [U-044](U-044.md), owner direction and answers, 2026-09-29.

## Problem / outcome

Every weapon or device must be drawn by its slot key before it is used: 1 primary, 2 secondary, 3 knife, 4 grenades, 5 equipment, 6 health kits. Nothing throws or fires without first swapping to it.

## Scope

Slot model and key routing on the desktop client and the server's authority over what is in hand. Move the grenade from the `G`/`5` throw-and-ready keys to slot 4 with swap-to-use, and free `G` for U-029's drop-gun. The Support's second primary occupies slot 2 (owner-confirmed). Slot 5 holds the launcher for the character who has one until U-048; slot 6 is empty until U-047 (an empty slot does nothing). The aim and use buttons are U-046. Onboarding and help text update with the keys. Desktop only: mobile is spectator-only.

## Start here

`packages/client/src/weapons/weaponKey.ts`, `packages/client/src/input/LocalInput.ts` (`THROW_KEY`), `packages/client/src/weapons/pouchTrigger.ts`, the pouch and Equip handling in `packages/server/src/session/Session.ts`, `packages/client/src/ui/onboarding/hints.json`, U-005, U-018, U-022, U-029.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Keys 1–6 select their slot; a slot with nothing in it is a no-op.
- [ ] Grenades and launchers are used only after swapping to their slot; `G` no longer throws.
- [ ] The swap is server-authoritative and survives reconnect, possession and retry like the current weapon selection.
- [ ] Help/onboarding text names the new keys.
- [ ] Tests cover routing, the empty slot, swap timing and the desktop-only boundary.

## Verification

Client input tests, hosted-session tests for the swap, `pnpm verify`. Feel of the swap timing is a playtest judgement.

Not started.
