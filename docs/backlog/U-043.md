# U-043 — Distinct report and handling sounds for the roster weapons

Epic: **Audio**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: split from [U-020](U-020.md) on 2026-09-29 (that card required splitting before READY: more than one substantial new weapon behavior, plus art, audio and wire changes).

## Problem / outcome

After U-040 the four roster guns borrow the carbine's or marksman's report. The SMG, scoped carbine and snipers (including a bolt-cycle sound for the bolt-action) need their own.

## Scope

Add sound recipes to the synth data, map them in `weaponSounds.json` and regenerate the renders (edit sources, then regenerate). Keep to the existing audio pipeline and budgets.

## Start here

`packages/shared/src/data/audio/sounds.json`, `weaponSounds.json`, `docs/COMMANDS.md` (audio rows), `packages/client/public/audio/renders.json`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Each roster gun has its own near and far report (`smg-`, `carbine-scoped-`, `sniper-semi-`, `sniper-bolt-` near/far); the bolt-action has a `bolt-cycle` cue 0.55 s after each shot.
- [x] Renders regenerated (`pnpm gen:audio`) and the audio tests/bounds pass.
- [ ] Listening review by the owner is pending; no listening verdict is claimed.

## Verification

Weapon sound tests, audio generation, `pnpm verify`.

## Handoff / evidence

Branch `task/U-043-roster-sounds`.
- Nine new recipes in `sounds.json`, derived from the existing ones by changing pitch, decay and filter cutoffs (SMG: higher and shorter than the carbine; scoped carbine: deeper and longer; semi sniper: deeper than the marksman; bolt sniper: deepest and longest), plus `bolt-cycle`, two metallic clacks 0.2 s apart taken from the reload-bolt recipe. Mapped in `weaponSounds.json`; the renders are regenerated and committed.
- `weaponSounds.json` guns may carry an optional `cycle: { sound, delaySeconds }`, validated (`parseWeaponSounds`). Only the bolt-action gun has one. `cycleSoundPlan` (client) reads it and `main.ts` plays it after the local shot and after remote shots by that gun, through `setTimeout`.
- Tests: distinct sounds per gun, cycle only on the bolt action and before the next shot is due, validation errors by name, and the client plan.
- **Load-time budget:** the first CI run failed `check:load-time` (34.9 s against the 30 s gate at 4 Mbit/s; U-042's models had already taken it to 28.8 s). The 2.5 MB of new WAVs were the difference. I did not loosen the gate. Instead a recipe may be `lazy: true` (validated in `sounds.json`; omitted, and so hashed as before, when false): the engine skips it at start, loads it on first use or `audio.preload(ids)`, and misses that first play. All nine new recipes are lazy, so they cost nothing until a roster gun is fired; U-021, which seats the characters, should `preload` the seated soldier's guns.
- These are synthesised recipe variations, not recorded or hand-designed sounds. Whether they sound like an SMG, a scoped rifle and two snipers is the owner's listening verdict and is **pending**; none is claimed.
