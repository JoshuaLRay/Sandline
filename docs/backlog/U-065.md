# U-065 — Campaign mission 1: rescue, escort and the armoured ambush

Epic: **Campaign design**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [U-032](U-032.md); retitled and re-scoped by the owner's campaign decisions, 2026-10-01 ([design doc](../design/CAMPAIGN.md)).

## Problem / outcome

The first mission of the ten-mission season (it was "a mission that holds a rescue"). The squad frees an **unarmed POW**
from the compound he is held in; he joins them as a seventh member they can command (go / stay / follow) and spectate but
not control. After he is freed a **tank rides in along the route home**; the squad must destroy it, then reach the
extraction point **with all seven alive**. Three lanes, 30–45 minutes, Afghanistan after 9/11, objectives in any order
where that makes sense, and no obligation to clear every enemy.

## Scope

INBOX until its brief is written and its dependencies exist. The brief (the section 4 template) comes first: the lanes and
what each offers, where the compound and the extraction are, the route the tank takes and what happens if the squad
leaves by another lane, which objectives are required and which optional, and the set pieces. Then the level (sized by
the U-076 spike), encounter, script, mission file and verification, as separate cards. The tank, its path trigger and
the rescue objective already exist (U-066 to U-070, U-063). The POW is U-075; objectives in any order is U-074; bots
fighting the tank is U-079.

### Owner map feedback — 2026-10-03

The owner reports too little map art, a grid-like walking surface and insufficient verticality. Follow-ups are U-097 (connected playable elevation), then U-096 (authored map art). This is a request for revisions, not a visual acceptance verdict. The owner playtest and full headless completion criteria above remain open.

### Owner lane feedback — 2026-10-04

The owner reports that switching lanes anywhere prevents a true three-lane map.
[U-105](U-105.md) records the shared creation standard and
[Qalat rework](../design/maps/qalat-road.md): direct road assault, connected eastern
overlook support, and a narrower western flank with no intermediate crossings.
The documentation PR does not implement the geometry or close the playtest gate.
Use that record for the next level rework; prior U-092/U-097 reachability evidence
does not establish the new isolation criteria.

### Full map replacement requested — 2026-10-04

The owner rejects the rectangular valley revision as insufficient and requests a
naturally hidden spawn, obvious primary road, overlooking ridge and a completely
underground basement route, with enemies on every path. [U-107](U-107.md) supplies
the complete replacement map/mission construction specification and diagrams.
U-106's merged implementation and evidence remain historical/current-build work;
its checks do not establish acceptance of the new map. Actual replacement work
