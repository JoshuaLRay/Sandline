# U-087 — Squad tactics: bounding, focus fire and fighting as a group

Epic: **Enemy AI**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [U-085](U-085.md) (owner's decision 2026-10-01: not now).

## Problem / outcome

The six-bot squad loses mission-01 at the six-human budget (0 of 20 seeds) and wins the one-human budget about half the time. The traces in U-085 show several riflemen firing at a squad that crosses open ground, and a wave spawning at the objective as the squad enters. U-086 showed the simple fix (fight from cover) is worse in a 1v1. What is missing is group play: one fireteam moving while the other covers, focus fire on one target, and not entering a killing ground all at once.
