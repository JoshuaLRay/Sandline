# U-030 — Exclude downed characters from enemy gunfire targets

Epic: **Enemy AI**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [owner feedback, 2026-09-27](2026-09-27-feedback.md).

## Problem / outcome

Downed characters should not be targeted by enemy fire at all. The enemy must choose another eligible target or stop firing when its current target becomes downed; a downed character may later be the subject of a separate capture interaction (U-031).

## Scope

Exclude downed squad characters from enemy firearm target acquisition and continued engagement. Cover target selection, target revalidation and any firing path that can keep shooting at a character after that character is downed. Preserve the existing rules for living targets and the distinct future prisoner capture interaction.

## Start here

Confirm the active paths before editing; these are investigation entry points.

- `packages/server/src/ai` — enemy target selection and combat behavior
- `packages/shared/src` — shared AI and vitality state
- `packages/server/src/session/Session.ts` — authoritative health/downed state and damage

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An enemy does not acquire a downed character as a firearm target.
- [ ] If the current target becomes downed, the enemy stops firing at that character and reselects an eligible living target or ceases fire.
- [ ] The rule applies consistently to all enemy firearm behaviors, including any suppression/retarget path that otherwise continues firing at a downed character.
- [ ] A downed character remains eligible for the separate capture behavior specified by U-031; firearm-target exclusion does not prevent capture interactions.
- [ ] Deterministic AI regressions cover target acquisition and a target transitioning from alive to downed; relevant AI tests and `pnpm verify` pass.

## Verification

Use a deterministic enemy-AI fixture with more than one squad target, plus a case where the currently engaged target becomes downed. Verify no further enemy damage/fire is directed at the downed character and that living-target behavior remains intact.
