## Problem / outcome

The owner wants enemy weapons to be dropped and equippable in the primary slot.

## Scope

On enemy death, spawn one authoritative world pickup representing the actual held firearm and its defined ammo state. Add client representation and lifecycle; U-018 implements equipping.

- [ ] Unsupported items such as mounted weapons/launchers have a documented policy; the primary firearm path is covered first.
