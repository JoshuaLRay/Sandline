# U-086 — Attack orders fight from cover instead of standing in the open

Epic: **Enemy AI**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: found while investigating U-085, 2026-10-01 ([U-085](U-085.md) has the traces).

## Problem / outcome

A bot under an `attack` order with a clear line to its target stands where it is and fires (`orderAttack`, `ai/friendly/orders.ts`); with no line it sprints to a firing position at the target. It takes cover only once it is already hit or suppressed. Against riflemen who are holding and firing, a standing bot is down in about 1.5 s at 20 m. The same bots fighting freely (no order) peek, fire and hide (`rifleman.ts`, measured by `cover-duel`: exposed while idle about 7-14%).

Outcome wanted: an ordered attack fights as the free fight does where cover is to be had, and advances only when there is no line, so an ordered squad takes casualties more like the free one.

## Scope

Observations first: reproduce the U-085 trace (6-human seed 1, 24-34 s) and record exposure (ticks standing in a target's line) per bot. Then the smallest change in the attack leaf (or the tree) that uses the peek/hide cycle when a line exists and cover is within reach, keeping the formation-band rule off for ordered bots. Not in scope: fire-and-manoeuvre between fireteams, the spawner's rules, the scripted lead.
