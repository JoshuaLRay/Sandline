# U-086 — Attack orders fight from cover instead of standing in the open

Epic: **Enemy AI**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: found while investigating U-085, 2026-10-01 ([U-085](U-085.md) has the traces).

## Problem / outcome

A bot under an `attack` order with a clear line to its target stands where it is and fires (`orderAttack`, `ai/friendly/orders.ts`); with no line it sprints to a firing position at the target. It takes cover only once it is already hit or suppressed. Against riflemen who are holding and firing, a standing bot is down in about 1.5 s at 20 m. The same bots fighting freely (no order) peek, fire and hide (`rifleman.ts`, measured by `cover-duel`: exposed while idle about 7-14%).

Outcome wanted: an ordered attack fights as the free fight does where cover is to be had, and advances only when there is no line, so an ordered squad takes casualties more like the free one.

## Scope

Observations first: reproduce the U-085 trace (6-human seed 1, 24-34 s) and record exposure (ticks standing in a target's line) per bot. Then the smallest change in the attack leaf (or the tree) that uses the peek/hide cycle when a line exists and cover is within reach, keeping the formation-band rule off for ordered bots. Not in scope: fire-and-manoeuvre between fireteams, the spawner's rules, the scripted lead.

## Start here

`docs/backlog/U-085.md` (traces and the experiments already tried), `ai/friendly/orders.ts` (`orderAttack`, `coverFrom`), `ai/actions/rifleman.ts` (`takeCover`, `peek`, `hide`; `coverNear`), `tools/src/scenarios/coverDuel.ts`.

## Acceptance criteria (revised: the premise was tested and did not hold)

- [x] Exposure of an ordered attacker is measured on a deterministic fixture (`ai/friendly/attackCover.test.ts`): 24 starts of a bot ordered to attack a holding rifleman, range 20-44 m.
- [x] The change was tried and its effect measured against the same fixture: it is **worse**, so it is not shipped (below).
- [x] `pnpm verify` green; no game or scenario behaviour changes.

## Handoff / evidence

**Result: no change to the game. The fixture is committed as a regression test.**

On the fixture the current behaviour (stand where there is a line and fire; advance to a firing position when there is none) is better than fighting from cover:

| ordered attacker | bot downed | rifleman downed | mean bot damage | mean exposed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| stands and fires (current) | 0 of 24 | 24 of 24 | 8.9 | 106 ticks |
| free-fight cover cycle (`attackEngaged`: take cover, peek, hide; advance after 6 s without sight) | 5 of 24 | 13 of 24 | 34.2 | 135 ticks |

The cover cycle's cooldown and bursts give up more firepower than the cover saves against one rifleman, and a bot that has hidden can stall. So "ordered attackers stand in the open and that is why the six-human squad dies" is not supported by a 1v1 test: standing and firing wins it. What the traces in U-085 show is several riflemen firing at a squad crossing open ground, which this fixture does not model. A fixture with several attackers against several holders (and a squad that moves in bounds) is the next experiment, not a tweak to the attack leaf.

**Tried and reverted:** `attackEngaged` condition in `orders.ts`; the attack branch in `friendly.json` as a selector over the free-fight sub-tree; `coverNear` null for attack orders in `Session`.

**Not claimed.** Nothing about the six-human mission is fixed here.
