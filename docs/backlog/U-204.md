## Problem / outcome

Enemies today are effectively either at ease or in combat; they hear only the
squad's sounds, never investigate, never raise alarms, and a suppressed weapon
does not exist. Outcome: five authoritative awareness states (Unaware,
Suspicious, Investigating, Alerted, Combat) with search, communication, alarms
and reinforcements, no omniscience, stealth that gives an advantage without
being required, and temporary overhead indicators that make the states
readable while voices are unavailable.
