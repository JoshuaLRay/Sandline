# U-156 — Qalat Road reaches its first playable frame inside the 30 s budget again

Epic: Streaming and load time. Pillar: maintenance (ADR-013's load budget).
Size: S. Human verdict: none (engineering criteria only).
Status, priority, dependencies and branch/PR live in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: owner, 2026-10-09 — "It failed": CI `streaming` red on main and on U-153's
PR #324 at the "Qalat Road first playable frame budget" step.

## Problem / outcome

Observed: `SANDLINE_LOAD_WORLD=qalat-road pnpm check:load-time` took 31,195 ms on
main bb7b8f6 (run 1792), and 30,534 / 31,115 / 30,420 ms on #324. The limit is
under 30,000 ms (ADR-013). Main 61ea4d6 passed with the same code (#323 was docs
only), so Qalat sits on the line and runner noise decides. Every merge is blocked.

Cause (measured locally, 4 Mbit/s throttle): about 9.4 MiB crosses the wire
before the first playable frame; the generated tank (`vehicle-tank`, 1.2 MiB,
~2.3 s at 4 Mbit/s) was in the initial pack although no world needs it for its
first frame — Qalat's tank comes in by mission script, and the code-built
stand-in draws any tank until the model arrives (U-126).

## Scope

Add a `deferred` asset pack, fetched once play can start (beside U-043's audio
release), and move `vehicle-tank` into it. `check:packs` requires every vehicle
in the initial or deferred pack. The 30 s budget is unchanged. Out of scope:
weapon models in the initial pack (T-4.06 keeps every weapon there) and asset
compression.

Pack parser and loader unit tests; `pnpm check:packs`; local
