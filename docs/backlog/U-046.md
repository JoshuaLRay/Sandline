# U-046 — Right click aims, left click uses

Epic: **Controls**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [U-044](U-044.md), owner direction and answers, 2026-09-29.

## Problem / outcome

With the item in hand (U-045), **right click aims** it and **left click uses** it, for every slot: fire a gun, throw a grenade (right click shows the arc), swing the knife, fire a launcher, apply a kit.

## Scope

Unify the aim/use input path across weapons, grenades, knife and equipment on the desktop client, replacing the special-case throw and ready keys. Aim on a slot with nothing to aim (knife, kit) is a no-op. The Support cannot ADS or use first person (U-019, U-021/U-022): right click for the Support is a no-op on guns. Preserve the existing hold-to-fire, reload and ADS behaviour for guns.

## Start here

`packages/client/src/input/LocalInput.ts`, `packages/client/src/weapons/pouchTrigger.ts`, `packages/client/src/weapons/ThrowQA.ts`, `packages/client/src/main.ts` (ADS and throw handling).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Right click aims and left click uses the item in hand for guns, grenades, knife and launcher.
- [ ] Grenade aiming shows the arc on right click and throws on left click.
- [ ] The Support never ADS or enters first person through this path.
- [ ] Existing ADS, fire and reload behaviour is unchanged for guns.

## Verification

Input and pouch-trigger tests, `pnpm verify`; feel is a playtest judgement.
