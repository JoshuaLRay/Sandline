# U-046 — Right click aims, left click uses

Epic: **Controls**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [U-044](U-044.md), owner direction and answers, 2026-09-29.

## Owner refinement (2026-09-29)

For **grenades and C4, hold left click to aim the throw and release to throw it.** **Right click interacts with the equipment:** it **pulls the pin to "cook" a grenade** and **detonates placed C4.** Right click still aims (ADS) guns. This supersedes the "right click aims everything" reading below. UNKNOWN: right click on the rocket launcher (proposal: unchanged, aim); cooked-grenade-in-hand-past-fuse (proposal: it explodes in the hand); a grenade thrown without cooking (proposal: fuse starts on release, as today).

## Problem / outcome

With the item in hand (U-045), **right click aims** it and **left click uses** it, for every slot: fire a gun, throw a grenade (right click shows the arc), swing the knife, fire a launcher, apply a kit.

## Scope

Unify the aim/use input path across weapons, grenades, knife and equipment on the desktop client, replacing the special-case throw and ready keys. Aim on a slot with nothing to aim (knife, kit) is a no-op. The Support cannot ADS or use first person (U-019, U-021/U-022): right click for the Support is a no-op on guns. Preserve the existing hold-to-fire, reload and ADS behaviour for guns.

## Start here

`packages/client/src/input/LocalInput.ts`, `packages/client/src/weapons/pouchTrigger.ts`, `packages/client/src/weapons/ThrowQA.ts`, `packages/client/src/main.ts` (ADS and throw handling).

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Guns: right click aims, left click fires (unchanged). Knife: left click swings.
- [x] Grenade: hold left click to aim (the arc shows), release to throw; right click pulls the pin and starts the fuse (cook), and the throw carries the remaining fuse.
- [ ] C4 (with U-048): hold left click to aim, release to throw (Support) or place (others); right click detonates. **Not in this task:** there is no C4 until U-048; it reuses the same right-click press and the `cook` input.
- [x] The Support never ADS or enters first person through this path.
- [x] Existing ADS, fire and reload behaviour is unchanged for guns.

**What changed.** Hold-to-aim and release-to-throw for a grenade already worked (U-045). New: right click with a thrown item in hand pulls the pin. The page sends `INPUT_BUTTONS.cook` (right button held); the host starts the fuse on the press (a hold does not re-cook the next grenade), and a throw carries the fuse used up (`Throw` is unchanged: the host's own clock says how long it was cooked, capped so a sliver of fuse is always left). A cooked grenade not thrown goes off in the hand, where its soldier stands (dead or alive), and is spent. The page mirrors it (ghost starts as old as the cook, a "Pin pulled" note) and right click does not aim (no ADS/first person) while a thrown item is in hand. Guns, the knife and the rocket launcher keep their behaviour (right click aims a gun and the rocket's arc).

**Evidence.** `packages/client/src/weapons/cook.test.ts` (end to end over loopback: cooked throw age from the host's clock, uncooked whole fuse, in-hand detonation spends it and hurts the holder, held right click is one press, no pin with a gun) and a `ThrowQA` test for the cooked ghost.

**Proposals (labelled, reversible).** Switching away from a cooked grenade does not defuse it (the pin is out); the rocket launcher's right click is unchanged (aim); an uncooked throw starts its fuse on release as today.
