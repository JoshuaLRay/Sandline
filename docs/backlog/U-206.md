# U-206 — Detection inputs: weapon noise, muffled sound and enemies hearing the fight

Epic: Stealth and enemy awareness ([U-204](U-204.md)). Pillar: P4.
Size: M. Human verdict: none (engineering); feel in U-212.
Status, priority, dependencies and branch/PR live in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: owner, 2026-10-16 — detection should account for "distance, field of view, terrain occlusion, movement and stance, weapon noise, suppressed weapons, nearby combat, previous knowledge … Avoid omniscient AI or instantaneous map-wide awareness." Design §3.

## Problem / outcome

Every gun is heard at 150 m through walls; enemies hear only squad-made
sounds, so a fight next door is silent to them while a shot 140 m away through
a mountain is not. Outcome: per-weapon noise radius as data; sounds through
solid geometry are muffled (shorter, suspicion only, no location); enemies hear
their own side's gunfire and blasts as "combat nearby"; a suppressed weapon
(U-210) uses a reduced radius.

## Scope

- `weapons.json` `noiseM` (and later attachment modifiers); stimuli use it.
- Occlusion test for sound (cheap ray to the listener; budgeted).
- Enemy-made shots/blasts become stimuli for other enemies (Suspicious/Alerted
  per U-205), not squad-locating threats.
- Bots unaffected except that their own shots now carry their weapon's noise.
