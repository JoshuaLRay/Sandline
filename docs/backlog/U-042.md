# U-042 — Distinct models for the roster weapons, and the left-handed rifle

Epic: **Squad**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: split from [U-020](U-020.md) on 2026-09-29 (that card required splitting before READY: more than one substantial new weapon behavior, plus art, audio and wire changes).

## Problem / outcome

After U-040 the SMG, scoped carbine and both snipers borrow the M4/DMR model. Each needs its own silhouette, and the left-handed rifle must be built and held left-handed (mirrored hold, view model and reload pose), not just a flag.

## Scope

Extend the generators in `packages/tools/src/art/weapons` with the four models, regenerate the processed assets in `packages/client/public/assets` (edit sources, not outputs), map them in `weaponModels.ts`, handle the left-handed hold in the rig/view model, and keep asset budgets green. Visual quality is an owner verdict.

## Start here

`packages/tools/src/art/weapons`, `packages/client/src/weapons/weaponModels.ts`, `viewModel.ts`, `reloadPose.ts`, `docs/COMMANDS.md` (generation rows).

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Four distinct generated models (`weapon-smg`, `weapon-m4s`, `weapon-sniper`, `weapon-bolt`) on the shared atlas, within budget.
- [x] The left-handed sniper is held left-handed in first and third person: left hand on the pistol grip, right on the fore-end, the rifle mirrored to the left at the hip, and the support (right) hand takes the magazine on reload. **Not done:** a bolt-cycle pose (nothing animates the bolt yet; the bolt handle is modelled on the left side).
- [x] Generated assets regenerated and committed (`pnpm gen:art`, `pnpm gen:assets`). **No screenshot capture was produced**; owner acceptance of the in-game model/handedness visual review is recorded below (2026-10-09).

## Verification

Weapon asset and rig tests, the art generation command, `pnpm verify`. Do not invent a visual verdict.

## Handoff / evidence

Branch `task/U-042-roster-models`.
- `packages/tools/src/art/weapons/weapons.ts`: `SMG` (short M4-family with vents and a compact hider, straight magazine part), `M4_SCOPED` (the M4 with an optic), `SNIPER_SEMI` (the DMR with a cheek riser and a longer, heavier barrel with a brake) and `BOLT_LEFT` (walnut stock, heavy barrel, fixed magazine box, scope, bolt and knob on the LEFT). Mapped in `weaponModels.ts` (`smg`, `carbine-scoped`→`m4s`, `sniper-semi`→`sniper`, `sniper-bolt-left`→`bolt`).
- Left-handed hold: `WeaponModelSpec.handed?: 'left'`. Third person (`humanoidSoldier.ts`): the strong-hand grip goes to the left arm and the support grip to the right, and the reload reach moves the support hand. First person (`viewModel.ts`): hands swap, the hip position mirrors, and the reload hand is the right. Right-handed guns are untouched.
- Tests: tools art tests cover each new model (hold, sight, muzzle, magazine part, atlas, winding, committed-as-generated); client tests cover the hold on both hands and the mirrored viewmodel; the data's `handedness` matches the model.
- `pnpm verify` and CI on the PR. The four models are procedural first passes in the existing style; whether they read as an SMG, scoped AR and snipers is the owner's call.
