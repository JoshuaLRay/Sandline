# U-042 — Distinct models for the roster weapons, and the left-handed rifle

Epic: **Squad**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: split from [U-020](U-020.md) on 2026-09-29 (that card required splitting before READY: more than one substantial new weapon behavior, plus art, audio and wire changes).

## Problem / outcome

After U-040 the SMG, scoped carbine and both snipers borrow the M4/DMR model. Each needs its own silhouette, and the left-handed rifle must be built and held left-handed (mirrored hold, view model and reload pose), not just a flag.

## Scope

Extend the generators in `packages/tools/src/art/weapons` with the four models, regenerate the processed assets in `packages/client/public/assets` (edit sources, not outputs), map them in `weaponModels.ts`, handle the left-handed hold in the rig/view model, and keep asset budgets green. Visual quality is an owner verdict.

## Start here

`packages/tools/src/art/weapons`, `packages/client/src/weapons/weaponModels.ts`, `viewModel.ts`, `reloadPose.ts`, `docs/COMMANDS.md` (generation rows).

- [ ] Generated assets regenerated and committed; capture for the owner's visual review, verdict pending.
