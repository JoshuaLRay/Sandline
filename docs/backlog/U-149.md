# U-149 — Construct continuous y8 road supports, shoulders and turning aprons

Epic: Map implementation. Status, dependencies and work live in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Parent: [U-139](U-139.md). Depends: U-138.
Canonical design: [Mission 1 construction specification](../design/maps/qalat-road.md) §§2, 5.1, 11.1.

## Scope

Extend the accepted isolated insertion with the exact A0/D0–A9 road and final
6 m run to the south gate. Build y8 supports with 12 m clear carriageway and
4 m shoulder per side; 36 m diameter turning aprons at A2/A5/A8 and every
changing tangent used by the A8→A1 tank return (A1–A8). Open only the insertion's
temporary north road cap. New slabs begin at y5.5, preserving all future
basement voids below their highest ceiling and the exact ridge bridge space.

This support whitebox deliberately has unfinished road edges. Road-facing
geology, fight segmentation, cover/landmarks and edge isolation are U-150;
X ingress and tank clearance are U-151; return protection is U-152. No campaign
activation, actor/AI tuning, caches, checkpoints, ridge or basement construction.
Support traversal is a mechanic fixture; it does not claim playable combat.
