# U-055 — Claymore for the left-handed sniper

Epic: **Controls**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [U-048](U-048.md), owner design decisions 2026-09-30.

## Problem / outcome

Marsh (slot 4, the left-handed sniper) carries **1 claymore** (owner, 2026-09-30).

## Scope


A **placed** mine built on U-054's placement code: placed like C4 (aim at a surface within 2.5 m, left click), facing where the sniper looks. It detonates **itself** when an enemy enters a **3 m cone** in front of it (proposal: 60 degrees wide, 3 m deep), and the owner's right click may also detonate it. Blast is directional: damage only inside the front cone (proposal: 150 damage, no cover reduction inside 3 m), suppression to the enemy as for any blast. Same friendly-fire rule as the frag (a mate stepping in front is hurt, the owner included). Expiry, pickup (E, own), retry and death rules are C4's. Not carried by bots.


## Start here

`packages/shared/src/data/projectiles.json`, `packages/server/src/session/Session.ts` (projectiles, pouch, detonation), the foundation from [U-048](U-048.md) and, for placed items, [U-054](U-054.md).

## Acceptance criteria


- [ ] Placed facing the sniper's aim; triggers on an enemy entering the cone, not behind it.
- [ ] Owner can detonate it by hand; E picks it up if unexploded.
- [ ] Damage only in the cone; friendly fire as the frag; tested headless.

## Verification

Server tests for each rule, a hosted-session test, `pnpm verify` and the CI jobs. Numbers are placeholders until playtested.
