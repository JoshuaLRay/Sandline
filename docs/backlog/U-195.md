# U-195 — Left-handed firearms across categories

Epic: Loot and progression ([U-183](U-183.md)). Pillar: P8.
Size: M (data, art generator, tests). Human verdict: batched in U-193 (look).
Status, priority, dependencies and branch/PR live in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: owner, 2026-10-16 — "we must not limit left-handed loot to sniper rifles. The future loot system should include compatible left-handed variants across multiple weapon categories, including rare: left-handed assault rifles, left-handed light machine guns, left-handed sniper rifles, other suitable left-handed firearms."

## Problem / outcome

Only two left-handed weapons exist (`sniper-bolt-left`, `sniper-semi-left`).
Outcome: left-handed variants of an assault rifle, an LMG and at least one more
category (SMG or shotgun), defined as items with original names (ADR-020),
present in the approved loot tables at the approved rarity, so Marsh can become
a rifleman or machine gunner.

## Scope

- Weapon/item data rows with `handedness: left`; viewmodel and third-person art
  from the generators (mirrored ejection and handling cues).
- Placement in loot tables per U-185 (default: Modified/Named tiers, signature
  sources in at least two missions).
- Not in scope: new right-handed weapons.

## Read first

- U-185 specification; `packages/shared/src/data/weapons.json` (left-handed rows); `packages/tools/src/art`; [ADR-020](../adr/020-setting.md) §4

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] At least three new left-handed firearms across AR, LMG and one more category, equipable only by left-handed characters (L-11 default).
- [ ] Each appears in a loot table with a known source.
- [ ] Bots use them as they use the right-handed versions.

## Verification

Data validation tests, handedness tests, art captures; CI.

## Handoff / evidence

Not started (blocked by U-185, U-187).
