## Problem / outcome

The owner asked for “Taliban-sounding shouts” when enemies engage. Implement combat barks for the game’s period Afghan enemy fighters, with language and delivery checked during U-013.

## Scope

Trigger the reviewed enemy voice bank on confirmed engagement events, with per-enemy/group cooldowns and positional falloff. Extend event replication only if needed.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Entering combat produces an appropriate enemy shout with variation, not a loop on every AI tick.
- [ ] Direction, distance and occlusion match the enemy; dead/despawned enemies cannot keep shouting.
- [ ] Barks neither use the friendly radio channel nor leak enemies outside the game’s replication/interest rules.
- [ ] Content follows ADR-020’s military tone and uses the reviewed lines from U-013.
