# U-044 — Long-term control scheme: slots 1–6, swap to use, right-click aim, left-click use

Epic: **Controls**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: 2026-09-29 owner direction, recorded as a long-term plan. Verbatim: "For all equipment, we will force a swap to use. Long term plan for weapon/device usage: 1 is primary weapon, 2 is secondary weapon, 3 is knife, 4 is grenades, 5 is equipment, 6 is Health Kits. Then right click to aim, and left click for using it."

## Problem / outcome

Owner's target scheme. Every weapon or device is drawn (swapped to) by its slot key before it is used, then **right click aims** and **left click uses** it:

| Key | Slot |
|---|---|
| 1 | Primary weapon |
| 2 | Secondary weapon |
| 3 | Knife |
| 4 | Grenades |
| 5 | Equipment |
| 6 | Health kits |

Nothing is used without first swapping to it. This is a plan, not a request for one release: UNKNOWN whether the owner wants it built in one step or migrated slot by slot.

## Observations against today's code (not a diagnosis)

- Keys 1–4 select loadout slots (`weaponKey.ts`, U-005: 1 primary, 2 pistol); `G` throws a grenade without swapping to it and `5` readies the grenade arc (`LocalInput.ts`, onboarding hint "G throws a grenade · 5 readies it").
- Frags and rockets live in the pouch, not a slot; a "health kit", and what "equipment" (slot 5) contains, do not exist in the data at all.
- Interaction with other cards: this takes over `G` from grenades, which resolves the U-029 conflict (G = drop gun) without moving grenades to `T`. U-022's two-primary support has no pistol; whether its second primary lives in slot 2 is a proposal, not decided.

## Scope (proposal, needs decomposition before READY)

Split into leaves once the owner confirms the approach, roughly: (a) the slot model and key routing, with swap-to-use for pouch items (grenades → slot 4, launchers under equipment); (b) right-click aim / left-click use across weapons, grenades (arc) and the knife; (c) health kits: a new item, heal rule and data; (d) equipment (slot 5) contents; (e) onboarding, help text and mobile controls. Keep it consistent with U-022, U-029 and ADR-001; server-authoritative; schema-validated data.

## Owner answers (2026-09-29)

1. **Equipment (slot 5) differs per character.** Known so far: the **support carries C4**; the **LMG gunner (Brennan, slot 1) carries a rocket launcher**. The other four characters' equipment is UNKNOWN.
2. **Health kits (slot 6):** usable on yourself or another character, **10 seconds** to apply. Brings a **downed** character to **half health**, and any other character to **full health**. **A downed character cannot use one on themselves; someone else must.** How many kits each character carries is UNKNOWN. Relationship to the existing 3-second revive (hold E, T-2.15) is UNKNOWN; proposal: the kit is a second, slower route that also heals, and does not replace hold-E revive.
3. **Yes:** the Support's second primary goes in slot 2 (it has no pistol).
4. **Mobile:** mobile is spectator-only, so the scheme is desktop controls only. (Recorded from the answer "Yes, as mobile is spectator only"; the owner should say if that reading is wrong.)
5. **Yes:** `G` becomes drop-gun (U-029) and grenades live only on slot 4. No grenade key change to `T` is needed.

## Owner decisions, second round (2026-09-29)

- **Health kits:** **3 per character**. The **Support gets a 20% discount on all interactions**, so a kit takes the Support **8 s** (10 × 0.8). "All interactions" is read as every timed interaction (revive, kits, and the like); see U-049.
- **Grenades and C4:** **hold left click to aim the throw, release to throw it.** **Right click interacts with the equipment:** it **detonates C4**, or **pulls the pin to "cook" a grenade**. This refines U-046: for grenades and equipment, right click is *interact*, not aim.
- **C4 placement:** **only the Support can throw C4; everyone else must place it.**
- **Equipment is droppable too** (like guns, U-029).
- Still UNKNOWN: how placing works for non-Support characters; how many charges may be out at once; what happens to a cooked grenade held past its fuse (proposal: it detonates in the hand); whether a non-cooked grenade starts its fuse on release as today (proposal: yes); what right click does for the rocket launcher (proposal: unchanged, aim); the other four characters' equipment.
