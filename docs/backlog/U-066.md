# U-066 — Tank entity: hull, turret, hitbox, armour and health

Epic: **Mission / vehicle combat**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [U-035](U-035.md), owner decisions 2026-09-30 (all the proposed defaults).

## Problem / outcome

The game has no armoured enemy. A tank is **an enemy entity on rails**, not a rigid-body vehicle (owner, 2026-09-30): it reuses enemies, replication, damage, nav and sessions. This card is the thing itself; it does not yet move or shoot.

## Scope

- A `tank` row in the enemy data (schema-validated): health **1000**, a chunky multi-part hitbox (hull, turret), a hull facing separate from a turret facing, and a radius large enough that it blocks movement and is targetable at range.
- Armour as data: a damage multiplier by damage class — bullets and frags about **1/10**, rockets (170) and C4 (250) and claymores full, so a tank takes about 6 rocket hits or 4 C4.
- Replication: the tank's position, hull yaw, turret yaw and health ride the existing enemy snapshot; bump `PROTOCOL_VERSION` only for fields that have to be added (turret yaw).
- It is not a squad-side seat: nobody drives it.
- Destruction: a dead tank stays as a wreck that blocks the road but does not block exfil (decided in U-069).
- Unit tests for the armour table, the hitbox and replication; the AI budget ceiling (`sim-run --scenario mission`) is re-measured.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A `tank` spawns through the existing enemy path with the data above; clients agree on its place, yaws and health.
- [x] Rockets, C4 and claymores hurt it as specced; bullets and frags barely do.
- [x] Its hitbox is hit from the front, side and turret, and shots register on the right part.
- [x] A dead tank is a wreck; U-031 and the other enemy rules are unaffected.
- [x] Tests cover each of the above; `pnpm verify` and the mission sim pass.

## Handoff / evidence

Implemented on `task/U-066-tank-entity`; awaiting review. No owner playtest verdict is claimed.

**What changed.** `tank` is a sixth `ENEMY_IDS` entry with its own `vehicle` block in `enemies.json` (schema-validated; required on the tank, refused on every other archetype): health 1000, a hull capsule (2.6 m wide, 5.8 m long) and a turret capsule above it in the tank's frame, a blast radius of 2 m, and its armour: a bullet gets through at 0.1, a blast by projectile (rocket, C4 and claymore at 1; frag at 0.1; anything else, such as a concussion, 0.1 of a blast that already does nothing). The body is shot at in its own shape: `Hitbox.body` replaces the soldier's capsules (`vehicleHitbox`), `HitboxHistory.setShape` gives it to one entity, and a hit on it reports `part` ('hull' or 'turret'). Bullets are cut by the armour in `traceShot`; a blast is measured to the hull's skin (not its middle) and cut by the armour in `detonate`. Measured: a rocket does 170, C4 250, a claymore 150, a frag 13, so six rockets or four C4 kill it. It is an ordinary enemy entity that never goes down and lies as a wreck for 600 s (the Health component's corpse timer saturates at 63 s on the wire). Its turret yaw is `turretYaw` on the entity and a third field of the `Enemy` component (a tank's facing apart from its hull's `Transform`; 0 for a soldier), so no entity pays another bit of the presence mask and the per-entity snapshot budget holds; `PROTOCOL_VERSION` 59. It runs the `idle` tree for now (no brain until U-067/U-068) and is drawn as a soldier until U-070.

**Not done here, by design.** Soldiers do not collide with the tank yet (movement and blocking are U-067's); a wreck blocks nothing (U-069 decides that); no movement, no weapons (U-067, U-068); no mesh (U-070).
