# U-196 — Bots keep their orders through switches, leaves and cache runs

Epic: Squad command reliability ([U-203](U-203.md)). Pillar: P2, P3.
Size: S–M. Human verdict: none (engineering criteria).
Status, priority, dependencies and branch/PR live in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: owner, 2026-10-16 — review command gaps including "Maintaining orders when switching between characters" and "Correctly handling command ownership in multiplayer"; parity CP-14 (*unverified*), gap G-14.

## Problem / outcome

Audit 2026-10-16: no test covers what happens to orders when a human switches
character (U-026) or leaves. Observed in code: the bot a player switches into
loses its order (correct: a human now drives it); the soldier the player leaves
reverts to formation-follow under them; bots that were ordered by a player who
leaves keep their orders but still name the departed slot as the order's
source; a commander supply request ends a bot's current order and does not
restore it. In the series the soldier you leave keeps his last order.

Outcome (Conflict default, adapted): orders are durable — a bot keeps its
current order until a commander changes it, whoever is seated; order source
and commander update cleanly when people come and go; a bot sent to a cache
resumes its previous order afterwards.

## Scope

- When a commander leaves, their bots keep their orders, which are re-attributed
  to the new commander (U-025's hand-over), and the HUD/order markers update.
- The soldier a player switches out of: **Conflict default** — it keeps the
  squad's standing posture: if the player's fireteam or whole squad is on a
  Hold or Move order, it joins that order; otherwise it follows its new
  commander (today's behaviour). Proposal, reversible.
- A spectator seat's stale order is cleared on switch.
- Commander supply use (U-146/U-147) restores the bot's previous order when the
  use completes or is cancelled.
- No wire change expected; if one is needed, bump the protocol.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A bot holding/moving under an order keeps it when its commander switches to another soldier, leaves, or reconnects within grace; the order's source is the current commander.
- [ ] The soldier left by a switch joins a standing Hold/Move of its fireteam or squad, else follows.
- [ ] A bot sent to a cache resumes its prior order after collecting or cancelling.
- [ ] Headless multiplayer tests for each case, including two humans and a spectator commander.
