# U-079 — Bots fight armour: help destroy a tank

Epic: **Enemy AI**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: owner decisions on the campaign design, 2026-10-01 ([design doc](../design/CAMPAIGN.md)).

## Problem / outcome

The friendly bots cannot beat the tank (mission sim: 0 of 6 runs complete since U-069, 1 of 6 before; the 10-seed completion floor fails). Campaign mission 1 requires the squad to destroy a tank on the way out.

## Scope

Proposal: bots prioritise the tank when it is a threat; those carrying rockets, C4 or claymores use them against it (the only things that hurt it, U-066), the rest keep cover and suppress infantry; every bot takes cover from the tell (U-068) instead of standing in the lock; the tank counts as a target for the 'attack' order. Restore the mission sim's completion floor (U-069 recorded that it fails without this).

## Start here

`docs/backlog/U-069.md` (the numbers), `server/src/ai` (targeting, `useProjectile` leaves), `data/trees`, `tools/src/scenarios/mission.ts`, `tankWeapons.test.ts`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `sim-run --scenario mission --seeds 10` meets its completion floors with the tank.
- [ ] A bot with a launcher or C4 engages the tank and a bot caught in the tell moves.
- [ ] Deterministic session tests for both; the squad scenario still passes with zero friendly hits.
