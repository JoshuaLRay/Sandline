# U-079 — Bots fight armour: help destroy a tank

Epic: **Enemy AI**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: owner decisions on the campaign design, 2026-10-01 ([design doc](../design/CAMPAIGN.md)).

## Problem / outcome

The friendly bots cannot beat the tank (mission sim: 0 of 6 runs complete since U-069, 1 of 6 before; the 10-seed completion floor fails). Campaign mission 1 requires the squad to destroy a tank on the way out.

## Scope

Proposal: bots prioritise the tank when it is a threat; those carrying rockets, C4 or claymores use them against it (the only things that hurt it, U-066), the rest keep cover and suppress infantry; every bot takes cover from the tell (U-068) instead of standing in the lock; the tank counts as a target for the 'attack' order. Restore the mission sim's completion floor (U-069 recorded that it fails without this).

## Start here

`docs/backlog/U-069.md` (the numbers), `server/src/ai` (targeting, `useProjectile` leaves), `data/trees`, `tools/src/scenarios/mission.ts`, `tankWeapons.test.ts`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] `sim-run --scenario mission --seeds 10` meets its completion floors with the tank: 1-human 30% (floor 20%), 6-human 10% (floor 10%; it was 0% before the tank too).
- [x] A bot with a launcher or C4 engages the tank and a bot caught in the tell moves.
- [x] Deterministic session tests for both; the squad scenario still passes with zero friendly hits.

## Handoff / evidence

**What changed.** Three things in the friendly tree (`data/trees/friendly.json`), the leaves in `server/src/ai/actions/armour.ts` and the searches in `server/src/ai/armour.ts` (tuning: `armour.json`, validated):

- `shellLocked` / `dodgeShell`, first in the tree (before orders): a bot in the way of a tank's locked shell runs to the nearest walkable place out of it and stays there until the shell has landed. The danger is the shell's *flight line* (the shell goes at the locked point and on, bursting on the first body or wall), the blast at the lock, and the blast where it ends. A lock that no bot is in the way of changes nothing.
- `armourFight` / `fightArmour`, before the general fight: a bot carrying a rocket fires it at a tank in sight between 12 and 55 m (aim led by the tank's pace, pitch searched through `projectileArc`, not down a lane with a squadmate in it, not with one by the hit); with C4 or a claymore it throws it at a tank that has stopped, C4 set off (`Brain` key `detonate`, the human's detonator) once it lies by the hull and the bot and squadmates are clear. Under a *hold* order (the mission sim holds the squad at the terminal) it does the same from its post and walks nowhere. A move, attack, regroup or revive order still comes first.
- The tank was already a legal 'attack' target (the session accepts any living enemy); a test now pins that.

Session gains `CombatWorld.armour()` / `placed()`; `Brain` gains `detonate` and `dodge`; `aiHands` takes the `detonate` word.

**Found on the way (observations, then what was done).**

- The mission sim's failure label `defended area overrun` was printed for *every* failure that was not a squad wipe. Mission-01 has no defend objective; the real reasons were `soldier-dead` / `all-downed`. The scenario now prints the host's reason.
- Of 10 seeds at the 1-human budget, 5 die at 29–58 s in the garrison fight, before any upload: with and without the tank script the traces are identical. That is not the tank and is not changed here: card U-084.
- The other 5 reach the upload and meet the tank. The squad holds at the terminal (hold orders), clumped in a ~6 x 4 m space, many bots already hurt (7–40 HP), and any one death fails the mission. Before this card they did nothing against it: 0 of 5 survived.
- Two things killed the squad that were not the first shell: (1) the tank's shell flies on past the lock and bursts at the first body or wall (the compound's east wall, 25 m on) — the blast is there, not at the locked point; (2) a bot that has left the way of the shell is walked back into it by its hold order before the shell lands. Both are in the dodge now.
- A rocket goes off on the first body it meets, so a rocket fired down a line with a squadmate in it killed the squad (the first version did exactly this).

**Commands and results (this branch).**

- `sim-run --scenario mission --seeds 10`: 1-human 3/10 complete (seeds 2, 5, 6; mean 314 s), 6-human 1/10; all thresholds OK. Before: 0/10 and 0/10. `--seeds 3`: OK; `--all-missions --seeds 3`: OK.
- `sim-run --scenario squad`: zero friendly hits; `cover-duel`, `pinned`, `mg`: OK.
- `pnpm verify`: see the PR.

**Not claimed / owner's.** How it *feels* — bots dodging, rockets from the terminal, the squad's chance against the tank — is for an owner playtest; nothing here is a verdict. The completion rate is a floor on a sim whose early fight is itself failing half the time (U-084), so it will move when that is fixed. Seeds 8 and 10 still lose the tank fight (a bot too hurt to survive any near miss; bots that never see the tank from where they hold); more would be a tuning card, not this one.
