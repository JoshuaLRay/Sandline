# U-092 — Mission 1 level: the three-lane valley and the compound

Epic: **Campaign design**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [mission 1's approved brief](../design/MISSION-01.md) (owner, 2026-10-02), split out of [U-065](U-065.md).

## Problem / outcome

Mission 1 is played on a new level: a valley with three lanes (the dry riverbed in the west, the road in the centre, the terraces in the east) running from the squad's start in the south to a walled compound in the north, and the same valley back. This card is the map alone; who is on it and what happens is U-093 and U-094.

## Scope

Per the approved brief (section 3): a **first cut at about 4× the slice's play area** (the brief's default; three lanes of about 40 m), with the lanes' characters as written (low cover and boulders in the riverbed; open hard-packed road under the compound wall's MG line; stepped terraces with sight lines onto the compound's east gate) and the three sight lines worth building around (the MG nest down the road, the east gate from the upper terrace, the road bend where the tank first appears). The squad starts at the south edge and extracts at the same place (a loop). Use the committed kit pieces (`kit.json`, `?kit`); new kit pieces only if the level cannot be built without them. Register it (`docs/COMMANDS.md`, "Adding a mission": level file, a world registered in `campaignRegistry.ts` with its mission/encounter placeholders from U-093/U-094, `pnpm gen:nav`). Growing the map toward the full ~10× is a later step: U-081 (the cover search) and U-082 (rectangular floors) are done, so nothing blocks it.
