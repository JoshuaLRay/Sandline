# U-094 — Mission 1 mission file and script: staged objectives, rescue and extraction

Epic: **Campaign design**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [mission 1's approved brief](../design/MISSION-01.md) (owner, 2026-10-02), split out of [U-065](U-065.md).

## Problem / outcome

The mission itself: its objectives in the brief's stages, the failure rules and the checkpoints, and its place in the campaign.

## Scope

Per the approved brief (section 2): stage 1 *Approach* (reach the compound by any lane); stage 2 *The compound* (free the prisoner: the `rescue` objective; the radio operator is optional, in any order); stage 3 *The way home* (destroy the tank; reach the extraction point with the prisoner and everyone standing: `reach` with `who: all` and `escort: true`). Failure is the first death of a squad character or the prisoner; checkpoints on entering the compound, on freeing him and on the tank's destruction, restoring him on a retry. Add the mission file and any script (`missions/`, `scripts/`), register it with its `lobby` label if offered, and add its entry to `campaign.json` with its title, briefing and debrief lines drawn from the brief's premise. The slice mission-01 stays as the test/QA mission and is not in the season.
