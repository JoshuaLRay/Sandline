# U-201 — Friendly bots lay suppressive fire on an attack order

Epic: Squad command reliability ([U-203](U-203.md)). Pillar: P2, P4.
Size: M. Human verdict: none (engineering).
Status, priority, dependencies and branch/PR live in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: owner, 2026-10-16 — review "Suppression and coordinated movement"; parity CP-12.

## Problem / outcome

Enemies suppress and flank (T-3.20–T-3.23); friendly bots only fire at targets
they can see, so they cannot pin an enemy behind cover for a teammate to move.
The `suppress` behaviour exists for enemies only. Outcome: an attack order on
an enemy the bot cannot hit (or a mark on a position) makes the bot fire
bursts at its last known position to suppress it, within fire discipline and
without hitting squadmates.

## Scope

- Reuse the enemy `suppress` leaf for friendly bots under an attack order or a
  mark; ammunition cost (with U-199 if built).
- Bounding (one fireteam moving while the other covers) and focus fire stay in
  U-087, which the owner deferred.
