# U-095 — Mission 1 verification: the headless run and its completion baseline

Epic: **Campaign design**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [mission 1's approved brief](../design/MISSION-01.md) (owner, 2026-10-02), split out of [U-065](U-065.md).

## Problem / outcome

Prove mission 1 loads, plays and completes headless, and record how often, before anyone tunes it. Only a human playtest can say whether it is good.

## Scope

Per the brief (section 7): `pnpm sim-run --scenario mission` covers the new mission at both budgets (the CI step already runs every registered mission for three seeds, and twenty for the rates); the **completion rate is measured and recorded**, as for the slice, before any floor is set in `mission.json` (no floor is guessed). Scenario tests: the rescue frees the prisoner; he follows, holds and goes on an all-squad order; the tank appears after he is freed and can be destroyed; the extraction needs all seven alive (prisoner dead is a failure, and the retry restores him). If the bots cannot finish it at all, report why rather than weakening a gate. The owner's playtest of length, lanes, difficulty and feel is listed as owed; no verdict is invented.

## Handoff / evidence

Measured 20 seeds at each budget with the committed friendly tree, real scripted orders, health and damage unchanged. **Budget 1: 0/20 (0%). Budget 6: 0/20 (0%).** At each budget, 13 runs fail during Approach and seven reach rescue; 18 die on the first squad death, two fail all-downed. None frees the POW. With fixed authored counts, the two budgets produce identical results. The current bots lose the approach/compound firefights before rescue; this baseline cannot judge the tank encounter's difficulty. Full run details: [U-095-qalat-baseline.txt](../verification/U-095-qalat-baseline.txt).

The scripted leader now follows the approach lanes and clears nearby hostiles before holding interact beside the captive. Existing slice completion floors and combat/AI gates remain; no Qalat floor is guessed. CI retains three seeds for all registered missions plus the slice's 20-seed gate, and adds an explicit 20-seed Qalat diagnostic; malformed data, runner errors and encounter stalls still fail.

Session scenario tests prove rescue through actual bot interact, follow/hold/move on all-squad orders, hold persistence on retry, real rocket blasts destroying the spawned tank, a retry after its destruction, POW death failure/restore, and extraction requiring all six soldiers standing plus the POW. The downed-sixth-soldier case caught and fixed an extraction rule gap; generic reach semantics remain unchanged. Real map capture also exposed the renderer’s fixed 200 m ground plane: it now follows the world’s rectangular authored floor bounds, so the northern valley is visibly grounded.
