Epic: Enemy AI (Conflict parity). Pillar: P4 / P7.
Size: M (split before claiming if the AI and presentation exceed one PR).
Human verdict: batched in U-119 (Mission 1 review) once placed in a campaign mission.
Status, priority, dependencies and branch/PR live in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: owner, 2026-10-09 — first Conflict parity gaps (G-2, row CP-41 in
[the parity tracker](../design/CONFLICT-PARITY.md)).

## Problem / outcome

RPG-7 troops are a staple enemy of the series and of the period (ADR-020 lists
the RPG-7 for the enemy). Sandline's enemies are riflemen and MG gunners only;
nothing punishes a squad bunched behind cover or forces it to move. An RPG
gunner fires a slow, visible rocket at groups and positions in cover.

## Scope

Conflict default (ADR-021), using the infrastructure already reserved:

- An `rpg` row in `enemies.json` (the schema already accepts its `launcher`
  block, ADR-015 reserved the ID) firing the existing `rocket` projectile, with
  the enemy rifle (`carbine`, drawn as an AK) for close range and its own
  perception and accuracy values.
- Behaviour: prefers targets in cover or two or more squad members within the
  blast radius, at medium range; a visible wind-up (tell) before firing; long
  reload; relocates after firing; never fires when a friendly is within the
  blast; falls back to its rifle when close.
- Presentation: the fighter model carrying the existing generated `rpg7`, a
  launch sound and trail (reuse the squad rocket's recipes where they exist,
  add a recipe where they don't), and the rocket's existing explosion.
- Bots react like any explosive threat (suppression, cover); no new bot logic.
- Available for encounters and on the QA range (for example `?enemies`
  variants); placement in campaign missions belongs to their integration cards
  (Mission 1: U-117).

Out of scope: enemy snipers and officers; anti-vehicle targeting of squad
vehicles (there are none yet).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `rpg` validates, spawns from an encounter and replicates to clients.
- [ ] It fires rockets only at medium range, at targets in cover or groups,
      after a visible tell, never when a friendly is inside the blast radius.
- [ ] It reloads slowly, relocates after firing and uses its rifle up close.
- [ ] Clients draw the fighter with an RPG-7, the launch, trail and explosion.
- [ ] Squad bots survive and fight it in a headless scenario (a new seeded
      scenario or extension with asserted floors).
- [ ] Existing mission scenario floors are unchanged (no RPG placed in them).

## Verification

Unit and AI tests; a seeded `sim-run` scenario with logged floors;
`pnpm verify`; regenerate assets/audio if inputs change; all four CI jobs. Look
and feel are reviewed with Mission 1's batched verdict (U-119).
