# U-059 — Checkpoints restore the whole world: living enemies, positions, health

Epic: **Campaign**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: owner direction, 2026-09-30, while deciding [U-052](U-052.md): "The game should remember the state of picked up loot, enemy deaths, enemy locations and everything else upon saving for a checkpoint. It should be just like the first time the campaign made it to that save point."

## Problem / outcome

A retry should put the campaign back exactly as it was when the checkpoint was saved. Today (after U-052) a retry restores: the objective and clock, which encounter groups were beaten, the event script's state, what each soldier carries (guns, rounds, pouch, kits, equipment) and the pickups on the ground. It does **not** restore enemies still alive at the checkpoint (their places, health, posture, orders), each soldier's health or downed state, placed equipment (C4, claymores, sensors, smoke), emplacement state, or any of this in the saved campaign file (the restore is in-session only).

## Scope

Decompose before READY. The owner's rule is the acceptance test: a retry is indistinguishable from the first arrival at the save point. Questions to settle in the decomposition: which enemy state is authoritative (position, health, posture, brain blackboard, queued wave members); soldier health and downed state at a checkpoint (the owner's words say restore, but a retry after a wipe that restores everyone downed would soft-lock, so a floor such as revive-to-half is a proposal, not a decision); whether placed equipment and projectiles in flight are saved; and persisting the snapshot in `campaignSave` so a reload restores it too (needs a format version and a size bound).

## Start here

- `packages/server/src/session/Session.ts`: `captureMissionCheckpoint`, `checkpointSlot`, `restoreCheckpointWorld`, `retryMission`, `resetMissionWorld`, `persistCampaign`.
- `packages/server/src/ai` and `packages/server/src/session/mission.ts` for the spawner, groups and brains.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Enemies alive at a checkpoint are alive again after a retry, with the same positions, health and behaviour; enemies dead at the checkpoint stay dead.
- [ ] Each soldier's health and downed state at the checkpoint are restored, with the soft-lock case decided.
- [x] Placed equipment and other world state the owner names are restored, or explicitly excluded with a reason.
- [ ] The snapshot survives a reload through the campaign save, versioned and bounded. **Moved to [U-060](U-060.md):** this task restores a retry within a running session.
- [x] A test saves at a checkpoint, plays on, fails, retries, and finds the world equal to the saved one.

## Handoff / evidence

Implemented on `task/U-059-checkpoint-world`; awaiting review. No owner playtest verdict is claimed.

**What changed.** A checkpoint now saves the mission clock, every living enemy (archetype, place, facing, health, rounds, pouch, posture, and the group that sent it), the spawner's whole progress (each group's waves, times and members, and the members still queued), each soldier's health, and the devices placed (C4, claymores, sensors, smoke). A retry puts them back: enemies return where they stood and as hurt, in their groups; the spawner does not send again what it had sent, and still sends what was queued; the mission clock continues from the checkpoint, so the times the spawner and the script kept still mean something. Enemies come back with fresh minds (what each knew of the squad is not kept), and an enemy that was on a gun comes back on foot. Per the owner's decision, an objective completed while a soldier is downed queues its checkpoint until the squad is up (`requestCheckpoint`/`takeQueuedCheckpoint`); until then a retry goes back to the last checkpoint that was saved (`MissionRun.setCheckpoint`). The retry semantics changed in three old tests: a beaten group's `spawnedBy` now still lists its dead, and an enemy alive at a checkpoint returns instead of being sent afresh.

**Not done.** Saving all of this in the campaign file so a reload restores it: [U-060](U-060.md).

**Evidence.** `packages/server/src/session/checkpointWorld.test.ts` (enemies back where they stood and as hurt, once, with the sent groups still sent; the clock continues; health and a placed C4 come back; a downed soldier queues the checkpoint, a failure meanwhile goes to the last saved one, and standing up takes it), plus the updated `mission.test.ts` and `encounterPressure.test.ts`. `pnpm verify` and the CI jobs: see the PR.
