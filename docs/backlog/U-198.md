# U-198 — Order a soldier to plant charges and to set them off

Epic: Squad command reliability ([U-203](U-203.md)). Pillar: P2, P4.
Size: M. Human verdict: none (engineering).
Status, priority, dependencies and branch/PR live in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: owner, 2026-10-16 — "Planting and detonating explosives" and "Ensure bots using C4 can eventually select the appropriate detonation behavior through commands"; parity CP-11 (Global Storm: order C4 planted at a point), gap G-4. Depends on [U-182](U-182.md) (two detonators).

## Problem / outcome

Bots use C4 only on their own against a stopped tank (U-079) and set off
everything at once. No order makes a bot plant a charge at a chosen point, and
no order makes it set charges off. Outcome: a player orders a soldier carrying
charges to plant one at a point, and later to set off its oldest charge or all
of them — the same two detonators a human has (ADR-026).

## Scope

- Planting: a contextual move order onto a point with "plant" intent; Mission
  2's charge points use the same path (kestrel-dam.md §9.5, N8).
- Detonating: an order that chooses oldest or all, honouring the squad's
  safety margin (the bot waits until squadmates are clear, as U-079 does) and
  fire discipline.
- Bots never plant on their own initiative outside the armour attack.

## Decision needed

**How should a player give "plant" and "set off" orders?** The order wheel's
eight sectors are all used.
A — **an equipment ring**: holding the wheel key a second time (or a modifier)
opens a second ring with Plant, Set off oldest, Set off all, and later Give
ammo and Pick up (one place for every equipment order, works the same on
mobile as a second page);
B — **contextual only**: a move order onto a door, vehicle or charge point
plants there, and an attack order aimed at your own placed charge sets it off
(no new UI, but "set off all" and ordinary ground spots are hard to express);
C — keep bots' C4 automatic (armour and mission charge points only).
**Recommended default: A**, because it gives every bot interaction the owner
listed (plant, detonate, give, pick up) one consistent home without
overloading move and attack. Reply "agree" or pick another.
