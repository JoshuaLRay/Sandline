## Problem / outcome

Sandline should make players excited to keep playing, discovering gear and
building their own squad: weapons, attachments, equipment and rewards found in
missions and earned by completing and replaying them, any soldier able to use
any equipment (Marsh: left-handed only), without making grounded tactics
irrelevant. The mechanics are decided in an owner design session first.
