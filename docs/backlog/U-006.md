# U-006 — Keep the gun visible during a readable reload animation

Epic: **Weapon feel**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [owner feedback, 2026-09-26](2026-09-26-feedback.md), plus any legacy bug named below.

## Problem / outcome

Reloading currently appears to make the gun disappear. The owner wants an actual animation.

## Scope

Animate the AR reload with the existing procedural approach: lower/tilt, magazine handling and return to ready. Match the weapon reload clock. Do not change reload balance as part of the presentation fix.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Weapon swap, down/death, interruption and checkpoint retry clear/reset presentation correctly.

## Handoff / evidence

### What was wrong (reproduced on `main`, f51fe25)

- **The reload was only a dip:** the first-person reload was `plateau(progress, 0, 0.25, 0.75, 1)`, which dropped the hold 6 cm, pitched the muzzle down 0.5 rad and rolled it 0.4.
- **So the gun left the screen:** from the hip the whole rifle was below the frame for most of the reload. Captured at 0, 0.5, 1.0 and 1.4 s after R, with no gun; it reappeared at about 1.9 s. At ADS it dropped out of view the same way. An empty-magazine reload did the same. The third-person reload layer (T-2.26) was fine.

### Design as built

- **The magazine is a part** (generator, `tools/src/art/weapons`):
  - `WeaponBuilder.part(name, draw)` records a run of geometry as a named part.
  - The glb writer puts each part in its own child node under the weapon's, sharing its vertices and material.
  - The AR lower's magazine is a part, so it is on the M4 and the DMR. Their triangle counts are unchanged (752, 680). Only those two assets are regenerated (`pnpm gen:art`, `pnpm gen:assets`), within budget.
  - The processed file keeps the node, and the pipeline's quantization gives it a rest transform. So the page moves it *from* its rest, never overwriting it (`weaponMagazine`, `weaponModels.ts`).
  - The code-built carbine and marksman fallbacks name their magazine box the same way.
- **The pose** (`weapons/reloadPose.ts`) is a pure function of reload progress on the weapon's own clock, with its beats on the reload sound stages (`stages.in` 0.55, `stages.bolt` 0.85). It is zero at both ends and continuous between:
  - **From the start:** the gun comes up and out to arm's length, canted with its top to the right so the well faces the left hand. The hand leaves the foregrip for the magazine.
  - **By 0.34:** the hand pulls the magazine down along the well's axis and out of view.
  - **At `in`:** it brings a fresh one up and seats it exactly on the "in" sound, with a small jolt.
  - **At `bolt`:** the gun rocks back.
  - **By 1:** back to the ready hold.
- **The viewmodel** (`viewModel.ts`):
  - applies the pose to the hold and moves the magazine and the left hand (the forearm is re-solved each frame);
  - a reload takes the gun off the sight, which comes back after;
  - a reload cut short (a swap, a checkpoint retry resetting the weapon) eases out in about a quarter second instead of popping;
  - hidden (down, dead, a vault, third person), it drops any pose;
  - a swap puts the old gun's magazine back in its well.
  - The weapons without a magazine part (shotgun, pistol, launcher) reload with the hands alone, and are no longer hidden either.
- **Unchanged:** reload seconds, magazine and ammo, the third-person and remote reload layers, and the stage sounds.

### Ammo authority (the fourth criterion)

- **Not this change's to affect:** the animation reads the reload clock and never touches ammo.
- **But it is not met:** checking it found that a manual reload (R) never reaches the host. There is no reload message, and the server reloads a slot only on a Fire at an empty magazine. So a manual reload's refill is the page's alone.
- **Recorded as [U-028](U-028.md)** rather than widening this presentation PR, because it needs a protocol change. The criterion stays unchecked here until U-028 lands.
