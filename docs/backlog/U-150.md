## Scope and acceptance

Extend U-149's isolated road supports with all A-C01–12 at approved coordinates,
sizes and along/across-road orientations; toll office, four orchard trunks and
closed loading façades; T-C spurs and adjoining T-W/T-E road-facing landforms.
Reserve future ridge view fans/shelves and basement voids. Prove three distinct
fights (A2, A4/A5, A8), no ordinary hostile firing lane >90 m, 4–8 m free regroup
spaces, ≥4 m infantry passages and central 12 m vehicle corridor. Clip turning
apron shoulders around fixed cover; never move the cover into the carriageway.

Prove real nav/controller travel both directions, nontraversable road boundaries
and no unlisted surface connection. Recheck accepted insertion occlusion and
court reveal on the extended scene. Preserve approved coordinates and full tank
return clearance provision; final full rotating sweep belongs U-151. No X
construction, campaign activation, AI tuning or supplies/checkpoints.

Include reproducible exact-geometry captures, affected generation, asset/pack
budgets, `pnpm verify` and all four latest-head CI jobs. Human road map/play
acceptance remains required. Split further before claiming if oversized.

## Owner decision — 2026-10-10

Scoping found the accepted road too straight for three separate fights: from the
A4 bend a standing soldier saw straight down the tank's own 12 m lane to the D0
court (`(-9,197)`→`(37,66)`, **138.8 m**), and nothing may stand in that lane.
Options were A bend the road at the fight boundaries (default), B keep the road
and put rock on its edges, C keep it as built. The owner answered **"agree"**:
option A. [U-159](U-159.md) bends the road ahead of this card, keeping A2, A5,
A6, A8, the ridge crossings, the buried vault and the gate where they are, and
regenerates U-149's supports. This card builds the fights on the bent road.

### Inputs from U-159's bent road

The fights are built on the bent road (A3 `(-37,142)`, A4 `(-28,210)`, A7
`(-18,302)`). Its supports already prove the 12 m lane holds no straight view
over 90 m (86.74 m) and that no straight view on the walking surface joins two
fights. This card must keep that true when it adds terrain, and:

- Fill the inside corners of the separating bends (A3 and A7) with rock above
  head height; neither may be a drop. Place the T-C spurs there.
- Re-place the items the bend displaced, updating the §5.2/§9.1 tables (§15):
  A-C04, A-C05, A-C07, A-C08, A-C12 (off the walking surface; A-C12 may belong to
  U-151's forecourt) and A-C10 (now in the tank lane); the orchard trees and depot
  façades; frontage riflemen AF2 and AF3 (off the walking surface).
- Cut the bare-surface views longer than 90 m from the frontage fight with cover
  or rock on shoulders and aprons: from A4 116.2 m toward A6, from A5 128.9 m down
  the west leg toward A3 (both in `artifacts/qalat-road-supports/construction.json`).
  Each leaves the tank lane somewhere, so each can be cut.
