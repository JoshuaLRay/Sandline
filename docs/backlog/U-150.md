## Decision needed

**Owner, 2026-10-10:** "agree" — U-149 is accepted as the road foundation now;
the road's look and play are judged once its fights exist (U-139). U-149 is DONE.

Scoping then found that the accepted road is too straight for this card. From the
A4 bend a standing soldier sees straight down the tank's own lane to the D0 court:
the line `(-9,197)`→`(37,66)` is **138.8 m** long and stays at least 0.36 m inside
the 12 m lane all the way, past every approved cover piece. Nothing may stand in
that lane, so no rock or cover can block it. With the full 20 m road and its turning
areas, open straight views reach about 165 m. "Three separate fights" and "no
enemy view longer than 90 m" therefore cannot both be built on this road. (Enemies
see 80–90 m, and the A2 and A4 fights are only 74 m apart.)

Options:

- **A: bend the road at the fight boundaries.** Move or add a few road points so
  each fight sits round a real corner, as the insertion's added bend (U-138)
  did. Keep the fight spots, ridge crossings, buried vault and gate where they are.
  Players get three distinct fights, each seen only on arrival. Cost: one
  design-and-rebuild task before this card (the road is generated).
- **B: keep the road and break up what can be broken.** Put rock on the road
  edges, never in the tank lane. The straight views down the middle stay (up to
  about 140 m). Enemies won't shoot that far, but you will often see the next fight
  from inside this one, and the A2 and A4 fights will tend to merge.
- **C: keep the road exactly as built.** One long, open firefight up the road.
  Fastest, and the least like the plan.

**Recommended default: A.** The separate fights are the point of the road route
(§5.1), and A is the same fix chosen for the insertion. The road is generated
from its points, and its look has not been reviewed yet. Reply "agree" or pick
another.

Under any option, four approved cover pieces do not fit the road as built.
A-C07 `(8,212)` sits 0.5 m from the tank lane's centreline. A-C05 `(10,184)` and
A-C12 `(44,344)` lie outside the built road; A-C12 may belong to U-151's
forecourt. A-C08 `(12,244)` lies 0.4 m outside its turning area. Specification
§15 lets the construction task move cover if it updates the table, so this needs
no decision.
