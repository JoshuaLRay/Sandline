# U-085 — Mission-01 at six humans: the squad is lost before the upload

Epic: **Enemy AI**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: found while doing U-084, 2026-10-01.

## Problem / outcome

`sim-run --scenario mission` (20 seeds) at the 6-human budget completes 0% against a floor of 10%, and has not met it since the mission started failing on the first death (`dadd365`). After U-084, 8 of 20 seeds end before 60 s and 11 between 60 and 200 s. Traced causes (seed 1): the squad sprints between stops across the east lane and a group of riflemen spawned there downs four bots in four seconds; (seed 12) after the garrison falls an extra wave appears at the compound's entrance and downs five of six within two seconds.

Outcome wanted: the 6-human completion floor met by the game and the scenario, not by loosening the floor.

## Handoff / evidence

Investigation done; the fix is not. The 6-human floor is **not met** and was not weakened. U-086 tried the cover-fighting lever on a 1v1 fixture and it made things worse (see that card), so what is left is the owner's choice below.

**Baseline (20 seeds, 6-human, after U-084, via a one-budget runner):** 0 complete; 8 end before 60 s, 11 between 60 and 200 s, 1 later.

**Observations.**

- The 6-human budget is the director at size 1.0: twice the enemies of the 1-human budget (size 0.5), against the same six bots.
- Early (traced seed 1, 6-human): the squad's `attack` orders send bots at the east-lane group (`assault-hold`, 4 riflemen). Three bots cross open ground at a sprint to a firing position about 20 m from riflemen who hold and are already firing; the first is hit 0.5 s after the group becomes aware and three are down within 2 s. Two bots that had already taken cover (slots 0 and 2 in cover, 60 and 30 rounds fired) were not hurt. Riflemen put a standing bot down in about 1.5 s at 20 m.
- Upload stage (seed 12): the garrison's counterattack spawns at the objective, 3-8 m from where the squad is entering, and puts five of six down in 2 s.
- The spawner places a wave only where no *seated human's* eye can see it (`SpawnerHost.humanEyes`). In the sim every slot is a bot and `testHumanCount` only sets the budget, so the rule never applies there; in the game it applies to humans, never to bots.

**Experiments, each reverted (20 seeds; complete / ends <60 s):**

| change | 6-human | 1-human |
|---|---|---|
| none (after U-084) | 0 / 8 | 10 / 4 |
| lead: only the fireteam nearest the enemy attacks, the other holds ("bounding") | 3 / 7 | 2 / 4 |
| sim: the first `testHumanCount` slots' eyes count for the spawner's visibility rule | 4 / 8 | 1 / 4 |

Each trades one budget for the other (the 20-seed standard error is about two completions), so none is a fix. The second is a fidelity question for the owner (below); with it, 1-human runs lose at 148-161 s to riflemen appearing in the compound as the tank arrives.

**Decisions for the owner (defaults) — all accepted 2026-10-01:**

1. Squad tactics: several attackers against several holders, moving in bounds with focus fire, is a project, not a tweak (U-086 showed the simple version loses 1v1). Default: not now; it is an epic to schedule against the campaign work.
2. Whether the spawner should hide waves from *every* squad member's eyes, bots included, in the game (not only humans'). Default: no change until U-086 is measured; it changes real play.
3. What the 6-human floor means. It guards the bots, and 0 of 20 says the six-bot squad cannot beat twice the enemies under the first-death rule. Options: keep it and leave `pnpm sim-run --scenario mission` failing; set it to what the squad can do (a gate change only you should make); or run the 6-human budget at a smaller director size in the sim. Default: re-baseline both floors from 20 seeds once you agree to it, and keep the run honest in CI (assert at 20 seeds, not 3).

**Not claimed.** Nothing here is a fix or a playtest verdict.
