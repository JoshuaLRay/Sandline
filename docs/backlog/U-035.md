# U-035 — Enemy tank attacks during the mission-01 upload

Epic: **Mission / vehicle combat**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: owner request, 2026-09-28: “I’d like to add enemy tanks. It should come down the dirt road from our spawn once the upload obj starts.”

## Problem / outcome

Mission-01 currently sends infantry when the upload objective begins. The owner wants an enemy tank to come down the dirt road from the squad's spawn when the upload starts. This is a new enemy vehicle, not an infantry archetype or a static prop.

## Scope

Add an authoritative enemy tank encounter to mission-01. Spawn it near the squad start, drive it toward the compound along the dirt-road approach, let it threaten the squad during the upload, and give the squad a clear way to disable it. Preserve the existing infantry counterattack and lever behavior. Split vehicle simulation/combat/art and mission integration into bounded implementation cards before moving this epic to READY.

Proposed encounter defaults for implementation review: one tank per upload attempt, with a visible arrival warning; no respawn during the same attempt. Its movement stops at an appropriate firing position if the road does not extend into the compound. Checkpoint retry recreates the encounter once; upload interruptions and restarts do not produce extra tanks. A tank surviving upload completion must have an intentional fate that does not make exfil impossible.

## Start here

- `packages/shared/src/data/levels/mission-01.json`: dirt-road tiles run along x=12 from the spawn side toward the compound, but walls cross that route; inspect collision, cover and a driveable path rather than following decals alone.
- `packages/shared/src/data/missions/mission-01.json`: objective 3 is the upload.
- `packages/shared/src/data/scripts/mission-01.json`: its existing `objective-start` trigger sends `counterattack-push` on entering objective 3. The player's successful terminal interaction happens later. Trigger the tank on the actual transition to the upload's active phase, including a friendly bot starting it; do not use `objective-start` alone.
- `packages/server/src/session/Session.ts` (`startUploadAt`), `packages/server/src/session/mission.ts`, `packages/server/src/session/events.ts`: host-side upload transition and script handling.
- `packages/shared/src/net`, `packages/client/src`, and `packages/server/src`: vehicle state, rendering, damage and lifecycle have to be designed and replicated. The existing mounted MG does not supply a driveable armored enemy.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Starting mission-01's upload at the terminal spawns exactly one visible enemy tank near the squad start, on a navigable route along the dirt road toward the compound. Merely reaching the upload objective does not spawn it.
- [ ] The tank moves and attacks under host authority; clients agree on its position, fire, damage and destruction. It has a recognizable tank presentation and meaningful counterplay using an explicitly specified weapon or objective mechanic.
- [ ] Existing infantry pressure and enemy lever interruption still work while the tank is active.
- [ ] Interrupting/restarting the upload does not duplicate the tank. A checkpoint retry or mission restart resets it predictably; joining mid-encounter displays its current state; completion/exfil handles a surviving tank intentionally.
- [ ] Deterministic mission/session tests cover the trigger, route, attacks, destruction, retries and replication; a playtest checks sight lines, timing and difficulty.

## Verification

Exercise the upload with player and bot interaction, interruption and restart, checkpoint retry, two connected clients, and a late join. Inspect the actual road and collision in the browser; run the relevant tests and `pnpm verify` after implementation.

## Handoff / evidence

Intake only. Vehicle combat, tank art, anti-armor counterplay, and the exact route still need decomposition and implementation. No tank is currently added to the game by this card.
