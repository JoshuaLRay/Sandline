## Problem / outcome

Slot 5 (equipment) differs per character. Owner decisions so far: the **Support carries C4**; the **LMG gunner (Brennan) carries a rocket launcher**. The other four characters' equipment is UNKNOWN and is not invented here.

## Owner decisions (2026-09-29)

- **Only the Support can throw C4; everyone else must place it.** Hold left click to aim the throw and release to throw (Support); **right click detonates** placed C4.
- **Equipment is droppable** (same lifecycle as a dropped gun, U-029; E picks it up).
- Still UNKNOWN: how a non-Support character places it (proposal: aim at a surface within reach and left click places it), how many charges may be out (proposal: one at a time per character), blast damage and radius, friendly fire, and the reset on checkpoint retry.

## Scope

Equipment is assigned by character (U-021), not by class.
