# U-049 — Per-character interaction-speed multiplier (the Support's 20% discount)

Epic: **Squad**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: owner decisions, 2026-09-29 (second round on the U-044 scheme).

## Problem / outcome

"Support gets a 20% discount on all interactions": every timed interaction takes 0.8× as long for the Support (a 10 s health kit becomes 8 s). The multiplier is per-character data, not a class constant.

## Scope

A validated per-character `interactionTimeScale` (Support 0.8, everyone else 1.0), applied by the server to every timed interaction: revive (hold E, 3 s), health kits (U-047), lever/upload interactions and any later timed one. Prediction, the HUD progress bar and bots must use the same scaled duration. Does not change non-timed actions (firing, reloading, swapping). UNKNOWN: whether reload counts as an "interaction" (proposal: no); a proposal-only reading, to confirm with the owner.

## Start here

`packages/server/src/session/Session.ts` (revive, upload and lever interactions), `packages/shared/src/data/damage.json` (`reviveSeconds`), `packages/shared/src/data/classes.json` and the character data from U-021, `docs/design/squad-roster.md`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Character data carries `interactionTimeScale`; the Support is 0.8 and the rest 1.0, schema-validated.
- [x] Revive and health kits use it, on the server, the progress the HUD shows (it is the server's percent) and bots (a bot reviver is scaled by its character too). The other timed interactions: none exist for soldiers (the upload is a press that starts a timed objective, the lever is an enemy's).
- [x] A test proves a Support revive takes 0.8 of the base time and another character's is unchanged.

## Verification

Server tests per interaction, `pnpm verify`. Numbers are owner-decided; feel is a playtest judgement.

## Handoff / evidence

Implemented on `task/U-049-interaction-scale`; awaiting review. Numbers are owner-decided; feel is a playtest judgement and no verdict is claimed.

**What changed.** `classes.json` gains `interactionTimeScale` (default 1; `holloway` 0.8, schema-bounded 0.1 to 2). The host scales the revive by the reviver's character (`reviveSeconds`) and the health kit by the applier's (`kitSeconds`); the replicated revive percent uses the same scaled time, so the HUD bar is honest. Firing, reloading and swapping are untouched.

**Evidence.** `packages/server/src/session/interactionScale.test.ts` (the scaled seconds per slot; a real revive loop where the support finishes at 0.8 of the base ticks and Brennan at the base), `healthKit.test.ts` (a hosted support applies a kit in 8 s while Preach needs 10 s), `classes.test.ts`.

**Proposal (labelled).** Reload is not an "interaction" (the card's proposal, unconfirmed by the owner).
