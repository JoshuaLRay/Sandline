# U-001 — Restore reliable mission enemy pressure

Epic: **Mission**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [owner feedback, 2026-09-26](2026-09-26-feedback.md), plus any legacy bug named below.

## Problem / outcome

After the initial spawn, mission-01 can become empty while an objective timer keeps running. Reported after checkpoint retry or killing almost a whole squad; a hidden, unaware survivor may remain. The cause is unconfirmed.

## Scope

Reproduce and fix the encounter lifecycle or pacing defect. Inspect checkpoint restore, pending waves, group completion, alive caps, visibility rejection and unreachable survivors before choosing a fix. Keep diagnostic counts/reasons useful for subsequent reports; preserve intentional quiet periods.

## Handoff / evidence

Branch `claude/keen-hypatia-42tw5p` (the session's assigned branch, not `task/U-001-…`). Nothing here is an owner playtest: the checklist below is for one.

### Observations (reproduced before any fix)

1. **Checkpoint retry empties the map.** `session/encounterPressure.test.ts`, "a checkpoint retry brings back the garrison…": the committed mission-01 on a real `Session` (1-human budget, no seed — nothing in it is random), the west-lane patrol killed (objective 0 → 1, checkpoint), the squad wiped, `retryMission()`. Before the fix: **0 enemies** after the retry (`garrison 0 alive, patrol 0, log` empty); the garrison was alive at the checkpoint (3 placed). Cause: the checkpoint restored the event script's fired set (`@encounter:garrison` among it) but the retry built a fresh spawner, so a group sent before the checkpoint and not beaten was never sent again — and the counterattack, waiting on the garrison's death, could never come. The same restore ran on a hosted room's saved campaign checkpoint.
2. **The defend ran on an empty map, every six-human run.** The mission scenario with a new per-run measure (the longest stretch with nothing alive and nothing queued while a timed objective runs), 6 seeds a budget: at six humans every completed run stood empty **343–388 s** in the defend (its whole 330 s plus the hold before it); at one human, one of six (390 s), the rest about 20 s. Cause: `counterattack` and `counterattack-support` were sent on the garrison's death, so they were spent during the clear-and-hold (whose hold cannot finish while they come) and nothing was left for the defend.
3. **A lone survivor held everything that waited on its group.** A `dead` trigger (and a script's `group-dead`) needed every member dead; one garrison survivor out of sight kept the counterattack back for ever.

### Changes

- **Checkpoint restore** (`Session.restoreEvents`, `EventRun.groups`): after the script is restored, every group a fired event sent that the checkpoint did not count beaten is sent again from its first wave; every group a fired event stopped is stopped again. Used by `retryMission`, a hosted room's `startRoom`, and a started session built from a saved campaign checkpoint (which previously built its spawner without the checkpoint's completed groups).
- **Script-sent groups and `stop-group`** (encounter trigger `{ "kind": "script" }`, event action `stop-group`): a group only the script sends, and an action that ends a group's waves and drops its queued members (the living fight on). mission-01's defend now has its own counterattack, `counterattack-push` — a rifleman into the compound (garrison posture) every 40–65 s, eight waves — sent when the defend starts and stopped when it is won. `counterattack` (now two waves of three, 30–60 s apart) still answers the garrison's fall and contests the hold. `counterattack-support` is gone: it held its post in a spawn zone no human can see, and measured 0 % contact with the squad — presence, not pressure (a hold-posture defend measured no damage taken and no enemy in contact for the whole 330 s). `exfil-ambush` is a script group instead of a one-hour timer.
- **Stragglers** (encounter `stragglers`, default 1 alive for 60 s; mission-01 1 for 45 s): a group down to that many (with at least one lost) for that long counts as beaten to `dead` triggers, `group-dead` events and checkpoints. A `destroy` objective still needs every member.
- **Diagnostics**: `Spawner.status()` / `describe()` give each group's state (waiting on its trigger kind, queued, fighting, stragglers, beaten, dead, stopped), waves sent and alive/placed, and `heldBack` counts queued members the last step could not place by reason (cap, seen by a human, occupied). The scenario report prints each run's longest empty stretch.
- **No-stall assertion**: `mission.json` `maxQuietSeconds` 150 — the longest a timed objective may run with no enemy in contact with the squad (an enemy knowing of a squad soldier; contact, not presence) — asserted on every run, CI's three seeds included, and by `--all-missions` as a failure, not a warning. The ceiling allows the compound's intentional quiet hold (60 s) and the defend's first rifleman reaching the squad.
- **`--all-missions` plays the committed script**: it parsed the mission file into a new object, and the session only loads the committed script for the committed object itself, so those runs had no script (and, now, no defend counterattack). The scenario passes the committed script when the file is the committed mission by value.

### Mission-01 playthrough / retry checklist (for the owner; not run by the agent)

1. `?mission&squad`: at the start, the west-lane patrol and the compound garrison are out; the east-lane position appears as you reach the east entry.
2. Kill the west-lane patrol, then let the squad die before touching the garrison; press P. The garrison is back in the compound (not an empty map), once.
3. Take the compound: kill the garrison; one counterattack (two waves) comes; hold a minute (quiet once it is beaten — intentional until U-011).
4. The defend: a rifleman comes at the compound every 40–65 s for the whole 5 min 30 s; you are never left alone for long.
5. Lose inside the defend and press P: the defend's counterattack comes again from its first wave, not twice.
6. Win the defend: its waves stop; the exfil ambush appears on the way back. Lose there and press P: the ambush comes again, the defend's counterattack does not.
7. Leave one garrison member alive somewhere out of the way: the counterattack still comes, about 45 s later.
8. Same on a hosted room (`HOST_AI=1 pnpm host`, lobby → mission map); `?qa` + B shows AI debug.

### Not done / next

- Balance: the defend is now a fight. Headless completion is 50 % at one human and 60 % at six (from 70 % / 75 % with an empty defend); losses are nearly all in the defend, by bots that come into it hurt (no healing between objectives) and lose close fights (B-11). Whether a person finds the defend right is the owner's playtest (m5 run sheet).
- The scenario's cover share sits at its 25 % floor: the defend's attackers cross open ground into the compound under fire. A change that sends more attackers across it will trip the gate; the fix then is attackers that use cover on the way in, not a lower floor.
- The compound's minute-long hold stays quiet once its counterattack is beaten: intentional and bounded, and U-011 replaces hold timers.
- Stragglers are released by time only; they are not sent to hunt the squad.
