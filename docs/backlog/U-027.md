# U-027 — Keep bots responsive after traversing large rubble

Epic: **Bot navigation**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [owner feedback, 2026-09-26](2026-09-26-feedback.md).

## Problem / outcome

Bots can navigate onto large rubble, then become stuck and stop responding to commands. The build, mission, exact rubble piece, command types and frequency are UNKNOWN. The cause has not been investigated.

## Scope

Reproduce and diagnose the bot navigation/command failure on large rubble, then fix it so a bot remains able to follow subsequent valid player orders after traversing the obstacle. Preserve existing order semantics and navigation constraints. Do not assume the navmesh, collision, path-following or order system is the cause before investigation. If a destination cannot be reached, the bot must not silently enter a permanently unresponsive state.

## Start here

- `packages/server/src/ai` — authoritative bot behavior and order handling; locate the active navigation/path-following code
- `packages/shared/src/ai` — shared behavior tree and navigation interfaces
- `packages/tools/src` — level/navmesh generation; inspect the rubble collision/nav data after reproducing
- `docs/adr/006-navigation.md` — navigation decision

These are investigation entry points; confirm the live call path and asset/nav data before editing.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A regression reproduction places a bot on or routes it across a large rubble obstacle, then issues a valid movement order.
- [x] After traversing the rubble, the bot accepts and executes subsequent valid player orders instead of remaining stuck or silently ignoring them.
- [x] The fix respects walkable geometry and does not make rubble or other blocked surfaces traversable by changing movement constraints.
- [x] If a commanded destination is unreachable, the bot remains responsive to a later valid order; it does not enter a permanent stuck state.
- [x] A focused automated regression test covers the rubble traversal followed by a new order; relevant bot/navigation tests and `pnpm verify` pass.
- [x] The handoff records the reproduced obstacle/mission, observed command behavior, diagnosed cause and verification evidence. Unknowns remain explicit until observed.

## Verification

Use a deterministic bot/navigation test or add a bounded fixture reproducing the obstacle and order sequence. If the issue only reproduces in the game, record the mission, obstacle, order sequence and result in the handoff in addition to automated coverage. Run `pnpm verify`.

## Handoff / evidence

Branch `claude/keen-hypatia-42tw5p` (the session's assigned branch). No playtest or owner verdict is claimed.

### Reproduced obstacle and behaviour

- **Obstacle:** `rubble-large`, the only large rubble in any level. It stands in the kit gallery (`data/levels/kit-gallery.json`, the lobby's Kit gallery map) at x −18, z 24; its collision is one box 2.2 × 1.6 m and 1.0 m high. Which mission and build the owner saw it in is still unknown; this is the only place the piece is placed.
- **Navmesh as baked** (a probe of the committed kit-gallery bake):
  - a walkable island on the pile's top (polygons 1393 and 1394 at y 1.05);
  - four vault links, all one-way from the ground **up** onto the top, and none down;
  - a path from the top to the ground east of the pile ends at the island's edge (−17.30, 1.05, 24.00).
- **Order behaviour on a real Session** (`ai/friendly/rubble.test.ts`, friendly tree, the gallery's baked navmesh, a human lead over loopback, before the fix):
  - ordered onto the pile, the bot vaulted up and reported `done, at the point`, standing at (−17.62, 1.00, 24.03);
  - ordered to (−10, 0, 24), it reported `failed, unreachable` and stayed at (−17.46, 1.00, 23.88);
  - every later order would do the same: the owner's "will not move, refuses commands".
- A soldier a player leaves on the pile after switching away (U-026) becomes such a bot too.

### Cause

A vault carries 1.5 m and the pile is 1.6 m deep, so every vault at it lands on its top. The bake (`tools/src/nav/bake.ts`) made one-way vault links only, and nothing on the mesh led down. The top was a dead end on the mesh, though not in the world: the controller walks off a 1 m edge and falls like any soldier.

### Fix

- **Drop links** (`dropLinks`, baked beside the vault links):
  - for every box whose top stands above a step, at points along each face, a one-way link from just inside the edge on the top to just outside it below;
  - only where the drop is more than a step and no more than a vault's height, so any top a soldier can vault onto, he can walk off again;
  - both ends must be on the mesh.
- They carry the walk flag and not the vault flag, so the path follower walks them as ordinary legs, and they are priced as vault links are.
- Nothing about movement, collision, the vault or which surfaces are walkable changes.
- The staleness hash covers the new rule. `pnpm gen:nav` rebaked every world:
  - the kit gallery gains exactly 4 drop links, all off the pile;
  - `range`, `greybox-01` and `mission-01` gain none, and their links and routes are unchanged (only their hash moved).
- One consequence: a path across the pile now goes over it (vault up, walk off) where it went round before. This is legitimate existing movement.

### Verification

- `ai/friendly/rubble.test.ts` (3 tests):
  - **sent onto the pile then ordered away:** the bot comes down and arrives 0.4 m from the point with `done, at the point`;
  - **left standing on the pile, then ordered away:** it comes down and arrives, `done`;
  - **an unreachable order:** it reports `failed, unreachable`, the lead is told, and the next order is carried out.
- Before the fix, the first two failed exactly as the owner described.
- `tools/src/nav/vault.test.ts` covers drop links:
  - all four sides of a vaultable top, from on the top to the ground below;
  - none off a steppable top, and none off a top above vault height;
  - the kit gallery has exactly 4 on the pile, and no other world has any.
- `pnpm verify`: 188 files, 2028 tests, all passed.
- `pnpm sim-run --scenario mission --seeds 3` gives the same results as before seed for seed. `--all-missions` is OK. `cover-duel`, `pinned`, `mg` and `squad` meet every threshold.
