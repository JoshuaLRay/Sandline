# U-174 — Finish crowded squad move orders after reaching the room

Epic: Squad command. Pillar: P2.
Size: M (confirm the cause before implementation).
Human verdict: none for the deterministic order regression.
Status, priority, dependencies and branch/PR live in BACKLOG.md.
Source: agent observation during U-173, 2026-10-15; no owner playtest claimed.

## Problem / outcome

A six-bot squad plus POW can reach an open room but leave a few move orders
active indefinitely while completed teammates occupy nearby destinations.
Observed with the real Session, friendly/escort trees, standing nav and ordinary
wire Spread/Order messages in the isolated depot. Normal Hold followed by another
Move lets the whole group continue; this is distinct from a floor/path escape.

Reproduction from U-173's initial fixture: spectator commander slot 1, Standard
spread, all-squad Move through B3 (-36,0,158), B5 (9,0,230), B6 (30,0,270), then
B7 (-14,0,310), with Hold between rooms. Wait for every soldier to finish its
individual spaced destination. At B7, slot 1 remains around (-12.190,0,306.245)
against its accepted goal (-11.804,0.05,308.948), after 7,200 ticks. Several other
slots report done; all seven are inside the clear room. Reverse Tight/Standard
also reproduced crowding in B7. Ordinary player frequency: UNKNOWN.

Hypothesis: completed bodies and arrival/avoidance radii obstruct the remaining
short approach to densely packed destinations. No geometry defect established.

## Construction-proof observation — U-221, 2026-10-16

The furnished outpost repeats the need to distinguish physical passage from
order completion. A Wide all-squad Move onto the west porch (3,8,375) assigns
some soldier destinations at x8.1..8.4 inside the west gate; an east-pad Move
(59.5,8,374) can similarly leave trailing destinations in the court. Closely
packed exterior stands can also stop the POW behind already arrived soldiers.
U-221 proves every actor crosses using ordinary individual wire staging with
clearer separated stands, then Hold; it does not fix these order constraints.
Keep the assigned-pattern observation separate from the crowded arrival
hypothesis above. Reproduce and scope independently before changing behavior.
Evidence: [U-221](../verification/U-221-outpost.md),
`qalatOutpostTraversal.test.ts` staging logs in [#352](https://github.com/JoshuaLRay/Sandline/pull/352).

## Audit note — 2026-10-16 (command reliability review, U-203)

A code audit found that a move order has no no-progress timeout: the order
checks reachability only while active (`ai/friendly/orders.ts`), the session
ignores the path follower's stall status for ordered bots, and only commander
supply requests fail on repeated re-plans. The fix here should add a general
"stuck on an order" rule (no progress for N seconds → re-plan, nudge to the
nearest free spot, or report the order failed to its commander) rather than a
crowding special case, so it covers every order kind. Stays INBOX until
reproduced on open floor, as above.
