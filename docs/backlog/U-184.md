answers the open decisions L-01–L-30 in

playable slice, and the answers are recorded verbatim.

  asynchronously by replying to §15 line by line), record each answer and its

## Decision needed

**How would you like to make the loot decisions?**
A — a live design session following the agenda (best for exploring; needs your time);
B — reply to the thirty decisions in writing, "agree" or a change per line (fastest);
C — approve the recommended starting assumptions as a block and revisit after the first playable slice.
**Recommended default: B**, because most decisions have a reasonable default and only a few (L-04 model, L-08 carry rule, L-22 ownership, L-25 account progression) really need discussion. Reply "agree" or pick another.
