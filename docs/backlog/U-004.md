## Handoff / evidence

Branch `claude/keen-hypatia-42tw5p` (the session's assigned branch). No owner visual approval is claimed. The captures below are agent screenshots for the owner's review.

### What was wrong (on `main`, 1a73cb1)

- **The rear sight hid the target.** The generated M4's rear sight was a 12-sided ring with a 5.5 mm hole in a 12 mm outer radius, 0.17 m ahead of the eye at ADS. At the eye that is a 1.9° hole in a 4.4° disc: at 1280×720 a solid dark octagon about 100 px across, hiding everything round the aim point except the post tip.
- **The aim point was hard to pick out.** The front post's two protective ears ended 4 mm below its tip, so the sight picture showed three bars of nearly equal height.

### Change (source generator, `packages/tools/src/art/weapons/weapons.ts`)

- **Rear sight:** now a ghost ring, `AR_RING_OPEN` 10.5 mm and `AR_RING_OUTER` 12.5 mm, with 20 sides so it reads round. At the eye the opening is 3.6° round the aim point, and the rim a thin circle just outside it. Its base block is lowered to meet the rim, not poke into the opening.
- **Front post:** `frontPost` takes an ear drop. The M4's ears end 11 mm below the tip, so the post alone rises to the line. The M249's post and ring are unchanged.
- **Unchanged:** the sight line, the sight point, the eye relief and the viewmodel, so aim semantics are as before: the post's tip is the screen's centre.
- **Regenerated:** `pnpm gen:art` and `pnpm gen:assets` changed `assets/src/weapon-m4.glb`, `client/public/assets/weapon-m4.glb` and the manifest entry (688 → 752 triangles, 208816 → 209964 bytes). No other asset changed.
- **Fallback:** the code-built fallback carbine (`weaponModels.ts`) has no ring and is unchanged.

### Verification

- The existing sight-line tests still pass for the M4, shotgun, pistol, AT4 and M249: an open line, and the post tip on the screen's centre. So do the hold, budget and committed-as-generated tests.
