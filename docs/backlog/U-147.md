## Implementation and evidence — 2026-10-09

Selected from fresh main `4151ce9` after confirming #319/#318 merged, U-146
DONE, and no open PRs or competing task work. The navigation/collection scope
fits one focused PR; mobile controls and physical-phone acceptance stay U-148.

Commander requests now keep a transient approach/collect phase. Approach uses
the real PathFollower, local avoidance and the normal character controller,
with the cache's exact authored feet and a complete endpoint within the existing
0.3 m support tolerance. A legitimate vault can finish, but never collect in the
air. The collection clock remains zero until authored-floor reach, full 3D
range and LOS are valid. Collection keeps the existing 30-tick duration (Support:
24), capacity checks, stable completion order and finite, unreserved stock.

Unsupported floors retain the constructor's rejection. Missing nav, disconnected
floors, invalid path arrival and repeated stalled-path replans fail without a
transfer, reusing the existing `OrderFailed` move cue and a supply-specific host
report. Cancellation, replacement and every ownership/save/mission cleanup
release the owned follower and input immediately. Pressure, recent damage,
locked shells and a traveller's needed self-healing yield to defensive AI;
requests never resume automatically. No generic revive/upload/pickup press or
bot fire is synthesized while supply owns the hands.
