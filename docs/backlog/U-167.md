hillside, so it stands outside the fan cap but no listed ray may touch it.

reveal captures then count the whole ridge (only `east-gate` stays for U-116).

north of west cross its lip strip lower than the spec's 1.0 m lip allows. At the
anchor's standing eye the south-gate gun ray crosses at 0.99–1.17 m and §11.1's
"first tank silhouette visible from V3" X5 turret ray at 0.46–0.80 m. V3's lip
therefore steps down northward in strata, each keeping exactly the 0.70 m
opening (lip top to canopy underside) that rejects the 0.8 m prone body:
1.00/1.70 m to z287, 0.75/1.45 m to z288.15, then 0.50/1.20 m on the strip's
inner 0.10 m with an unreachable 0.30/1.00 m outer ledge to z290. Every lip a
body can touch stays above the 0.45 m step height (a 0.25 m lip let a prone
body step onto it under the canopy). V1, V2 and V3's A7/A8 rays use the
standard 1.00/1.70 m opening. The canopy's high part spans only the 0.8 m strip
west of each landing; side returns run to the canopy top or the T-E crest.

Out of scope: C12-L/C12-U stairs and the C12-L road cap (U-168); disconnected
approach, integrated shortcut, insertion/tank repeat, basement/outpost interface
