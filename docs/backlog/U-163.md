# U-163 — Cut the continuous R0–R7 ridge shelf with its stairs, landings and barrier rock

Epic: Map implementation. Pillar: P6 (objective missions; the ridge is Mission 1's alternative route).

Human verdict: the ridge look/play verdict is batched in U-140.
Status, priority, dependencies and branch/PR live in BACKLOG.md.

Canonical design: [Mission 1 construction specification](../design/maps/qalat-road.md) §§2.2, 3.1–3.2, 6.1, 15.

## Scope

Extend the isolated surface build (`gen:qalat-road-supports`) with the continuous
ridge shelf from the D0 mouth R0 (62,8,68) to R7 (70,20,322): 8 m stepped
ribbons between the listed nodes, the R0 corner bevel, R1/R2/R4/R5/R6 12×12 m
flat landings, the R3 combat shelf x74..86/z158..182 and R7's 8×4 m landing,
all at their listed y. Riser counts are §6.1's (30/30/30/20/0/20/30), 0.20 m
risers on 0.40 m treads. Cut it out of the insertion and road rock and the T-E
ridge body (§3.2 crest anchors), with rock at least 2.2 m above the adjacent
floor along every edge, so no walk, jump, vault, crouch or crawl leaves the
shelf. The only mouth is the D0 court's existing ridge continuation.

Conflict default (§2.2 "distribute risers evenly … remaining length as flat
treads/landings"): each segment's risers form flights of ten on 0.40 m treads,
spaced evenly between the two landing edges, so the equal flat runs before,
between and after flights are the rest landings; every one must be ≥2 m.

Temporary caps, recorded in `qalat-ridge.json`, replace unbuilt work: the V1,
V2 and V3 bay fronts (their §6.2 fans reach the shelf edge, so a barrier there
would break the fan cap; U-141 builds the lip and canopy and removes these caps),
and two hanging walls at R7's bridge mouth (U-164). R7's north-east corner
overhangs the X4 apron: there the floor is a 0.6 m slab and the caps hang above
y14 so the apron stays clear. Screening proofs count permanent solids only.

Out of scope: the R7b bridge and R8/R9 (U-164), huts, survey post, ridge cover
and the R5 hold proof (U-165), bays and C12 links (U-141), campaign activation (U-117).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Nodes, riser counts, landings and the 8 m width are transcribed independently;
  every climb is 10-riser flights of 0.20 m risers and 0.40 m treads (≤5 cm
  rasterization) with ≥2 m rest landings, and every landing is flat at its y.
- [ ] The whole 8 m shelf is supported and clear to head height; every edge
  stands against rock ≥2.2 m above the adjacent floor; edge escapes in four
  stances end on the shelf.
- [ ] Real nav and controller travel D0→R7 and back; the ridge joins the road
  and start only through the D0 mouth; its rock tops are unreachable islands.
- [ ] Permanent ridge rock stays 0.3 m under every bay fan; the temporary bay
  and R7 caps are recorded; the X4 apron and bridge deck volume stay clear.
- [ ] Inherited insertion, road, ingress, first-X5, sweep, nav and return suites
  pass; affected generation, captures, budgets, `pnpm verify` and all four
  latest-head CI jobs pass before merge.
- [ ] Owner ridge look/play verdict — batched in U-140.
