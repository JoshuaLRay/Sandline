# U-025 — Assign bot command authority to human players

Epic: **Squad command**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [owner feedback, 2026-09-26](2026-09-26-feedback.md).

## Problem / outcome

The squad needs explicit human command ownership for bots. Every bot must always have a human commander, and players need to be able to assign any bot to any human player.

## Scope

Implement server-authoritative commander assignment for bot-controlled squad slots and the player-facing assignment control. At campaign start, assign every bot to the human in the lowest-numbered occupied slot. Any connected human may reassign any bot to any connected human, including themselves. A bot must never be left without a valid human commander. Preserve assignments while the campaign runs and reconcile them when players join or leave. If the last human disconnects, pause campaign simulation until a human returns; bots cannot continue without a human commander. Keep assignment separate from who currently possesses a character.

### Design as built

- **Server** (`Session`): `commanders[slot]` holds the slot of the human in command of each bot, or -1 for a human and for everyone while no human is seated.
  - `reconcileCommanders` runs on every seat and release. A bot keeps a still-seated commander; any other goes to the lowest-numbered seated human.
  - A lobby room's start (`startRoom`) resets every bot to the lowest human: the campaign's start. A session without a lobby starts when its first human is seated.
  - `AssignCommander { bot, commander }` (protocol **34**) is accepted from any seated human for any bot and any seated, connected human, themselves included. It is refused before the room starts, for a human-occupied bot slot, a bot as commander, a nonexistent slot, or a dropped or disconnected commander. A refusal leaves the state unchanged and sends the asker the roster as it stands; a connection that never took a seat is ignored.
- **Replication:** `RosterEntry.commander`, sent on every roster (join, leave, start, assignment).
- **Pause:** a started session that has had a human seated, and has none now, is `paused`. Its clock stops: no tick, AI, movement, bleed-out, respawn or mission time. The wall time spent paused is taken off every later step, so ticks and the rewind history carry on where they stopped. A session nobody has been seated in, like the headless scenarios, runs as before.
- **UI:**
  - the HUD's squad list shows "→ you" or "→ name" on each bot, brighter on yours;
  - the Esc menu has a **Squad command** section: six rows, each bot with a picker listing the seated humans ("(you)" marked). The picker sends `AssignCommander` and redraws from the host's roster.
- **Unchanged:** who may give orders (ADR-001: any player, within T-4.27's class rules), possession, and the slots.
- **ADR:** recorded in the ADR-001 addendum of 2026-09-26.
