# U-083 — Flank assignment must be bounded (a ray broad phase was tried and did not help)

Epic: **Campaign design**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [U-081](U-081.md), the large-map spike, 2026-10-01.

## Problem / outcome

After the cover search was indexed (U-081) a 10x map with enemies spread along it still cost 47 ms a tick (114 ms at 20x) against a 33 ms budget, and a CPU profile put 45% in `rayWorld`. The card as first written ("a broad phase for `rayWorld`") was the wrong fix.

## What was found

- **A broad phase was built and measured, and made it worse** (reverted on this branch): a ground grid of boxes, walked per ray, took the 5x map from 13.8 ms to 79 ms spread and from 8.7 ms to 32 ms packed. At 200–700 boxes the scan's cheap bounds reject is already about as fast as gathering cells, so the cost was never a ray's length of scan; it was the number of rays.
- **Who casts them:** counting by caller, `EnemyGroup.assign` (the flank assignment, `group.ts`) cast 5.7 million `seesGround` rays in 600 ticks from just **20 calls**: about 286,000 rays a call, a tick of hundreds of milliseconds each time. It prices every cover point in the ring round the target (6–30 m, about 236 candidates) as a goal, for every flanker, along a route sampled with a ray every half metre, **the whole length of the route**, including the part hundreds of metres from the target.

## Scope (done)

Bound the work without changing what the manoeuvre is:
1. `pricedRangeM` (60): only the stretches of a route within that of the target are priced for exposure; beyond it a stretch costs its length and casts no ray.
2. `flankCandidates` (48): only the cover points nearest a flanker are priced as its goal (a long way round costs more than a short one, so the cheapest is among them).
Both are `group.json` data, schema-validated.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] `pnpm spike:large-map` (server step per tick, 40 enemies, five bots, a human): 10x spread / packed **12.6 / 8.3 ms** (was 47 / 11 after U-081, 83 / 111 before it); 20x **34.4 / 12.7 ms** (was 114 / 17.7; 268 / 295 originally). The 10x target (under about 15 ms) is met; 20x is at the budget's edge.
- [x] The `pinned`, `cover-duel`, `mg` and `squad` scenarios give the same results (`pinned`: the flank walk seen 62.1% against 77.3% on the direct route, the same numbers).
- [x] mission-01's AI cost is not worse (4.8 ms of the tick, 14.5%).
- [x] Tests: `pricedLength` with a range prices the same route to the metre inside it and casts no ray beyond it; the config refuses a bad `pricedRangeM` and `flankCandidates` (`group.test.ts`).

## Handoff / evidence

Implemented on `task/U-083-ray-broadphase` (the name is from the first attempt); awaiting review.

**This is an AI behaviour change, and the owner should know.** The `pinned` scenario and the others are identical, but in mission-01's full mission sim the flank decisions differ in some runs, because a flanker's cheapest goal is occasionally not among its 48 nearest, or an exposed stretch lies more than 60 m out. Per-seed outcomes move (all of those runs already fail at the tank, U-069), and suppression episodes per engagement are 4.8 against 3.6 before (the floor is 2). With 24 candidates it was 8.0, which is why the default is 48. Whether the flanking *feels* different is for a playtest; no verdict is claimed.

**Method.** The profile is `node --import tsx --cpu-prof` on `spike-large-map.ts` 480 with `FLOORS=tight`, aggregating `rayWorld`'s self time by caller; the counts are from temporary counters in `group.ts` (removed).
