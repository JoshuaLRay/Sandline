## Outcome and scope

Turn the existing composed construction into one reproducible **unregistered**
replacement bundle: level, authored squad starts, matching render/collision
inputs, tiled standing nav and cover, hashes and bounded review entry point.
Reuse the accepted construction, U-225 holdings and U-226 fixture; do not rebuild
the map from old production coordinates or introduce new tactical geometry.

`buildOutpostLevel` already composes the predecessor terrain/depot/outpost.
`bakeRoadSupportNav` already tiles the large constructed scene, while the normal
`bakeWorld` path uses the solo bake. Prepare a deterministic export/generator seam
that can later serve registered production without switching its current inputs.
Keep candidate IDs consistent and inject candidate data explicitly; avoid falling
back to the live mission through a committed-world lookup.
