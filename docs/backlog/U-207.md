# U-207 — Investigate, search and return to duty

Epic: Stealth and enemy awareness ([U-204](U-204.md)). Pillar: P4.
Size: M. Human verdict: none (engineering); feel in U-212.
Status, priority, dependencies and branch/PR live in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: owner, 2026-10-16 — "Losing visual contact. Searching last-known positions. Returning to patrol or guard duties. Remaining alert after an encounter." Design §2.

## Problem / outcome

Enemies advance on a heard shot as if in combat and drift back after 20 s.
Outcome: Investigating enemies walk to the suspicious point (one may bring a
buddy), scan, search a small area around the last-known position for a timed
period, then return to their post or patrol as Wary; losing a target in combat
leads to a wider search; a body found raises Alerted (proposal).

## Scope

- Behaviour-tree leaves `investigate`, `search` for rifleman, MG and RPG trees;
  search points from the navmesh around the last-known position; timers from
  `awareness.json`.
- Bodies: dead enemies already stay as corpses for a while; one seen by an
  Unaware enemy raises Alerted (data flag).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A sound off to the side sends one enemy to look, who returns Wary if nothing is found.
- [ ] Losing sight of a soldier in combat leads to a search of the last-known area, not omniscient pursuit.
- [ ] Patrols and guards resume their authored paths (existing patrol tests still pass).
