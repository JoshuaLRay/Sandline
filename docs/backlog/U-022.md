# U-022 — Implement dual-primary rules for Preach and Support

Epic: **Squad**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [owner feedback, 2026-09-26](2026-09-26-feedback.md), plus any legacy bug named below.

## Problem / outcome

The roster allows two primaries only for squad leader Preach and Support. They may carry ARs, shotguns and SMGs as dual-primary weapons; neither may have a second primary while holding an LMG or sniper rifle. Support also carries the approved SMG/shotgun loadout, runs faster, cannot ADS, and is third-person only (no first-person view at all, decided 2026-09-29).

## Scope

Implement authoritative two-primary inventory and selection for Preach and Support, and enforce the same constraints in local prediction. Only these two characters may carry a second primary. The only eligible primary categories are AR, shotgun and SMG; disallow a second primary while either character is holding an LMG or sniper rifle. Support's starting SMG/shotgun pair and speed/ADS behavior remain as specified by U-019; the support has no pistol (decided 2026-09-29); the two-primary selection keys and exact pickup replacement behavior remain open until decided. (Pick up/swap is E and drop is G by the 2026-09-29 decision recorded in U-029.)

- [x] Support can carry and switch between both SMG and shotgun with independent ammo/reload state.
- [x] ADS and FPS are unavailable via all paths, including saved view preference, recoil/scope toggles, respawn, reconnect and possession change.

- [x] Weapon pickup/exchange replaces only the intended active primary; the other primary and any sidearm policy remain correct. Left-handed-sniper eligibility is covered by U-029.

## Handoff / evidence

Implemented on `task/U-022-dual-primary`; awaiting review. No owner playtest verdict is claimed.

**What changed.** Weapons gain a `role` (ar/smg/shotgun/lmg/marksman/sniper/pistol/melee); only ar, smg and shotgun are dual-primary eligible. `classes.json` gains `dualPrimary` (Preach and Support). The Weapon component gains `secondary` (`NO_SECONDARY` = 15 when none), `PROTOCOL_VERSION` 46. The host keeps a per-gun magazine for a gun that is stowed (`drawGun`), so switching between the two primaries keeps independent ammo; the client (`CombatQA`) mirrors that. Checked by the server (`dualPrimary.test.ts`, 12 tests), `weaponKey.test.ts` and the updated `pickupEquip`/`hostPrimary` tests. ADS/first person for Support were done in U-021; the speed bonus is U-050.

**Proposals (labelled, not owner decisions; reversible).**
- Key 1 = primary. Key 2 = the secondary: the pistol, or the second primary. **Owner decision (2026-09-30):** Preach has no pistol while carrying two primaries (like Support); taking the second gun gives the pistol up, and a respawn restores one primary and the pistol.
- Pickup: a dual-primary character with one primary and an eligible ground gun carries it as the second primary (and draws it). With two, an eligible gun replaces the gun in hand; an LMG or sniper is refused while two are carried. The replaced gun lands on the ground with its rounds. Anyone else replaces the primary as before; the left-handed sniper still takes nothing (U-029).
