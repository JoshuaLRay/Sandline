# U-052 — Authored loot: a mission places a left-handed gun for the sniper

Epic: **Squad**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: split from [U-029](U-029.md) (its third criterion), owner direction 2026-09-27.

## Problem / outcome

The left-handed sniper takes no gun a squadmate or an enemy leaves; his other left-handed guns come from authored loot, including mission rewards. U-029 makes the host take a left-handed gun off the ground for him, but no mission places one yet: pickups exist only as enemy drops and dropped guns.

## Scope

A mission-authored pickup (a weapon id, rounds, a place, optionally a trigger or reward event) placed through the host's pickup list, surviving the enemy-drop rules (not in `pickups.json` `weapons`, not cleared by the despawn timer unless authored so). INBOX: needs the mission format read and a decision on which left-handed guns exist beyond the bolt-action sniper (the semi-auto sniper is left-handed?) before it is READY.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A mission can place a pickup of a named gun at a place, or as a reward on an event.
- [x] The slot-4 sniper can take a left-handed authored pickup; nobody's enemy drop changes.
- [x] Authored pickups obey the same host pickup rules (reach, line of sight, one winner).

## Owner decisions (2026-09-30)

1. A new left-handed semi-auto sniper (`sniper-semi-left`), the right-handed one mirrored, is the loot.
2. A mission event has a `pickup` action (gun, rounds, place, facing).
3. Mission-01 places it in a corner of the compound.
4. **Only the left-handed sniper takes a left-handed gun; nobody else can, his own put down included. He takes no right-handed gun.**
5. A checkpoint remembers the loot state, and the world as it was: "just like the first time the campaign made it to that save point". The loadout and ground half is here; the rest (living enemies, health, placed equipment, the campaign file) is [U-059](U-059.md).
