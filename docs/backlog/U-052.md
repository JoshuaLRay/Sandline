Epic: **Squad**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).

## Problem / outcome

The left-handed sniper takes no gun a squadmate or an enemy leaves; his other left-handed guns come from authored loot, including mission rewards. U-029 makes the host take a left-handed gun off the ground for him, but no mission places one yet: pickups exist only as enemy drops and dropped guns.

## Scope

A mission-authored pickup (a weapon id, rounds, a place, optionally a trigger or reward event) placed through the host's pickup list, surviving the enemy-drop rules (not in `pickups.json` `weapons`, not cleared by the despawn timer unless authored so). - [ ] The slot-4 sniper can take a left-handed authored pickup; nobody's enemy drop changes.
- [ ] Authored pickups obey the same host pickup rules (reach, line of sight, one winner).
