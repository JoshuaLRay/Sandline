# U-194 — Reconcile character-exclusive equipment rules with equipment freedom

Epic: Loot and progression ([U-183](U-183.md)). Pillar: P8, P1.
Size: M. Human verdict: none (engineering); the feel is judged in U-193.
Status, priority, dependencies and branch/PR live in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: owner, 2026-10-16 — "Existing character-specific permissions, two-primary restrictions, and special-equipment behaviors should be audited and reconciled against this approved direction. Do not silently preserve a restriction … However, do not invent the final inventory-slot, carry-capacity, or equipment-compatibility rules before the dedicated loot design session." [ADR-024](../adr/024-equipment-freedom-and-loot.md) decision 5.

## Problem / outcome

The code still enforces character-keyed equipment rules from the fixed-role
era (audit: [LOOT-PROGRESSION.md §14](../design/LOOT-PROGRESSION.md#14-restrictions-audit-and-recommended-reconciliation),
X1–X15). Outcome: every rule is either removed, made equipment-based for all
characters, or kept explicitly as a personal characteristic, per the owner's
answers to L-08–L-15, so any character can develop into any role (Marsh within
left-handed-compatible firearms).

## Scope

- X1/X4/X11/X14: one carry rule for every character (default L-08: today's
  weapon rule for two primaries applies to all; frag droppable).
- X3: Holloway starts without a pistol but may pick one up (L-09).
- X5/X6/X8: handedness from item data; sidearms, launchers and the knife are
  `any` (L-10); right-handed soldiers cannot use left-handed variants (L-11).
- X7: Marsh may acquire compatible firearms by any path (already decided).
- X9: Holloway keeps third-person and no iron-sight ADS; a magnified optic
  gives him its scope view (L-12).
- X10: Holloway alone throws placed charges; everyone places them (L-13).
- X12: supply caches refill what a soldier carries, capped by item data (L-14).
- Bots: whatever a human soldier may now carry, a bot may carry and use (ADR-001).
- Client: weapon keys and HUD reflect the carried set, not the class list.

## Decision needed

This card can start as soon as the owner answers **L-08 to L-14** (or agrees
to all their defaults at once).
**Question:** may the agent apply the recommended defaults for L-08–L-14 now,
before the full loot session? A — yes, apply the defaults now (players can
re-equip freely sooner; any later loot decision can still change the carry
rule); B — wait for the full session.
**Recommended default: A**, because these defaults only widen what each
character may carry and are easy to revise; they do not commit the loot system.
Reply "agree" or pick another.
