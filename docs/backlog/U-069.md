# U-069 — Mission-01 integration: the upload trigger, retry and the survivor

Epic: **Mission / vehicle combat**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [U-035](U-035.md), owner decisions 2026-09-30 (all the proposed defaults).

## Problem / outcome

Mission-01's upload brings a tank down the dirt road, per [U-035](U-035.md), and the encounter stays fair on retry and at the end.

## Scope

- **Trigger:** the upload actually starting at the terminal (including a friendly bot starting it), not merely reaching the objective: a script action/trigger on the upload's active phase. One tank per upload attempt, with a visible **arrival warning** (a message and a callout) and no respawn within the attempt.
- **Route:** the authored tank path (U-067) along the dirt road from the spawn side; script `toggle-blocker` actions open the walls on that line as the tank arrives, so the breakthrough is visible.
- **Interruption:** an upload interrupted and restarted does not produce a second tank.
- **Path and drive state:** the tank's path comes from the mission data (validated against the world: no waypoint inside a wall) and is given to `EnemySpawn.path`; a checkpoint must also save and restore its drive (next waypoint, heading, phase) and turret yaw, which U-059's enemy snapshot does not carry.
- **Retry and checkpoint:** a retry recreates the encounter once; the tank is part of U-059's checkpoint world, so a checkpoint during the fight restores it where it was, with its health.
- **Survivor:** when the upload completes a surviving tank **withdraws** (drives back out along its path) so exfil is possible; a destroyed one stays as a wreck that does not block exfil.
- Existing infantry pressure and the enemy lever keep working while the tank is active.
- Mission data stays valid (`checkMission`), and `sim-run --scenario mission` covers the encounter.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Starting the upload spawns exactly one tank near the squad start, on its path; merely reaching the objective does not.
- [x] An interrupted and restarted upload adds no tank; a retry recreates it once; a joiner mid-fight sees its current state.
- [x] A surviving tank withdraws on upload completion; a wreck does not block exfil.
- [x] Infantry pressure and the lever still work; the mission sim passes with the tank.
- [x] Deterministic session tests cover the trigger, route, retry and withdrawal (blockers: see the evidence).
- [ ] The owner's playtest judges sight lines, timing and difficulty.

## Handoff / evidence

Implemented on `task/U-069-tank-mission`; awaiting review. No owner playtest verdict is claimed: sight lines, timing and difficulty are the owner's to judge.

**What changed.**
- **Trigger.** `upload-start {objective}` (shared `events.ts`; the objective must be an upload) is true while that upload's phase is `active`, so a player's press and a friendly bot's press (both reach `MissionRun.startUpload`) start it and reaching the objective does not. Events fire once per attempt, so a cut and restarted upload does not fire it again.
- **Actions.** `spawn-vehicle {vehicle, x, z, yawDeg, path}` validates at parse time that the archetype has a `vehicle` block, the path has 1–64 waypoints and the hull (radius + 0.1, step 0.45, clearance 2.4 — the numbers the drive uses, now `VEHICLE_STEP_M`/`VEHICLE_CLEARANCE_M`) fits at the start and at every waypoint; `withdraw-vehicles` turns every living driving vehicle round (`withdrawDrive`: the waypoints it passed, last first, then where it started; a tank that has driven out is removed from the map, not left as a corpse). A withdrawing tank holds its fire.
- **Mission data** (`scripts/mission-01.json`): `tank-arrives` (upload-start, objective 3) shows a message, a `contact` callout and spawns the tank at (-22, -12) with an eight-waypoint path: north up the west road, a jog to x = -29.5 round `ow-low-2` (the hull cannot pass between it and `ow-low-1`), back to x = -22, up to the compound's west gap and stop at (-16, 70.2), where the terminal is in its sights through the gap. `tank-withdraws` (objective 3 complete) withdraws it. **No `toggle-blocker` is used:** the west road has no wall on it (the static walls are on the east lane), so the card's "open the walls" had nothing to open; the session still holds a tank for a script blocker on its line (U-067's tests).
- **Checkpoint.** `EnemyCheckpoint.vehicle` (`path`, `next`, `heading`, `phase`, `origin`, `withdrawing`, `turretYaw`, `cannonIn`) is saved, validated by `parseCheckpointWorld`, and restored onto the respawned tank. The shell's tell is not kept (a restored tank is simply ready again after its saved wait). A checkpoint saved the tick the upload finishes comes before the script has heard of it; the retry's first tick hears it again and withdraws the tank once.
- **Retry.** A retry from before the upload clears the world and the script's fired set with it, so the tank is created again once when the upload next starts.

**Evidence.** `packages/server/src/session/tankMission.test.ts` (9, on the committed mission): none while the upload waits, one when it starts (near the start, on the west road); none added by a cut and restarted upload; the whole road driven to the gap; a survivor withdrawn on completion and removed; a wreck left where it fell; no fire on the way out; a retry recreates it once; a checkpoint restores its place, heading, turret, health and cannon; a withdrawing tank stays withdrawing across the checkpoint. Plus `events.test.ts` (parse, hull validation, once-only trigger), `vehicle.test.ts` (`withdrawDrive`), `checkpointWorld.test.ts` (the vehicle block). `pnpm verify`, `sim-run` and the CI jobs: see the PR.

**Mission sim.** `sim-run --scenario mission --seeds 3` (the CI run) and `--all-missions --seeds 3` pass their thresholds (AI cost 21.5% of the tick, ceiling 25%); `--scenario squad` passes. **The friendly bots do not yet deal with the tank**, so they no longer finish mission-01: 0 of 6 runs complete, against 1 of 6 before (that one at 336 s), and the failures are `defended area overrun` from 51–143 s. The 10-seed completion floor (20% at the one-human budget, not asserted at CI's 3 seeds) therefore fails with the tank (0/10) where it held before (the 6-human floor already failed on `main`). The tank is the point of U-035 and bot handling of launchers/C4 is the owner's stated later work, so the floor is not loosened here; whoever picks up the bot response should expect to restore it.

**Not here.** The tank's mesh, the aiming tell's look and the HUD warning (U-070); bots choosing to deal with the tank; a joiner mid-fight is covered by the ordinary snapshot (the tank is an entity), not by a dedicated test.
