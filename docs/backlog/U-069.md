# U-069 — Mission-01 integration: the upload trigger, retry and the survivor

Epic: **Mission / vehicle combat**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [U-035](U-035.md), owner decisions 2026-09-30 (all the proposed defaults).

## Problem / outcome

Mission-01's upload brings a tank down the dirt road, per [U-035](U-035.md), and the encounter stays fair on retry and at the end.

## Scope

- **Trigger:** the upload actually starting at the terminal (including a friendly bot starting it), not merely reaching the objective: a script action/trigger on the upload's active phase. One tank per upload attempt, with a visible **arrival warning** (a message and a callout) and no respawn within the attempt.
- **Route:** the authored tank path (U-067) along the dirt road from the spawn side; script `toggle-blocker` actions open the walls on that line as the tank arrives, so the breakthrough is visible.
- **Interruption:** an upload interrupted and restarted does not produce a second tank.
- **Path and drive state:** the tank's path comes from the mission data (validated against the world: no waypoint inside a wall) and is given to `EnemySpawn.path`; a checkpoint must also save and restore its drive (next waypoint, heading, phase) and turret yaw, which U-059's enemy snapshot does not carry.
- **Retry and checkpoint:** a retry recreates the encounter once; the tank is part of U-059's checkpoint world, so a checkpoint during the fight restores it where it was, with its health.
- **Survivor:** when the upload completes a surviving tank **withdraws** (drives back out along its path) so exfil is possible; a destroyed one stays as a wreck that does not block exfil.
- Existing infantry pressure and the enemy lever keep working while the tank is active.
- Mission data stays valid (`checkMission`), and `sim-run --scenario mission` covers the encounter.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Starting the upload spawns exactly one tank near the squad start, on its path; merely reaching the objective does not.
- [ ] An interrupted and restarted upload adds no tank; a retry recreates it once; a joiner mid-fight sees its current state.
- [ ] A surviving tank withdraws on upload completion; a wreck does not block exfil.
- [ ] Infantry pressure and the lever still work; the mission sim passes with the tank.
- [ ] Deterministic session tests cover the trigger, route, blockers, retry and withdrawal; a playtest (owner's) judges sight lines, timing and difficulty.
