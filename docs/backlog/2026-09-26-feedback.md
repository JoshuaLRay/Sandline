## Audio → U-007, U-008, U-012–U-016

First pass at sound is DECENT at best. We need a reload sound. Gun sounds too
far to the right when hipfiring in fps. We need to simulate some type of
dialogue-through-radio sounding voices. We have no voices now. At least a
little grunt when getting shot, and a cry out when down, and a final sigh when
dying (all for the good guys). Taliban-sounding shouts from the enemies when
engaging would be cool, too.

## Weapon presentation → U-002–U-004, U-006

Visual art needs an upgrade too. Take the big metal ring off the AR, as it
obstructs ads vision too much. Make the sights unobtructive, but clearly pointing
to the target in the middle of the screen. The muzzle flash and bullet trace
needs work in fps on the AR, at a minimum. It looks like it has many different
tracers when ads. Also the bullets don’t look like they really come from my gun
when crouched or prone when ads in fps. When hipfiring, it never looks like it
comes from the gun. Reloading should have an animation rather than having the
gun disappear.

## Missions → U-001, U-009–U-011

Missions need work too. I do not like the idea of a mission to be, stand in an
area for x seconds. How about we always have some sort of interaction? “Protect
a file upload from the enemy interacting with a lever”. Keeps us holding down an
area without forcing us to stand in a certain box.

After the initial enemy spawn on mission start, sometimes enemies don’t spawn
(or there is just 1 somewhere oblivious and hidden) - like after loading a
checkpoint, or just wiping *almost* then entire enemy squad - I’m not sure of the
cause, but sometimes the mission 1 map is just empty, even while holding down an
obj and a timer goes down.

## Squad → U-019–U-023

The squad will be 6 unique, named characters, that each have their own set of
weapons/skills. 2 of the characters will have snipers, 1 an LmG, 1 an AR, 1 a
scoped AR, and the final will be a support role that can’t ads (or fps) that will
be able to hold 2 primaries - smg and shotgun, run a bit faster.
