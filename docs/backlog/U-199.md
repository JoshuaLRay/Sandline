Epic: Weapons and equipment. Pillar: P4, P2.
Size: M. Human verdict: batched in U-119 (Mission 1 play) once built.
Status, priority, dependencies and branch/PR live in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: owner, 2026-10-16 — review "Ammunition limitations" and "Soldier-to-soldier ammunition transfers"; parity CP-17 (corrected) and CP-04, gap G-5.

## Problem / outcome

Audit 2026-10-16: each gun has one magazine and **reloads are unlimited**
(`finishReload` refills to `magSize` from nothing); supply caches top up only
the magazine in hand. The series had finite ammunition, ammo pickups and
"Give" ammo. Outcome (Conflict default): every gun has a finite reserve by
ammunition family; empty reserves mean switching weapons, picking up
same-family ammo from dropped weapons and caches, or being given ammo by a
squadmate (human action and bot order).

## Scope

- Reserve rounds per weapon family as data; reload draws from the reserve;
  HUD shows magazine and reserve; caches refill reserves.
- Pick-up of same-family ammo from ground weapons (E on a weapon you do not
  want to swap — exact control per the decision below).
- Give: a human gives a magazine to the squadmate they face; a bot does so on
  the equipment-ring order (U-198 option A) or automatically when a squadmate
  is dry (proposal).
- Carry-over and checkpoints store reserves (save version bump).
- Bots manage reserves (switch weapon, ask, pick up).

## Decision needed

**How much ammunition should a soldier carry, and is it part of the loot
carry rules?** This is a carry-capacity rule, which the owner asked not to
invent before the loot session (L-16).
A — **Conflict default now**: a fixed number of spare magazines per weapon
family (starting point: rifle 6, LMG 3 boxes, SMG 6, shotgun 32 shells, sniper
5, pistol 3), data-tunable, later folded into loot carry rules (players start
rationing ammo now; numbers are easy to change);
B — wait for the loot session and decide with the carry model;
C — keep unlimited reloads (no scarcity; the series' tension is missing).
**Recommended default: A**, because ammunition scarcity is core to the series'
tension and the numbers are data, not a commitment of the loot system.
Reply "agree" or pick another.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Reloads consume reserve; at zero reserve a reload is refused and the HUD says so.
- [ ] Same-family ammo can be picked up and given; the host validates every transfer.
- [ ] Bots switch to another weapon when dry and can be ordered to give ammo.
- [ ] Checkpoint retry and carry-over restore reserves exactly; old saves load with full reserves.
