## Acceptance criteria

### What was wrong (investigated on `main`, 4bab73e)

- **The cues were already there, and on time.** The wiring worked. A probe in headless Chromium, logging every buffer the page starts, saw `reload-out`, `reload-in` and `reload-bolt` each start once per reload, at 0, 0.55 and 0.85 of the carbine's 2.1 s reload. That was so for both a manual reload and an empty-magazine one. Every render loaded.
- **But your own reload-out was buried under a chirp.** At the very instant of every reload of your own, the page also started `radio-chirp`. That is the callout director's stand-in for your soldier's unrecorded "reloading" line: `callouts.json`, `reloading.self: true`, and the voice lines do not exist yet (ADR-017). Measured on the renders, the chirp's loudest 50 ms is −10.6 dBFS against `reload-out`'s −23.0: about 12 dB louder, starting on the same sample.
- **And the cues were quiet in the mix.** Their loudest 50 ms:

  | cue | loudest 50 ms | peak |
  |---|---|---|
  | `reload-out` | −23.0 dBFS | −6 dB |
  | `reload-in` | −25.9 dBFS | −5 dB |
  | `reload-bolt` | −22.4 dBFS | −4 dB |

  That is below a wall impact (−21.6) and far under the carbine's report (−13.6).
- **A remote reload first seen part-way** (a late snapshot) played every stage it had passed at once: three cues in one instant.
- **A render that failed to load** was swallowed silently: the engine stored null and `play` quietly returned false.

### Change

- **Your own unrecorded lines are silent** (`callouts.json` `selfChirp: false`, schema-validated):
  - the chirp is how you hear that a *squadmate* spoke;
  - a line of your own that has been recorded plays as before;
  - a silenced one takes no cooldown and does not count as speaking;
  - squadmates' chirps are unchanged.
  - This is the owner's to reverse by flipping the flag.
- **Louder reload recipes** (`sounds.json`, re-rendered with `pnpm gen:audio`): `reload-out` peak −6 → −2 dB, `reload-in` −5 → −1.5, `reload-bolt` −4 → −1.5, their bounds with them. Only those six renders and `renders.json` changed. Now:

  | cue | loudest 50 ms |
  |---|---|
  | `reload-out` | −19.0 / −18.0 dBFS |
  | `reload-in` | −22.4 / −21.8 dBFS |
  | `reload-bolt` | −19.9 dBFS |

  That is about 4 dB up: above the impacts, still under the report.
- **`ReloadWatcher`:**
  - a reload first seen more than 10 % past a stage (`LATE`) does not play that stage;
  - a sharp fall in progress without a 0 between (one reload done and the next begun between two looks) starts the cues again.
  - Cancellation (progress back to 0) and repeated snapshots behave as before, now under test.
- **Diagnostic:**
  - `AudioEngine.missing` lists every render that failed to fetch or decode;
  - `readout()` says so in the QA panel (`audio … render(s) FAILED to load: …`, or `N sounds loaded, voices n/24`);
  - the page `console.warn`s the list once when loading ends.
- **Sound board** (`?sounds`): a new "Mix samples" section plays three scripts from the committed renders, on the game's own clocks (the carbine's cadence and reload time, the stages from `weaponSounds.json`):
  - *Reload, alone*;
  - *Burst, then reload*;
  - *Reload while a squadmate fires*.
- **Unchanged:** the reload's timing (still the weapon clock, which U-006's animation also keys on), placement and panning (U-008's), and the remote reload cue path.

### Verification

- **`weaponSounds.test.ts`:**
  - late-seen reloads play only the stage just reached;
  - the first snapshot of a fresh reload plays `out`;
  - a cancelled reload plays nothing more;
  - repeated snapshots replay nothing;
  - a new reload begun between two looks plays again;
  - for every gun, fed tick by tick on its reload clock, each cue plays once, within a tick of its stage.
- **`engine.test.ts`:** a render that fails to fetch is listed and shown in the readout, the rest still play, and the missing variant is refused; everything loaded reads as such.
- **`callouts.test.ts`:** your own unrecorded line is silent by default, and costs no cooldown; a recorded one plays as your own, dry; with `selfChirp: true` the chirp is back.
- **`soundBoardModel.test.ts`:** the mix samples run on the game's clocks, the reload after the burst, every cue a committed sound.
- **Browser probe after** (the same script as before): `reload-out`, `reload-in` and `reload-bolt` once each at their stages, for a manual and an empty-magazine reload, with no chirp at the reload's start. A squadmate's chirp during the burst still plays. No render failed to load. The one 404 is `voice/renders.json`: no voice lines are committed yet, as expected under ADR-017.
- **`pnpm gen:audio`:** only the six reload renders and `renders.json` changed, all within their recipe bounds.
