# U-057 — Motion sensor for the right-handed sniper

Epic: **Controls**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [U-048](U-048.md), owner design decisions 2026-09-30.

## Problem / outcome

Vance (slot 5, the right-handed sniper) carries **1 motion sensor** (owner, 2026-09-30; the implemented mechanics below were accepted by the owner on 2026-10-09; see below).

## Scope


A **placed** device built on U-054's placement code (aim at a surface within 2.5 m, left click). Proposal: it runs for 90 s, detects any **moving** enemy (speed over 0.5 m/s) within 15 m **through walls**, and **marks** it for the whole squad through the existing target-mark system (T-3.29), so bots engage marked enemies and every player sees the marker. It has no weapon and no blast. E picks it up (own). It is destroyed by a blast within 2 m, and a checkpoint retry clears it. Enemies do not react to it in this version. One sensor per character.


## Start here

`packages/shared/src/data/projectiles.json`, `packages/server/src/session/Session.ts` (projectiles, pouch, detonation), the foundation from [U-048](U-048.md) and, for placed items, [U-054](U-054.md).

## Acceptance criteria


- [x] Placed like C4; expires at 90 s; E picks it up.
- [x] A moving enemy within range is marked for the squad, a still one is not; walls do not block it.
- [x] Marks fade when the enemy leaves range or stops; tested headless.

## Verification

## Handoff / evidence

Implemented on `task/U-057-motion-sensor`; awaiting review. Numbers are placeholders until playtested; no owner verdict is claimed.

**What changed.** `projectiles.json` gains `sensor` (kind `placed`, 90 s life, no blast) and every row `senseM` / `senseSpeedMps` (0 = not a sensor); the sensor uses 15 m and 0.5 m/s. Vance carries one in slot 5, put on a surface within 2.5 m like C4. Each tick the host compares every living enemy's horizontal position with its last one; one over the speed and within range of a stuck sensor (no line of sight is tested) gets a squad mark through the T-3.29 marks, attributed to the sensor's owner. A mark fades 30 ticks (1 s) after the enemy stops, leaves range or the sensor goes (E, expiry, a blast, retry). Right click does not set it off; a blast within 2 m destroys it. `PROJECTILE_IDS` gains `sensor` (protocol 54); a placeholder held model. Enemies do not react to it.

**Evidence.** `packages/server/src/session/sensor.test.ts` (data and Vance's one; placed and spent; marks a mover in range, not a still one; range limit; marks fade on stop and on leaving; E removes it and its marks; right click ignores it and it expires at 90 s; blast within 2 m destroys it, 3 m does not). The wall criterion is met by construction (no ray is cast) rather than by a wall test. `pnpm verify` result and CI are recorded on the PR.
