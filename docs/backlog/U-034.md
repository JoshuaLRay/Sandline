# U-034 — Restore player and enemy use of mounted MGs

Epic: Emplacements. Status, priority, dependencies and branch/PR live in BACKLOG.md.
Source: 2026-09-28 owner playtest report.

## Problem / outcome

The mounted gun is currently bugged: a player attempting to mount it sees a slight camera change, but firing still uses the weapon they were carrying. Enemies are unable to use the mounted MG as well. Expected: a mounted player fires the emplacement weapon, and enemies can claim and operate an available emplacement. Exact build, input sequence, affected platforms, frequency, and enemy encounter conditions are UNKNOWN.

## Scope

Investigate the client interaction, camera and weapon presentation, host mount and fire routing, and enemy claim, aim and fire behavior. Fix the player and enemy paths together as one emplacement regression. Preserve existing occupancy, traverse, heat, dismount and authoritative damage rules unless investigation shows they are broken. No cause has yet been established.

## Start here

`packages/client/src/main.ts` (interaction, mounted camera and fire input), `packages/server/src/session/Session.ts` (mount and firing logic), `packages/shared/src/sim/emplacement.ts` (placement and arc rules), and mission-01's placed gun. Check relevant nested instructions before changing code.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A player within interaction range can mount an empty MG, aim within its limits, and fire its mounted weapon rather than their carried firearm; the camera, visible weapon, sounds and authoritative shot agree.
- [ ] Dismounting restores the carried firearm and ordinary camera/fire behavior; occupancy and heat remain consistent for other clients.
- [ ] An eligible enemy can take an empty MG, aim and fire at a valid target, and leave or release it under the established rules.
- [ ] Automated regression coverage exercises player and enemy mount/fire paths, including ownership and rejection of invalid fire or claim attempts.

## Verification

Run targeted emplacement and session tests and `pnpm verify`; reproduce in a mission with both a human and an enemy, recording manual results separately from automated results.
