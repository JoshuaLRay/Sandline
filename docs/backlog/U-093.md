# U-093 — Mission 1 encounter: the garrison, the patrols and the tank's arrival

Epic: **Campaign design**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [mission 1's approved brief](../design/MISSION-01.md) (owner, 2026-10-02), split out of [U-065](U-065.md).

## Problem / outcome

The opposition on mission 1's level: who is where, and what sets them off. The tank's arrival is the set piece.

## Scope

Per the approved brief (section 4): the **garrison** (six riflemen and an MG in the compound, garrisoned, set off by the squad entering the outer ring), the **road patrol** and the **riverbed patrol** (three riflemen each, on start), a **terrace sniper** *(a rifleman until the sniper archetype exists, as the brief decided)*, the **counter-attack** (four riflemen from the north, when the prisoner is freed), the **escorted prisoner** (`pow`, U-075) held in the compound, and the **tank** (U-066 to U-070) entering at the road's north end and driving the road south to the extraction point, set off 20 s after the prisoner is freed (sooner if the radio operator is alive). If the squad leaves by another lane the tank still comes down the road and stops at the extraction point; the squad must destroy it either way. The optional **radio operator** is a group of one in the compound.
