# U-214 — Armour hit feedback: impacts, damage stages, destruction and sound

Epic: Armour and vehicles. Pillar: P7.
Size: M. Human verdict: batched in U-119 (look and listen).
Status, priority, dependencies and branch/PR live in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: owner, 2026-10-16 — "Better visual and audio feedback for armour impacts." Audit 2026-10-16: lock lamp, muzzle flash, wreck and HUD hint exist; armour impact sparks/deflection, damage stages, a destruction explosion and all tank audio do not.

## Problem / outcome

A rocket into a tank looks and sounds like a rocket into the ground, and the
tank gives no sign it is close to death. Outcome: distinct impact effects for
a glancing/deflected hit, a penetrating hit and (with U-213) a weak-arc hit;
visible damage stages (smoke at half health, fire near death); a destruction
explosion; synthesised audio for engine and tracks, armour ping, penetration
and destruction (ADR-017 allows synthesised effects; no voices needed).

## Scope

- Client effects from existing Detonation/hit messages plus the arc (U-213)
  when present; damage stages from replicated health.
- Audio through the in-house synthesis pipeline; positional.
- No gameplay change.
