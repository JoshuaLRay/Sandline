# U-213 — Directional tank armour: weak sides and rear

Epic: Armour and vehicles. Pillar: P7.
Size: S–M. Human verdict: batched in U-119 (Mission 1 tank fight).
Status, priority, dependencies and branch/PR live in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: owner, 2026-10-16 — "Directional tank armour. Front, side, and rear effectiveness differences … A tank should encourage meaningful tactical decisions rather than simply requiring a large amount of damage." Parity CP-20, gap G-7 (series: tanks best hit in the rear).

## Problem / outcome

The tank's armour scales damage by damage type only (bullets ×0.1; rockets, C4
and claymores ×1); where a rocket lands does not matter. Outcome (Conflict
default): hits are classified front, side or rear from the hull's heading and
the hit direction (rockets and bullets) or the blast's bearing (C4, claymore),
with data multipliers so flanking is rewarded; the turret counts as the front
arc it faces (proposal); friendly bots prefer a side or rear firing position
when one is reachable.

## Scope

- `enemies.json` tank `armour.arcs` (default starting point: front ×0.5, side
  ×1.0, rear ×1.6 on top of the damage-type factor; data, tuned in play) and arc
  boundaries.
- Host: classify in the shot trace (`lagComp` already reports hull/turret) and
  in `detonate` for blasts; the Detonation/hit feedback carries the arc for U-214.
- Bots: U-079's armour action scores firing positions by arc.
- Mission 1's tank fight stays winnable with the same rockets in the squad's
  hands (verify with the mission sim and the tank tests).
