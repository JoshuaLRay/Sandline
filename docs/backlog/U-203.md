addressing, follow/regroup, hold, move with facing (U-154), attack, marks,
revive, stance (U-153), spread and fire discipline (U-101) all exist; command

order durability (CP-14), manning guns, planting/detonating, equipment
hand-off, friendly suppression, stuck orders, synchronised orders. Outcome:
those gaps closed in priority order, and the owner verifies that a mission
plays end to end with one human commanding five bots.
