# U-012 — Define voice cues and extend event routing

Epic: **Audio**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [owner feedback, 2026-09-26](2026-09-26-feedback.md), plus any legacy bug named below.

## Problem / outcome

No real voices are audible. Required cues are radio dialogue, friendly hit grunt, downed cry, final dying sigh, and enemy combat engagement shouts.

## Scope

Audit the existing callout/voice pipeline, define missing event IDs and cue mappings, and extend source script/manifests for these exact cues. Add routing and cooldown tests using fixtures. U-013 supplies real audio; U-014–016 integrate the audible families.

### Audit (before, on `main`)

- **Callouts:**
  - 20 callout events, all spoken by the six squad slots.
  - `hit` mixed words ("I'm hit") and pain sounds in one cue; `down` did the same.
  - A soldier dying, from down or outright, said nothing, and nothing could voice an enemy.
- **Routing:** one rule for everything. A squadmate within `radioBeyondM` was placed and dry; farther off went over the radio. A pain grunt from far away would therefore have been "radioed".
- **Placeholders:** every unrecorded line played `radio-chirp`, labelled a placeholder only in comments.
- **Reset:** the watcher and director were never reset on a rejoin or a retry.
- **The recording script:** it named no line IDs, and no test tied it to the data.

### Change

- **Cues** (`callouts.json`, `CALLOUT_EVENTS`):
  - `hit` is now the words alone (`im-hit`), beside a new `hit-grunt` (`pain-grunt`, `pain-breath`).
  - `down` is the words alone (`im-down`, `need-help`), beside a new `downed-cry` (`downed-cry`, `pain-groan`).
  - `dying` is new: the last breath (`dying-sigh`), only on a death.
  - `enemy-engage` is new: an enemy's shout as it opens fire (`open-fire`, `there-they-are`, `flank-them`, `push-forward`).
  - Order, reload, contact, objective and the other events are unchanged apart from their route.
- **Routes** (`callouts.json` `routes`; every event names one, validated):
  - `radio`: squad dialogue, exactly as before (placed near, radio far, one voice on air, the chirp placeholder).
  - `body`:
    - heard where the body is, never over the radio;
    - dropped beyond `audibleM` (15 m) without spending the cooldown;
    - −3 dB;
    - involuntary: it neither waits for its soldier's words nor holds them up;
    - its placeholder is silence.
  - `shout`:
    - said with the enemy's voices where the enemy stands, never over the radio, within 80 m;
    - its placeholder is silence.
  - Each route carries its own gain.
  - Data validation refuses a shout in a squad voice, a squad line on the shout route, a shout by your own soldier, and a placeholder that is not a sound.
- **Voices** (`voices.json`):
  - a section may say who speaks it (`speakers: squad | enemy`);
  - three `enemyProfiles` (`e0`–`e2`), chosen by enemy netId;
  - the `hit` section's hurt pass gains `downed-cry` and `dying-sigh`;
  - a new `enemy` section, shouted only.
  - `gen:voice` renders squad sections in the six squad voices and enemy sections only in the enemy voices.
- **Watcher** (`calloutEvents.ts`):
  - every cue is a remembered transition per soldier;
  - alive→downed gives cry, words and "man down";
  - any →dead gives `dying` once, never on a respawn;
  - a round into a squadmate who is up gives words and a grunt;
  - an enemy's first shot, hit or miss, after `engageQuietSeconds` (8 s) of quiet gives its `enemy-engage`;
  - `reset()` forgets everything, and its next look only learns what is already so.
- **Director** (`callouts.ts`):
  - enemy speakers;
  - the routes above;
  - every play carries `route`, `isPlaceholder`, `placeholder` and `gain`;
  - `reset()` clears cooldowns, speakers, the radio and its queue.
- **Page** (`main.ts`):
  - enemy cues are voiced from where the enemy stands, at the route's gain;
  - a missing file plays the route's placeholder, the chirp or nothing;
  - both the watcher and the director reset on a rejoin, a new session, or a new mission attempt (the host's retry).
- **Manifest and script:**
  - `pnpm gen:voice` now also writes `docs/audio/voice-cues.md`: every route, every cue, and every line's clips per voice and treatment, recorded or missing. Today it reads 0 of 414 clips, including the enemy's shouts and `dying-sigh`, and labels the chirp "a PLACEHOLDER, not a voice".
  - `docs/audio/voice-script.md` names every line's ID, with the new body sounds and the enemy section.
  - The sound board's empty-state text says what plays as a placeholder and where the missing list is.

### Verification

- **`client/src/audio/voiceCues.test.ts`:**
  - **Transitions:**
    - down gives cry, words and man-down;
    - the same snapshot repeated gives nothing;
    - bled out gives `dying` once, and a respawn gives nothing;
    - killed outright gives only `dying`;
    - a round into a downed or dead soldier gives no grunt.
  - **Engagement:**
    - an enemy's first shot, even a miss, triggers a shout;
    - its continued fire does not, until it has been quiet for 8 s;
    - another enemy gets its own;
    - a squadmate's shot or a dead enemy triggers nothing.
  - **Reset:**
    - the watcher's pending cues are dropped;
    - the next look calls out nothing already so;
    - the next enemy shot is a fresh engagement;
    - the director's cooldowns, speakers and radio queue are cleared.
  - **Routes:**
    - a body's sound is placed, never on the radio, and dropped beyond reach without spending its cooldown;
    - it is involuntary with respect to words;
    - your own is your own;
    - a shout is in the enemy profile the netId picks, dry, and refused for a squad speaker, for an enemy saying squad lines, and beyond reach;
    - unrecorded plays are labelled placeholders: the chirp for dialogue, silence for body and shout;
    - each route has its own gain.
- **`shared/src/audio/callouts.test.ts` and `voices.test.ts`:** each event's route; dying is not the downed cry; line speakers match their routes; each bad route or placeholder is refused; the enemy's voices and section.
