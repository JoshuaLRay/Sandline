# U-138 — Construct and prove the hidden S0–D0 insertion

Epic: Map implementation. Status, dependencies and work live in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Parent: [U-114](U-114.md). Canonical design: [Mission 1 construction specification](../design/maps/qalat-road.md) §§2, 3 (southern masses), 4.1–4.2.

## Scope

Build an isolated, reproducible replacement-authoring fixture with the full S0
floor, exact six y8 starts, 8 m S0–S3a ribbons, both reveal bends, D0 and its three
continuation sockets. Construct visible solid southern geology and the y13
overhang; prove screening against the approved hostile sockets, interpolated
patrols, ridge overlooks and full X/A return route using actual body/muzzle heights.
Keep the active campaign map playable until U-117 integrates the replacement.

- [x] Exact coordinates, floor/width contract, 5 m hollow headroom and western sky opening; southern terrain coincides with collision.
- [x] Real baked-nav/controller/PathFollower traversal in both directions, six starts, no bypass around the two bends or climb/drop escape from the insertion.
- [x] GEO-07: full 1 m S0 grid and six starts screened at all stance sample heights; approved sockets/patrols (≤1 m interpolation), ridge positions and full tank path/muzzle rays. No AI visibility rule used.
- [x] Reproducible construction captures, focused tests, generator freshness and applicable budgets, with full verification and CI delivery gates below.
- [x] PR/evidence recorded; production activation, D0 signage/dressing and human map/play quality clearly pending in U-117/U-118/U-119.

## Delivery evidence

[PR #310](https://github.com/JoshuaLRay/Sandline/pull/310), based on fresh main
including merged cache/checkpoint PRs #307/#308. Their remaining U-133/U-136
work is separate. U-114 was split before claiming; this PR completes only its
southern insertion leaf. No merge authorization.

The owner answered the straight S3→D0/reveal conflict with **“Add a small bend
to achieve the 12 m reveal.”** The canonical specification and surface diagram
now include S3a `(48,8,46.75)` and T-S3. The addendum explicitly defines the
measurement to the nearest court floor boundary. The standing-eye regression
checks all 575 court grid points from 7,395 legal floor/shoulder probes outside
12 m: 4,252,125 blocked rays, with a visible court control inside that limit.
Floor probes use a 0.5 m grid; both shoulders use ≤0.25 m interpolation and the
actual standing collision footprint. The original six starts and S0–S3/D0
coordinates are retained.

`qalat-insertion.json` compiles visible solid AABB strata, their exposed render
surface and collision from the same geometry. The 0.0125 m carving rows and
0.005 m skin on each axis preserve the 8 m clear ribbons within the design's
0.05 m tolerance. The current isolated build contains 5,777 boxes and 60,318
exposed terrain triangles. Three D0 continuation landings remain temporary
joins for U-139/U-140/U-115; this is outside the campaign registry.

The real production solid-nav bake plus CharacterController/PathFollower walks
all six starts outward and back through every named bend at y8, without vaults.
Direct D0 paths retain the ravine folds; all three continuation nav paths are
complete in both directions. Solid ground/rock prevents routes to crest/roof
islands and actual stand/jump/crouch/prone/vault perimeter attempts.

GEO-07 uses only the southern solids clipped at z60, including the foundation;
unfinished continuation caps and the rest of the map cannot supply screening.
Shared `rayWorld` checks all 10,143 body samples (full 1 m S0 grid plus six starts
at seven feet/eye/body heights) against these observers:

| Observer group | Positions/muzzles | Blocked rays |
|---|---:|---:|
| Guards and reserves | 36 | 365,148 |
| Patrol interpolation | 206 | 2,089,458 |
| Reserve advance interpolation | 291 | 2,951,613 |
| Ridge/overlooks/roof probes | 338 | 3,428,334 |
| Complete X ingress/A return, two tank muzzles × eight orientations | 7,488 | 75,950,784 |

Total: 84,785,337 blocked rays. The broad-phase index is checked against direct
shared-ray results and an empty-scene failure control. No AI range, leash or
script grace period participates in this proof.

- Focused nav/controller/screening/reveal and exposed-surface regressions — all 12 passed in the full suite; the targeted width and direct-controller regressions also passed separately.
- `pnpm verify` — passed: 293 files, 3,044 tests, including typecheck and lint.
- `pnpm check:assets`, `pnpm check:packs` — passed; initial pack 4,975,564 / 80,000,000 bytes.
- `pnpm --filter @sandline/client build` — passed.
