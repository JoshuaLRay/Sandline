# U-075 — Escort: the POW as an unarmed seventh friendly, commandable and spectatable

Epic: **Campaign design**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: owner decisions on the campaign design, 2026-10-01 ([design doc](../design/CAMPAIGN.md)).

## Problem / outcome

Campaign mission 1 frees an unarmed POW and brings him home with the squad. He is treated as a member who can be commanded (go / stay / follow) and spectated, but never controlled by a player, and the extraction needs all seven alive.

## Scope

BLOCKED on the owner's ADR-001 addendum (design doc Q1): the squad is always six; the POW is a **non-slot, non-playable escorted character**. Proposal: a new entity kind (not a slot, so the roster, picker, scoreboard and the six-slot machinery do not change) with health, no weapon and no fire; he follows the squad lead by default and obeys the existing move / hold / regroup orders as go / stay / follow; any player may order him, as with bots; spectators can watch him; enemies target him like a squad member and his death fails the mission (Q4); he walks on the navmesh at a pace the squad can match and goes down and bleeds out like a soldier (no revive needed in the first version: his death is the failure). A `escort` condition on `reach` (all escorted alive in the area) is the extraction. Rendering uses the existing humanoid in a civilian/prisoner palette until U-080.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The ADR-001 addendum is written and accepted.
- [ ] A mission can place an escorted character; he follows, holds and goes on command and keeps pace on the navmesh.
- [ ] He cannot be controlled or taken as a slot; he can be spectated.
- [ ] His death fails the mission and a retry restores him from the checkpoint; the extraction needs him alive in the area.
- [ ] Roster, picker, scoreboard and the six-slot tests are unchanged.
