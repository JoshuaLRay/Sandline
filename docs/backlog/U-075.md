# U-075 — Escort: the POW as an unarmed seventh friendly, commandable and spectatable

Epic: **Campaign design**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: owner decisions on the campaign design, 2026-10-01 ([design doc](../design/CAMPAIGN.md)).

## Problem / outcome

Campaign mission 1 frees an unarmed POW and brings him home with the squad. He is treated as a member who can be commanded (go / stay / follow) and spectated, but never controlled by a player, and the extraction needs all seven alive.

## Scope

The ADR-001 addendum is written (2026-10-01, Q1 accepted as proposed): the squad is always six; the POW is a **non-slot, non-playable escorted character**. Proposal: a new entity kind (not a slot, so the roster, picker, scoreboard and the six-slot machinery do not change) with health, no weapon and no fire; he follows the squad lead by default and obeys the existing move / hold / regroup orders as go / stay / follow; any player may order him, as with bots; spectators can watch him; enemies target him like a squad member and his death fails the mission (Q4); he walks on the navmesh at a pace the squad can match and goes down and bleeds out like a soldier (no revive needed in the first version: his death is the failure). A `escort` condition on `reach` (all escorted alive in the area) is the extraction. Rendering uses the existing humanoid in a civilian/prisoner palette until U-080.

## Handoff / evidence

Implemented, in REVIEW (the PR is the evidence).

- A new archetype `pow` (wire index 6 of `ENEMY_IDS`; appending needs no protocol bump) with `friendly: true` in `enemies.json`, so he reuses the enemy entity's movement, navmesh following, hit boxes, damage, replication and checkpoint (position and health). A mission places him with any encounter group's `pow` member. He has no gun in play: his tree `escort.json` never fires.
- Not a slot: `MAX_SLOTS`, the roster, picker, scoreboard and the six-slot machinery are unchanged (`escort.test.ts` asserts six slots with him placed). His wire faction is 3, his own side; the bots do not see, target or count him, `enemiesIn`, the director and kill XP skip him, and the cover system counts him with the squad.
- Enemies see and shoot him like a squad member (`perceive` iterates the slots and him), and his death fails the mission (`soldierDead`, reason `soldier-dead`).
- Orders: a `hold`, `move` or `regroup` to the whole squad addresses him as stay, go to the point, or follow (`escort.ts` leaves; default is follow the nearest living squad member, sprinting to catch up). An individual order to him alone is not possible: the order address names slots 0–5 only.
- `reach` takes `escort: true`: the squad is in the area and every living escorted character is too.
- Spectating: he is replicated to every client (always relevant), so the client has the entity; picking him in the spectate UI is **not** done. Spectate is slot-indexed on the wire (3-bit slot) and the UI is built on the roster, so it needs its own change and an owner call on how to pick him (it will be a follow-up card).
- Rendering: civilian palette `prisoner`, bare hands, until U-080.
- Deviation: he is `downable: false`, so zero health kills him at once, rather than going down and bleeding out; with his death being the failure, a downed state nobody can revive adds nothing in the first version.
- Not claimed: an owner playtest (he keeps pace, the look), and `pnpm sim-run` on a real mission with him (U-065 places him).
