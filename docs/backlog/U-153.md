# U-153 — Squad stance orders: stay low and hit the dirt

Epic: Squad command (Conflict parity). Pillar: P2.
Size: M. Human verdict: none (engineering criteria only).
Status, priority, dependencies and branch/PR live in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: owner, 2026-10-09 — queue the first Conflict parity gaps (G-1 in
[the parity tracker](../design/CONFLICT-PARITY.md), rows CP-09) and interleave
them with Mission 1 construction.

## Problem / outcome

In *Conflict: Desert Storm* one key drops the whole squad prone or stands it up,
and *Desert Storm II* adds "hit the dirt". Sandline's bots choose their own
posture (crouching only behind low cover) and cannot be told to stay low. A
player should be able to set a soldier, a fireteam or the whole squad to
**Auto**, **Crouch** or **Prone**, and the bots should keep that stance.

## Scope

Conflict default (ADR-021), modelled on the existing per-character aggression
setting (U-101) rather than a one-off order:

- A persistent per-character stance setting `auto | crouch | prone`, addressed
  like orders (slot, fireteam, all) from the desktop order wheel. `auto` is
  today's behaviour, unchanged.
- Bots under `crouch` or `prone` keep that stance while holding, moving and
  firing; prone moves at prone speed. They may rise briefly only when the
  controller requires it (vault, stairs a prone body cannot take) or to revive or
  interact, then return to the setting.
- The setting applies to bots only; a human controls their own stance. It
  follows U-101's lifecycle for aggression across join, leave, switch (U-026),
  reconnect and checkpoint retry.
- The squad HUD row shows a non-auto stance.
- New wire message, so `PROTOCOL_VERSION` bumps.

Out of scope: mobile commander buttons (a follow-up card, as U-103 followed
U-101); new animations; enemy stance changes.

Conflict default (ADR-021), settled in delivery: the stances are three more
sectors on the order wheel (eight of 45°; the five orders keep their clockwise
order, the stances take the lower left), so one hold-flick-release drops the
squad as one button did in the series. "Rise to revive or interact" covers the
whole revive or heal (going to the mate too) and a commander's cache use; a
tank-shell dodge (U-079) stays in the held stance, as the card lists no rise for it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] The order wheel sets Auto / Crouch / Prone for one soldier, a fireteam or all.
- [x] Bots set to Prone stay prone while holding, moving and engaging; Crouch
      likewise; each rises only for a controller-required traversal, a revive
      or an interaction, then resumes the setting.
- [x] `auto` leaves existing behaviour and every existing scenario floor unchanged.
- [x] The setting survives join/leave/switch/reconnect/checkpoint retry exactly
      as aggression does, and never changes a human-controlled soldier.
- [x] The squad HUD row shows Crouch or Prone when set.
- [x] `PROTOCOL_VERSION` is bumped with its pinned tests; wire round-trip tested.
