# U-015 — Add friendly hit, downed and death vocal reactions

Epic: **Audio**. Status, priority and dependencies live only in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).
Source: [owner feedback, 2026-09-26](2026-09-26-feedback.md), plus any legacy bug named below.

## Problem / outcome

The owner requests at least a grunt when shot, a cry when downed, and a final sigh when dying, for friendly characters.

## Scope

Play the three distinct nonverbal cue families from actual damage/vitality transitions, spatialized for nearby allies and correctly mixed for the local soldier.

## Start here

These are entry points, not a prescribed edit list; verify current code before changing it.

- `packages/client/src/audio/calloutEvents.ts`
- `packages/client/src/audio/callouts.ts`
- `packages/shared/src/data/audio/callouts.json`
- `packages/client/src/main.ts`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Damage produces a rate-limited grunt; downing produces its cry once; final death produces its sigh once.
- [ ] A lethal/downing hit resolves competing cues without an incoherent stack; revive/retry resets the event lifecycle.
- [ ] Predicted/confirmed state and repeated snapshots cannot duplicate a cue.
- [ ] Friendly and enemy voice banks are distinct; other squad callouts remain audible.

## Verification

Test alive→hurt→downed→revived and alive/downed→dead transitions, duplicate snapshots and retries. Record a listening matrix for local and remote soldiers.

Follow the shared [delivery workflow](../../.claude/commands/next-task.md), including `pnpm verify`. Record commands/results and observed acceptance evidence below. Visual/audio quality and owner decisions are not proven by a passing test.
