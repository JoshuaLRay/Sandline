## Problem / outcome

U-160 closed the road with rock, but the three fights have nothing to fight
from: no cover, no toll office, orchard or depot frontage, and the frontage
fight still has two bare views longer than 90 m. Players should meet each fight
with readable fixed cover and landmarks, and no ordinary hostile firing lane
longer than 90 m.

## Scope

Build all A-C01–12 at their table coordinates, sizes and along/across-road
orientations, re-placing the ones the bend displaced (A-C04, A-C05, A-C07,
A-C08, A-C10 and A-C12, which may belong to U-151's forecourt) and building
A-C06 at U-160's moved MG nest (-22,8,222). Add the toll office shell, four
orchard trunks and closed loading façades on the west side of the west leg;
re-place frontage riflemen AF2 and AF3 on the walking surface. Cut the two
bare-surface views over 90 m from the frontage fight with high cover or rock on
shoulders and aprons (from A4 116.2 m toward A6, from A5 128.9 m down the west
leg; both in `artifacts/qalat-road-supports/construction.json`). Prove no
ordinary hostile lane over 90 m with the cover in place, 4–8 m free regroup
spaces behind cover, ≥4 m infantry passages and a clear central 12 m vehicle
corridor; clip apron shoulders around cover, never move cover into the
carriageway. Keep U-160's fight separation, ridge-bay rays and edge closure.
Update the §5.2/§9.1 tables (§15). No X construction, campaign activation, AI
tuning, caches or checkpoints.
