# Known bugs

Running list of reported gameplay bugs that aren't yet tracked as `PLAN.md`
tasks. Not a task queue — see `TASKS.md` for that. When a bug here gets
scoped into a task, note the task ID in its row and leave the row (don't
delete) until the task is DONE.

Status: **OPEN** (not investigated/fixed) · **IN PROGRESS** · **FIXED**

| B-06 | Input | Holding Ctrl to crouch, then pressing another key, fires a browser shortcut (find, bookmark, print, etc.) instead of reaching the game — only Space was preventDefault'd. preventDefault now covers every key while the mouse is captured, but Ctrl+W (crouch-walk forward) is one of the browser's *reserved* combos that no amount of preventDefault can stop, so crouch was also moved off Ctrl entirely: it's now a toggle on C. | B-09 | Weapons/stance | Found during T-2.42: a CROUCHED soldier's shots still trace from standing eye height (1.55 m, above the 1.2 m crouch hit volume), so crouching behind low cover shoots over it with the body hidden. Throws (grenade/rocket origin, client arc and server) also leave from standing eye height in every stance, prone included. T-2.42 lowered only the prone gun trace; the fix is the same shape (`eyePosition`'s stance input plus a `crouchEyeHeight` on the muzzle rig). | OPEN | — |
| B-10 | Input/camera | Turning the view with the mouse felt slightly choppy while movement was smooth — each step moved "a little too much". The camera read yaw and pitch rounded to wire precision (1/1024 turn, 0.35°, about two pixels at the default sensitivity), so the view stood still and then jumped a whole step. The camera now takes the unrounded mouse angles (`LocalInput.viewYaw`, `fineSin` in `cameraSolve.ts`); the wire and the simulation still get whole units. | FIXED | — |
| B-11 | AI/balance | Found during T-3.35: six friendly bots lose most firefights on the grey-box mission. Over `pnpm sim-run --scenario mission --seeds 20` they complete 30% of runs at the one-human budget and 15% at six, usually wiped inside 30–80 s. In a probe, the bots fired about a third as many rounds as the enemies (98 against 284 in one run): of the ticks a bot wanted to fire and had a line, 56% were held because a squadmate's capsule, grown by `bot.friendlyMarginM`, was in the line. Downed squadmates count as in the line; the dead do not. Spreading a fireteam's move points and taking cover on contact (tried in the scenario's leader) did not lift completion. Likely levers: `friendlyMarginM`, a downed friend's capsule (lying, not standing), and move orders that sprint through fire. T-3.35's completion floors sit just under this baseline, so they catch it getting worse; they do not say it is good. **Worse since B-15** (pose-matched hitboxes): over 10 seeds main completes 40% / 10% and B-15 0% / 0%. Bisected to the squad's lying bodies — a downed bot used to be a standing capsule that soaked the enemy's fire (and the enemy spent rounds finishing it), and a dead one stayed in the shot history as an upright shield; now both lie where they are drawn and the enemy's fire goes to the bots still standing. `friendlyMarginM` 0.3 or 0.2 does not change it. The floors are asserted only at 10+ seeds, so CI (3 seeds) is green; the mission needs rebalancing on its own terms. | OPEN | — |
| B-12 | Death | A dead soldier lay in the downed pose, so a body and a squadmate waiting on a revive looked alike. Dead is now its own pose: face down and flat, both arms stretched above the head on the ground, no weapon (`humanoidSoldier.ts`, and the grey box's). | FIXED | — |
| B-13 | Death/netcode | When killed, the local player could still run around (but not shoot): the client predicted movement for a dead player because only `downed` was held still, and the local pose only handled downed. Prediction now holds a dead soldier as the server does, and the local body lies in the death pose; the banner reads KILLED with the respawn count. | FIXED | — |
| B-14 | Respawn | A respawn could be shot straight back down on the spawn point. A respawned soldier now takes no damage for `respawnImmunitySeconds` (2 s, `data/damage.json`). | FIXED | — |
| B-15 | Hitboxes | The server shot every stance as an upright capsule, so a downed or prone body was hit in the air above it and missed where it was drawn. Crouched, prone, downed and dead are now capsules per leg, torso, head and arm, fitted to the rig's skin in that pose and turned with the facing (`lagComp.ts` `POSED_BODIES`); standing keeps its capsule plus the arms on the rifle. The rig's crouch is deepened to the gameplay crouch's 1.2 m, so a body crouched in cover is hidden where the cover search says. Side effect on the mission's balance: see B-11. A test holds each pose's server body to the skin (≥ 95% of vertices inside, none more than 15 cm out). | FIXED | — |
| B-16 | Weapons/ADS | In first-person ADS the weapon art covered the aim: carry handles, sight blocks and lenses sat across the sight line, and the DMR's scope tube filled the view. The squad's weapons now have open rear apertures and front posts whose tips sit exactly on the screen's centre, with the crosshair hidden while aimed in first person; the marksman's scope is an overlay (4× view, a duplex reticle, slower look) with the rifle hidden once shouldered. | FIXED | — |
| B-17 | Input/netcode | Rarely, within ~20 s of joining a host, the player seemed to teleport and face another way, once. Most likely cause: Chromium reports one bogus pointer-lock `mousemove` of hundreds of pixels right after the lock is taken, fullscreen changes or the window resizes — and clicking into the canvas does the first two (B-06); at the default sensitivity that is a quarter turn or more in a frame, and in third person the camera swings round with it. `mouseGuard.ts` drops such jumps (over 120 px in the 250 ms after lock, fullscreen or resize; over 400 px out of stillness). The other way to get the same picture is a dropped socket: the client reconnects silently and the host seats it in a new slot, in that bot's body (ADR-011). That now shows a banner, so a repeat says which it was, and since T-4.18 a reconnect within 60 s takes back your own soldier. | FIXED | T-4.18 |
| B-18 | Weapons/netcode | Holding fire (carbine, magazine tuned to 100) against a target right in front, about 30% of shots drew a tracer but never registered, in clumps. The server judged fire cadence at the 30 Hz step a Fire arrived on (a 100 ms gap often landed as 67 ms, under the 83 ms interval, and was refused); it started its reload only on the first refused pull after emptying, so it refused a whole reload's worth of shots after each magazine; and the R key and the weapon panel's magazine/rpm edits never reached the server. Fixed with cadence slack scheduled from the due time, reload on the emptying round, `reload` on Equip (protocol 30) and the panel's rows handed to the in-page session. A remote host still fires its own data file's rows. | FIXED | — |
| B-19 | AI/orders | A friendly bot stood on the range's slab beside the block and would not come down. Reproducible only with a move order aimed at the block's top near an edge: the point passed the 1 m reachability check (`ORDER_REACH_M`) but a bot counts as there only within 0.4 m (`THERE_M`), so it parked 0.65–0.8 m away with the order never done. An order's point in that gap is now moved onto the navmesh when the order is given (`Session.onMesh`). Following in formation never stranded a bot there (0 of 40 fuzzed runs; bots placed at 64 points on the slab and block all came down). | FIXED | — |
| B-20 | AI/locomotion | Friendly bots following in formation look jittery. Measured per bot per second while the lead walks: stop–go 0.29 in the open and 0.74 round the slab (the formation spot moves ~0.4 m per think, under the 0.5 m re-plan threshold in `follow.json`, so a bot reaches its stale path end, stands, then restarts at full speed — `followPath.ts` gives zero input on arrival); facing snaps over 25° in one tick 0.6–1.6 (`followPath.ts` sets yaw straight to the next corner; 125° seen in a tick); walk/sprint flips 0.5–1.25 (`formation.ts` switches pace at a single 2.5 m threshold with no hysteresis). A prototype (turn rate cap, eased arrival, re-plan on a stale end, sprint hysteresis — formation-following only) took stops to 0.05/0.21, snaps to 0 and flips to 0.34/0.84, but the `squad` scenario got worse over 10 seeds (bots downed 11 → 23, kills 100% → 97%) though still inside its floors. Not landed; a render-only alternative is to smooth remote facing in `remoteSoldiers.ts`. The default in-page session's sparring partner moves abruptly by design (`SparringPartner.ts`). | OPEN | — |
