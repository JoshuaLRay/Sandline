| B-11 | AI/balance | Found during T-3.35: six friendly bots lose most firefights on the grey-box mission. Over `pnpm sim-run --scenario mission --seeds 20` they complete 30% of runs at the one-human budget and 15% at six, usually wiped inside 30–80 s. In a probe, the bots fired about a third as many rounds as the enemies (98 against 284 in one run): of the ticks a bot wanted to fire and had a line, 56% were held because a squadmate's capsule, grown by `bot.friendlyMarginM`, was in the line. Downed squadmates count as in the line; the dead do not. Spreading a fireteam's move points and taking cover on contact (tried in the scenario's leader) did not lift completion. Likely levers: `friendlyMarginM`, a downed friend's capsule (lying, not standing), and move orders that sprint through fire. T-3.35's completion floors sit just under this baseline, so they catch it getting worse; they do not say it is good. **Worse since B-15** (pose-matched hitboxes): over 10 seeds main completes 40% / 10% and B-15 0% / 0%. Bisected to the squad's lying bodies — a downed bot used to be a standing capsule that soaked the enemy's fire (and the enemy spent rounds finishing it), and a dead one stayed in the shot history as an upright shield; now both lie where they are drawn and the enemy's fire goes to the bots still standing. `friendlyMarginM` 0.3 or 0.2 does not change it. The floors are asserted only at 10+ seeds, so CI (3 seeds) is green; the mission needs rebalancing on its own terms. **2026-09-26: most of the 0% was the scenario's leader, not the bots.** It walked both fireteams to the compound's centre and held there, so every fight was at a median 7 m against a garrison in cover (bots hit 21% of rounds, enemies 31%). A leader that clears first — every bot attacks the nearest living enemy within `clearWithinM` (15 m) of the area once the approach is walked, and holds only when none is left, as a human lead would — completes 45% at one human and 25% at six over 20 seeds (from 0% / 0%), above T-3.35's baseline. Tried and rejected on the way: enemies never finishing a downed soldier (no change), halving enemy damage (still 0% with the walk-in), trebling enemy aim cones (≤ 20%). Still open: the bots themselves lose close fights, and a human who leads them into the garrison will too. | OPEN | — |

| B-17 | Input/netcode | Rarely, within ~20 s of joining a host, the player seemed to teleport and face another way, once. Most likely cause: Chromium reports one bogus pointer-lock `mousemove` of hundreds of pixels right after the lock is taken, fullscreen changes or the window resizes — and clicking into the canvas does the first two (B-06); at the default sensitivity that is a quarter turn or more in a frame, and in third person the camera swings round with it. `mouseGuard.ts` drops such jumps (over 120 px in the 250 ms after lock, fullscreen or resize; over 400 px out of stillness). The other way to get the same picture is a dropped socket: the client reconnects silently and the host seats it in a new slot, in that bot's body (ADR-011). That now shows a banner, so a repeat says which it was, and since T-4.18 a reconnect within 60 s takes back your own soldier. | FIXED | T-4.18 |
