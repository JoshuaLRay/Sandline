## Before you start

- `H` toggles the HUD, `G` the netgraph. The weapon panel is on the right;
  `1`–`4` select carbine, marksman, breacher, sidearm. `R` reloads, `T`
  resets the counters.
- Every number below is on a slider. Recoil: kick, drift, cap, recovery,
  aimed multiplier. Shake: per-weapon metres and roll degrees on the weapon
  panel, and one global "Shake" scale on the camera panel (0 is off). The
  panel's "Paste into data/weapons.json" block prints the whole weapon,
  including these; paste it into the Tuning section as you go.
- Fire at the doorway wall (west of spawn, the gap between the two long
  walls) for impacts. Fire at the ground on purpose once: it leaves no
  mark, by design (`m1.5.md` explains why the server has no ground box);
  note whether the absence reads as wrong.

## The questions, one weapon at a time

Answer each for every weapon, hip and aimed. A line each is enough; "fine"
is a valid answer, "did not try" is a required one.

### 1. Recoil reads as a pattern to learn (T-2.08)

Hold a full magazine on the wall and watch the marks. Do it twice.

- Does the second magazine walk the same path as the first?
- Pull down against the climb through a burst. When it stops, is the view
  where you put it, or has it drifted?
- Aimed: is the reduced kick still felt, or does aiming feel like turning
  recoil off?
- Which weapon's recoil is wrong, and which way? (Numbers in Tuning.)

### 2. Shake is felt, not aimed with (T-2.09)

Camera panel "Shake" at 1, then at 0, then back.

- With shake on, does the reticle stay put while the picture jolts? Or
  does it feel like the gun is shaking your aim?
- Is a long burst heavier than a single shot without becoming a blur?

### 3. Flash and shells (T-2.10)

- Does the flash sit at the muzzle in third person, and at the hip and at
  centre when aimed in first person? Does it light the wall you are next to?
- Do shells leave to the right and land near your feet? Are they visible at
  all in third person, or too small?
- Held trigger: does the HUD `fx` line stay under its caps, and go back to
  zero a few seconds after you release?

### 4. Impacts (T-2.11)

- From spawn, fire at the doorway wall. Does a mark appear where the tracer
  ended, every shot?
- On the host with a real link: how long after the tracer does the mark
  land? Does the delay read as lag, or as the round arriving? (It is one
  round trip; that is the honest order and this is the question of whether
  honest is acceptable.)
- Fire at the ground: no mark. Does that read wrong?
- Do sparks and mark together make the wall feel solid?

### 5. Against another person

Needs a second human on the host.

- When you hit them, do the hit marker, their flinch and the sound of
  nothing (there is no audio yet) add up to "that landed"?
- When they hit you: do you know you were hit, from the view alone?
- Does the flinch ever fire when you did NOT hit, or fail to when you did?
  (A mismatch here is a bug, not a feel note; say which.)
