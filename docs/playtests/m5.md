## What this is judging

PLAN.md §4 and §7.12: *"One finished 10-minute mission at §4.1 scope, 6
slots, demo-able."* §4.1's scope: **two classes** (Team Leader, Marksman),
**two enemy types** (rifleman, MG), **a ~25-piece kit**, **the mounted MG**,
six slots with bot backfill. The M5 epic adds: full audio pass, baked
lighting, onboarding, performance to §2.2 on target hardware. The thing the
slice exists to prove is **the netcode and the squad architecture**, not
breadth.

The questions:

1. Is the mission **finished** — does it start, play through and end the way
   a finished game's mission does, with nothing that reads as a test build?
2. Is it **about ten minutes**, and are they ten good minutes?
3. Does the **squad architecture** show — six slots, bots that fill, fight,
   follow and take orders, and people who drop in and out?
4. Is it **demo-able** — could you hand the link to someone who has never
   seen it and let them play without standing over them?

## Before you start

- **The page is the game.** Since T-5.07 the QA readouts and tuning panels
  are out of sight; H brings them back (and `?qa` opens with them). Play the
  gate without them unless a section asks.
- **What a new player gets** (T-5.03): a briefing when the mission starts
  (the objectives, the two routes, the squad, the first keys; *Move out*,
  Enter or Space), then one-line hints on the HUD the first time each thing
  matters. Hints are remembered per browser: for the newcomer, use a fresh
  browser profile, or Settings → *Show every hint again*.
- **Known, and not this gate's to fix — but say how much each costs the demo:**
  - **The voices are chirps** until the voice script is recorded
    (`docs/audio/voice-script.md`, T-2.48). Callouts play a radio chirp.
  - **Lighting is T-4.12's fallback** (hemisphere, one shadowed sun, baked
    vertex occlusion), not lightmaps — T-5.05 is the owner's decision.
  - **The mission (T-5.02)** is five objectives — the west-lane patrol, the
    east-lane position, take the compound and hold it a minute, defend it
    5 min 30 s, fall back to the start line past an ambush — with respawns
    and a checkpoint at each. Headless, six bots finish it about 70 % of the
    time in a median 8.5–9.5 minutes; a person plays slower.
  - **The bots lose close fights** (B-11, T-5.06): walked into the garrison
    they die. With cover under fire (T-5.06) and a lead who clears before
    holding, the headless scenario completes about 60 % alone and 20 % at six.
  - **B-18:** pressing T on a host resets the page's grenade count to the
    data's, not the class's.

## The questions

A line each is enough; "fine" is a valid answer, "did not try" is a
required one.

### 1. From the link to the first shot

Open the deployed site in a fresh browser profile.

- What does a stranger see first, and is it clear what to press?
- Lobby → room → ready → briefing: anything confusing, slow or ugly?
- The briefing: does it say what the mission is, and is it read or skipped?
- Time from opening the link to controlling a soldier: ______ s.
- The first hints (move, fire, crouch, the order wheel): helpful, or noise?

### 2. The mission, alone with five bots

Play it through to the end at least twice, once as Team Leader and once as
Marksman.

| run | class | length | outcome | where it was lost, or what made it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | | | | |

- **Length:** about ten minutes? Where did the time go?
- **Shape:** does it rise and fall — approach, contact, the compound, the
  counterattack — or is it one long fight?
- **The two routes:** did you use the overwatch/assault split? Did it matter?
- **The mounted MG:** did you find it, man it, and did it matter?
- **Checkpoints:** when you lost, did you restart from the right place (P)?
- **The end:** is it clear you won or lost, and what the after-action says?

### 3. The squad

- **Backfill:** five bots from the start — do they read as a squad?
- **Orders:** move, attack, hold, regroup, revive through the wheel (Q). Did
  each do what you meant? Did "can't get there" ever come up, and was it
  right?
- **Fighting:** do the bots take cover when shot at (T-5.06)? Do they get
  themselves killed in ways that feel stupid? Where?
- **Revives:** did a bot pick you up, and you them?
- **Callouts:** with chirps only, did the timing still tell you anything?

### 4. With people

At least one other person in a room on the host; if you can, more.

- Drop-in: someone joins mid-mission and takes a bot's slot. Seamless?
- Drop-out: someone leaves; does a bot take their place without a hitch?
- Ordering each other's bots, and each other: does the class split (Team
  Leader orders the squad, Marksman its fireteam) show?
- Anything that looked different on the two screens?

### 6. Showing it

Give the link to someone who has not seen it. Say nothing unless they ask.

- How long before they were playing? What did they ask?
- Did they understand the squad — that the others are theirs to command?
- What did they say at the end, unprompted?
- Would you send this link to someone you wanted to impress?
