## What this is judging

T-2.30 through T-2.33 added thrown and fired projectiles: a frag grenade
and a rocket, flown by one pure stepper over the same box list on the
server and in the page's preview (ADR-005 addendum, 2026-09-21), replicated
as entities that spawn, fly and despawn, thrown from the page with a
predicted ghost that hands over to the server's twin, and going off as a
pooled flash, fireball, debris and scorch, with a camera jolt scaled by the
same function that scores the damage. The server throws it; where it is,
when it goes off and who it hurts are the server's. The gate asks (PLAN.md
§7.6):

1. Does the **arc read where it is going**?
2. Does the grenade **land where the line said**?
3. Does a **bounce off a crate go where a bounce should**?
4. Does a **blast behind cover feel weaker** than one in the open?
5. Is the **rocket worth the two rounds it carries**?

And: **tune the numbers in `projectiles.json` while the feel is in hand.**

## Before you start

- **Add `?qa` to every URL in this sheet** (T-5.07): since the demo face, the QA layer — the readout, the tuning panels and the netgraph — is out of sight without it (H also brings it back).
- **Controls** (B-08 made the pouch items weapons). `5` equips the
  grenade, `6` the rocket; `1`–`4` go back to a gun. **Grenade:** hold
  `LMB` to see the arc, release to throw. **Rocket:** `RMB` (aim) shows its
  arc, `LMB` fires on the press. **`G`** is a quick throw of the selected
  pouch item whatever is in hand: hold to aim, release to throw. The last
  one gone puts the gun back in your hands. `R` does nothing with a pouch
  item in hand. `H` shows the QA readouts and tuning panels (they start
  hidden), `N` folds the netgraph, `T` resets the readouts.
- **The HUD lines this sheet reads** (press `H`): the pouch, `>5 M2 Frag x3
  6 RL-2 Rocket x2`, and under it the state (`ready in 0.6s`, `empty`) and
  `n in flight (predicted)`; after a blast, `last blast M2 Frag  <total
  dmg> dmg on <targets>`; the `fx` line's `blasts n/4`. Your health is on
  the player HUD; a blast that caught you also shows its direction there.
- **What you carry.** In the page, the data's full pouch: 3 frags, 2
  rockets, and every gun. **On the host your class decides**
  (`data/classes.json`): Team Leader 2 frags and **1** rocket; Marksman 1
  frag and no rocket. So the gate's "two rounds" can only be spent as two
  in the page; on the host, both of you should pick **Team Leader**. The
  server refills the pouch **on a respawn only**.
- **The numbers you are judging** (defaults; all data, in
  `packages/shared/src/data/projectiles.json`):

  | | M2 Frag | RL-2 Rocket |
  |---|---|---|
  | launch speed, loft | 20 m/s, 10° above the aim | 45 m/s, 0° |
  | gravity, air drag | 9.81, 0.1 /s | 3.2, none |
  | bounce, bounce friction, roll drag | 0.2, 0.7, 5 /s | none: goes off on impact |
  | fuse, max life | 3.5 s, 8 s | on impact, 4 s |
  | blast radius | 6.5 m | 5.5 m |
  | blast damage | 130 inside 0.65 m, falling linearly to ×0.12 (about 16) at the edge | 170 inside 0.55 m, to ×0.15 (about 26) |
  | fully behind cover | ×0.25 | ×0.2 |
  | carried, cooldown | 3, 1 s | 2, 1.8 s |

  **How far that is**, on open ground from a standing eye (the projectile
  panel prints the same numbers live): a level grenade first lands at
  18.9 m and goes off at 21 m after 3.5 s; 30° up lands at 35.6 m, goes off
  at 37.8 m; 45° up lands at 32.1 m. A level rocket meets the ground at
  about 42 m, a second after firing; any rocket aimed above level flies its
  4 s life and goes off in the air, over 120 m out.
  **How cover counts:** three probes up the body at 10%, 50% and 90% of its
  height (1.8 m standing, 1.2 m crouched, 0.8 m prone); each one the scenery
  blocks takes a third off, down to the cover floor — for the frag ×1,
  ×0.75, ×0.5, ×0.25. So standing behind the 1 m low wall a grenade on the
  far side still sees your head; prone behind it, nothing. For scale:
  health is 100 (Marksman 90); a frag at your feet downs you; one about 3 m
  away in the open does roughly 80; the same fully behind cover about 20.
  **The camera jolt** is 7.5 cm and 1.4° of roll at most, times the share of
  the blast's damage that reached you, so it is zero beyond the radius and
  cut by exactly the cover that cut the damage (`cameraShake.ts`).

## The questions

A line each is enough; "fine" is a valid answer, "did not try" is a
required one.

### 1. The arc (T-2.30, T-2.32)

In the page. Equip the grenade (`5`), hold `LMB`, and sweep the aim from
the ground at your feet to high up, then across the range. Then the same
with `G` while holding a gun, and the rocket (`6`, hold `RMB`).

- **Does the arc read where it is going?** From where it leaves you (the
  eye, per B-09, not the hand) to the ring where it goes off: can you tell
  at a glance how far, and where it will come to rest after the roll?
- The arc through the bounce and the skid after landing: readable, or a
  scribble? Does the ring sit where the grenade will actually stop?
- Does the arc bend correctly round cover — against the low wall, a crate,
  the long wall, the doorway?
- Rocket: does its sagging line tell you where it will hit?
- Is holding `LMB` to aim and releasing to throw natural? Is `G` useful?

### 2. Does it land where the line said? (T-2.30, T-2.31, T-2.32)

Ten throws in the page at marked spots (a distance post, the foot of a
crate, the low wall's end), then the same ten on the host.

- **Does the grenade land where the line said** — first touch, and where
  it goes off? Out of ten, how many were where the ring was? ______ / 10
  in the page, ______ / 10 on the host.
- The throw leaves the hand on release, not a round trip later? Is there
  ever **two** grenades on screen (ghost and server's twin), or a jump
  when one hands over to the other?
- Does the blast go off where the grenade is — never before it has landed,
  never somewhere it isn't?
- On the host, the other person watches you throw: does the grenade they
  see follow the arc you were shown and go off where it came to rest?
- Level throw distance (18.9 m first landing) and the 3.5 s fuse: too
  far, too short, too floaty, too heavy (B-03 and B-07 went both ways)?

### 3. Bounces (T-2.30)

Throw into crate c's face from about 6 m, then at its top edge, then
along the long wall at a shallow angle, then flat along the floor.

- **Does a bounce off a crate go where a bounce should** — back off the
  face it hit, losing most of its speed (bounce 0.2)?
- Off the top edge, off a corner: anything that goes through, sticks, or
  flies off in a direction that surprises you?
- A flat, hard throw: does it skid on after landing rather than stopping
  dead? Too far, too short (roll drag 5 /s)?
- Dropped at your feet: does it come to rest and then sit perfectly still?

### 4. Cover (T-2.30, T-2.33)

On the host, two of you. One stands in the open; the other throws a
grenade to land about 3 m from them. Then the same distance, but the
target behind the low wall standing, then crouched, then prone, with the
grenade on the far side. Then the long wall. Read `last blast` each time.

| target | in the open | low wall, standing | low wall, crouched | low wall, prone | long wall |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| damage (`last blast`) | | | | | |
| felt (jolt, flash) | | | | | |

- **Does a blast behind cover feel weaker than one in the open?** To the
  one in cover, and to the one watching?
- Is the jolt's size right for the damage, and does "no jolt past 6.5 m"
  feel right, or odd, for a grenade going off 8 m away?
- Does the damage indicator's direction point at the blast?
- Does the target's body take the blast as a hit away from it?
- Your own grenade: stand too close, once. Does the self-damage feel fair?

### 5. The rocket (T-2.30, T-2.31)

Both as Team Leader on the host (1 rocket each), and in the page (2).

- Fire one at the other person at 15 m and at 30 m. Does it go off **on
  them**, not behind them? At the wall beside them, what did they take?
- Does the flight read — the stub leaving the launcher, its sag, where it
  hit?
- Does the other person visibly hold a launcher (and a grenade) when they
  have one in hand?
- **Is the rocket worth the two rounds it carries** — against a person
  behind the low wall, against someone in the open? Would one be enough
  (as the Team Leader class has it), or three?

### 6. The blast (T-2.33)

From 5 m, 20 m, 40 m and from behind the long wall.

- Flash, fireball, debris and scorch: do they read as a grenade going off,
  and **from across the range**?
- Does the scorch sit on the ground where it went off (and none if it
  went off in the air)?
- At a low frame rate (resize the window big, or a weak machine): does the
  blast look the same, just choppier?
- On a conditioned host (`LINK_LATENCY_MS=100 LINK_JITTER_MS=20
  LINK_LOSS=0.05 pnpm host`; latency counts each way; the page's link
  panel is greyed against a host): does a grenade ever jump, stall, vanish,
  or go off early or late against where it is drawn?
