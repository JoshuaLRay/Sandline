## What this is judging

T-3.25 through T-3.29 made the five bots a squad: they follow their
fireteam's lead in formation (T-3.25), fight beside you with the enemy's
own perception, fire and cover, and revive (T-3.26), take orders and marks
over the wire from any human (T-3.27, ADR-001), carry them out and report
done, failed or replaced (T-3.28), and are ordered from a wheel on `Q` and a
mark on `F` (T-3.29). Headless, `pnpm sim-run --scenario squad` holds them
to kills with no friendly hits; this gate is whether it *plays* right. The
questions the gate asks (PLAN.md T-3.30):

1. Do the bots feel like **a squad or like followers**?
2. Does an order **get done the way it was meant**?
3. Does the wheel **stay out of the way in a firefight**?
4. Do bots **revive you when it matters**?
5. Does sharing bots between two humans cause **confusion about who is in
   charge**?

## Before you start

- **Controls.** `WASD`, `Shift` sprint, `C` crouch, `Z` prone, `RMB` aim,
  `LMB` fire, `R` reload, `1`–`4` guns, `5`–`6` the pouch, `E` held
  revives. `B` the AI debug overlay, `N` the netgraph, `H` the HUD, `Tab`
  the scoreboard, `T` resets the counters.
- **The wheel.** Hold `Q`: the view stops turning and the mouse picks a
  sector, clockwise from the top — **move** (up), **attack** (up-right),
  **hold** (down-right), **regroup** (down-left), **revive** (up-left).
  Release inside the 24 px deadzone and nothing is sent; the centre reads
  `<order> → <who>` or `cancel → <who>`. While `Q` is held, number keys
  choose who hears it instead of switching weapons: `1`–`6` a slot, `7`
  fireteam 1 (slots 1–3), `8` fireteam 2 (slots 4–6), `0` everyone — the
  default each time the wheel opens. The order goes to the point under the
  crosshair when you opened it. **Attack** is sent only with a living enemy
  under the crosshair and **revive** only with a downed squadmate; otherwise
  nothing goes. Tap `F` to mark the enemy under the crosshair, or the point.
- **What you see.** Orders and marks are drawn from what the host
  broadcasts, not from what you sent: a ring and pole labelled `4 · move`
  (the slot, 1-based, and the order), a line from the bot to it, and
  `mark · 1` (who marked). A bot acknowledges by voice (`roger`, `holding
  here`, `on it`) and says "can't get there" on a failed order. With `B`,
  a bot's label shows its running branch: `follow`, `orderMove`,
  `orderAttack`, `orderHold`, `orderRegroup`, `orderRevive`, `revive`,
  `takeCover`, `peek`. An order to a human's slot — yours included — is
  dropped by the host and never drawn; that is ADR-001, not a bug.
- **Who follows whom.** Fireteam 1 is slots 1–3 in a wedge, fireteam 2
  slots 4–6 in file. A fireteam follows its first human, else the squad's
  first human, else slot 1's bot. Joiners take the lowest free slot, so two
  people are slots 1 and 2 — **both in fireteam 1**, and fireteam 2 follows
  slot 1. The HUD's top line says `joined - slot n of 6`.
- **The numbers you are judging** (defaults; data unless marked code):

## The questions

A line each is enough; "fine" is a valid answer, "did not try" is a
required one.

### 1. One person, five bots, in the page (T-3.25, T-3.26)

`pnpm --filter @sandline/client dev`, open `localhost:5173/?squad&enemies`,
**Practise here**. You are slot 1 and lead both fireteams. One rifleman
stands up-range at about 46 m and fights; the two patrolling riflemen are a
QA fixture that never shoots back. `?squad&suppress` instead gives a
rifleman firing at slot 2's soldier, for watching bots revive each other.

- Walk, sprint, turn a corner round the walls at z = 4, stop. Does the
  wedge and the file behind it read as a formation, or a queue? Do they
  close up when you stop, and catch up after a corner without cutting
  through you?
- Do they ever stand where you want to go, or walk into your line of fire?
- First contact with the rifleman: do they take cover near you, fire, and
  kill it, or crowd, freeze, or leave you behind?
- **Squad or followers?** Did they ever do something useful you did not
  ask for?

### 2. The wheel (T-3.29)

Before any fight: open and release each sector, then each addressee.

- Can you pick the sector you mean without looking at it? Does a quick
  flick — `Q` down, mouse, `Q` up — ever get lost or go to the wrong one?
- Is the aim point the one you meant (the order's ring lands where the
  crosshair was)?
- The labels, lines and voice: can you tell which bot has which order, from
  where you stand, without `B`?
- **In a firefight:** open the wheel under fire. The view freezes while it
  is open. Is that tolerable for the second it takes? Did you ever open it
  by accident, or switch weapon when you meant to address a bot?

### 3. Each order, done the way it was meant (T-3.28)

One order at a time to one bot, then to a fireteam, then to everyone.

| | move | attack | hold | regroup | revive | mark (`F`) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| did what you meant? | | | | | | |
| how you knew it was done | | | | | | |

- **Move** to a point by cover: does it end up *in* cover, facing the
  threat? If you find a point it cannot reach (a path must end within 1 m
  of it), order a move there: does it say "can't get there" at once, and
  the ring go, rather than standing silently?
- **Attack** a rifleman behind a wall: does it go round to a firing
  position, and stop when the target is down?
- **Hold** under fire: does it stay put and fire back, and still hold
  afterwards?
- **Regroup** from far off: does it come back into formation?
- **Mark** the far enemy while a nearer one is in view: do bots switch to
  the marked one? Is 20 s about right?
- An order over an order: is the new one what stands?

### 4. Revive when it matters (T-3.26)

Get yourself downed by the rifleman in the open, then behind cover, then
with bots under fire nearby.

- Did a bot come? Time from down to up: ______ s. Would a human have been
  faster, or taken the same risk?
- When it did not come: was it pinned (≥ 0.6 suppression), too far
  (> 30 m), or did it just not? (`B` shows `downedMate`/`revive` or not.)
- Did a bot ever die walking into fire to reach you? Was that the right
  call?
- A revive order to a bot other than the one already going: sensible?

### 5. Two people sharing the bots, over the host (T-3.27)

`HOST_AI=1 AI_DEBUG=1 pnpm host`; one person **Host a room** with **Map**
set to *Grey box — mission layout fixture* (enemies to fight; the range
has none on a host), the other **Join** with the code. Optional, to put
the second person in fireteam 2: after the first joins,
`pnpm bot --url ws://localhost:8080 --room <code> --count 2 --ticks 1800`
fills slots 2–3 for a minute; the second person joins into slot 4, and
the stand-ins leave.

- Who does each bot follow? Could both of you tell, without `B`?
- Both order the same bot, a second apart. Do both screens show the later
  order? Did the one overruled understand why?
- One person moves a fireteam while the other is leading it: does anyone
  lose track of who is in charge? Would you want a rule the game does not
  have (the lead's orders stand, only your fireteam's bots)?
- Marks from both people at once (3 each): readable, or clutter?
- A person joining takes a bot's slot and its order lapses: noticed?
