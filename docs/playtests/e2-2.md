## Before you start

- `WASD` move, `Shift` sprint, `Ctrl` or `C` crouch, `Space` jump, `V`
  swaps shoulder (and exits first person), `RMB` aims into first person,
  `E` held revives. `H` hides the HUD, `G` the netgraph, `T` resets the
  counters, including the pose-step peak described below.
- Three HUD lines are this sheet's instruments. `locomotion <state>
  <direction> <speed%> rig skinned` is what the classifier decided from
  the rendered motion — if it disagrees with what you pressed, that is a
  bug, not a feel note. The stance line reads `grounded`, `airborne y …`
  or `vaulting <ms>`. `pose step <rad> peak <rad>` is the largest rotation
  any joint made in the last frame and the largest since `T`; a snap is a
  radian in a frame, a walking knee is a few tenths at 60 fps, less at
  higher frame rates. Press `T`, do a thing, read the peak.
- **The soldier is the skinned model.** Add `?greybox` to the URL for the
  grey-box fixture; it is judged separately in section 8 and only for
  still working.
- **The range, from spawn.** You spawn on the line at z = −6 facing north
  (+Z); the five other slots stand in a row beside you. The 1 m **low
  wall** is ahead-left of spawn (x −12..−6, z −1): the vault target. The
  **crates** are 1.2 m cubes, two at the far left (x −9 and −10.4, z 9) and
  one at the right (x 7, z −3): vaultable, and tall enough to hide a
  crouched soldier. The **slab and block** at the right (x 9, z 8) are a
  0.4 m step with a 0.8 m block on top of it. The long walls at z = 4 are
  2.4 m and cannot be vaulted; the doorway through the west one is at
  x −9..−7.8. The two rails at spawn are 5 cm and are stepped over.
  There are no ceilings on the range, so "crouch under something" cannot
  be judged here; say so in the verdict.
- **Sliders.** The movement panel has walk, sprint, crouch and crawl speeds,
  jump impulse, gravity, and the vault's seconds, distance and maximum
  height, with a paste-back block underneath. On the in-page session the
  server and the predictor share the config, so a slider moves both; on
  the host only your prediction moves, so tune on the in-page session and
  confirm on the host. The animation constants (stride rate and length,
  knee bend, hip bob, chest twist and lean, the vault's entry, exit and
  landing windows) are named constants at the top of
  `packages/client/src/character/locomotionPose.ts`, not sliders: changing
  one is an edit and a reload. Record any change in Tuning.

## The questions

A line each is enough; "fine" is a valid answer, "did not try" is a
required one.

### 1. The figure at rest (T-2.22)

Stand still in third person. Walk round the row of bots at spawn to see a
soldier from every side.

- Does it read as a soldier at rest: rifle at the right shoulder, both hands
  on it, weight on both feet?
- Is anything visibly wrong with the body — a joint gap, a limb through the
  torso, a hand off the weapon, the rifle pointing somewhere odd?
- Do the local and remote soldiers look like the same figure, apart from
  the uniform tint?
- From behind, over the shoulder, does your own soldier block anything you
  need to see? (The camera is signed off; this is only whether the new
  body changed the answer.)

### 2. Walk and sprint, eight ways (T-2.17, T-2.18, T-2.19)

Press `T`. Walk each of the eight directions for a few seconds, then sprint
each. Read the `locomotion` line as you go and the `peak` at the end.

| | forward | fwd-right | right | back-right | back | back-left | left | fwd-left |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| walk: legs follow the direction? | | | | | | | | |
| sprint: reads faster and longer? | | | | | | | | |
| classifier agrees with the keys? | | | | | | | | |

- Roll from one direction to the next without stopping (W → W+D → D → S+D
  …). Does the gait turn with you, or snap at the diagonals?
- Tap and release. Does the body settle to the rest pose cleanly, or hitch
  on the way down? Start again from rest: does the first stride pop?
- Sprint into a stop. Does anything overshoot?
- `peak` after a minute of this, at your frame rate: ______ rad. (For
  reference, a walking knee at 60 fps peaks near 0.17; at 30 fps near 0.33.)
- Verdict on natural: does it read as walking and running, or as a
  mannequin being moved?

### 3. Crouch (T-2.20)

- Crouch standing still. Is it readable at a glance from third person, and
  on a remote soldier at range?
- Crouch-walk in a few directions. Is the gait the crouch with a walk on it,
  or does the body stand up to walk?
- Stand up again. Any pop?
- Hide behind a crate crouched: does it hide you? Behind the low wall
  crouched, your head shows above it by design (crouched height 1.2 m, wall
  1.0 m). Is that the right height for a crouch, or should it be lower?
- Is crouch *useful*: is there a reason to be in it, at these speeds and
  this height?

### 4. Jump (T-1.11, on the new body)

- Jump on the spot and at a run. Does the body read airborne and landed, or
  does it walk in the air? (The gait keeps going in the air today; say
  whether that reads wrong.)
- Step onto the slab (0.4 m) at a walk: a step, no jump, no vault. Does the
  body's height change read as a step?

### 5. Vault (T-2.21, T-2.23)

Vault targets: the low wall (1.0 m), any crate (1.2 m, you land on top),
the block from the slab (0.8 m above the slab). The vault starts when you
walk or sprint at the obstacle pushing forward; it refuses from a crouch,
while firing, while jumping, and when you approach at more than 45°.

- Walk into the low wall. Does the vault start where you expect, or a step
  early or late?
- Sprint into it. Same question, and does the sprint-to-vault read as one
  movement?
- Approach at an angle, crouched, and with the trigger held: each should
  refuse and stop you at the wall. Does the refusal read as "you cannot
  vault like that" or as "the game ignored me"?
- Stand still touching the wall and press `W` from rest.
- Vault a crate: you land on top. Does landing on it read right, and does
  walking off the far side?
- Timing: the traversal is 0.55 s and cannot be cancelled once started.
  Does it feel controllable — could you choose not to, and does it end when
  you expect? If the number is wrong, which way, and try it on the slider.
- The body: does the lead leg go over first, the trailing leg follow, the
  chest lean into it, and the landing dip read as weight arriving? Any snap
  at the start or the end? Press `T` before a vault and read `peak`
  after: ______ rad at ______ fps.
- The camera through the vault: it follows the feet up and over. Does that
  read fine, or does the rise need smoothing?
- Firing: the trigger is refused mid-vault and works again on landing. Does
  the refusal ever eat a shot you meant?

### 6. Crawl and revive (T-2.13, T-2.14, T-2.15)

Needs the second person to down you.

- Downed, crawl in a few directions. Does the crawl read as crawling on the
  new body, and is the direction readable to your teammate?
- Get revived. Does the body come back up cleanly?
- (E-2.6's feel is signed off; this is only whether the new body kept it.)

### 7. The other person, over the host

Watch the other person do sections 2 to 6 while you stand still, then swap.
Then set the link to Poor, then Awful, on the panel, and watch again.

- Do their feet slide, or plant? Does their gait rate match how fast they
  are actually moving?
- Do their direction changes read on your screen as they felt on theirs?
- Their crouch, jump and vault: do they read at all, and do they read at
  about the right moment? (The vault is the same authoritative state on
  both screens; a vault that starts on theirs must start on yours.)
- Under Poor and Awful: does the body ever snap, or does it only lag?
