## What this is judging

T-2.40 through T-2.42 added a voluntary prone stance (ADR-016): a standing,
alive soldier chooses to drop to it, crawls slowly in it, fires from it, and
chooses to stand back up. **It is not the downed state.** The downed crawl
B-05 removed stays removed, and a downed soldier still lies still and cannot
fire. The three questions the gate asks (PLAN.md §7.8):

1. Does prone read as clearly different from **both** crouch and the downed
   pose, on your own screen and on the other person's?
2. Does the prone crawl speed feel **earned rather than crippling**?
3. Is firing from prone **usable, not a curiosity**?

## Before you start

- `Z` toggles prone; `C` toggles crouch. The two are exclusive: `Z` from
  crouch goes prone, `Z` again stands; `C` from prone goes to crouch.
  `Shift` (sprint) or `Space` stands up out of either — `Space` pressed to
  stand is only a stand, not a jump. Holding sprint and pressing `Z` goes
  prone (sprint is ignored while prone). You cannot jump or vault while
  prone. `RMB` aims, `LMB` fires, `H` hides the HUD, `N` the netgraph, `T`
  resets the counters, including the pose-step peak.
- **The HUD lines are this sheet's instruments.** `locomotion <state> …`
  should read `prone` whenever you are prone, moving or not — if it reads
  `crouch-walk`, `idle` or anything else while you are down, that is a bug,
  not a feel note. `pose step <rad> peak <rad>` is the largest joint
  rotation in the last frame and since `T`; a snap is about a radian in one
  frame. Press `T`, change stance, read the peak.
- **The numbers you are judging** (defaults; all data, not code):

  | | standing | crouch | prone |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | body height | 1.8 m | 1.2 m | 0.8 m |
  | move speed | 4.2 walk / 6.8 sprint m/s | 1.9 m/s | 1.1 m/s |
  | camera eye | 1.55 m | 1.15 m | 0.4 m (downed: 0.55 m) |
  | gun trace leaves from | 1.55 m | **1.55 m (B-09)** | 0.4 m |

  Spread while prone is the weapon's cone times its `proneSpreadScale`:
  carbine 0.6, marksman 0.5, breacher 0.85, sidearm 0.8. Recoil is the
  same in every stance (T-2.42 did not tune it).
- **The range, from spawn.** You spawn on the line at z = −6 facing north.
  The 1 m **low wall** is ahead-left (x −12..−6, z −1). The **crates** are
  1.2 m cubes, two at the far left (x −9 and −10.4, z 9) and one at the
  right (x 7, z −3). The long walls at z = 4 are 2.4 m, with a doorway in
  the west one at x −9..−7.8. **What that means for prone:** both the low
  wall and the crates are taller than a prone soldier (0.8 m) *and* taller
  than the prone eye (0.4 m), so prone behind them hides you completely and
  **you cannot shoot over them from prone** — only around their ends. That
  is geometry, not a bug. There is no knee-high cover on the range to
  shoot over from prone, and no low ceiling to crawl under; say so in the
  verdict.
- **Known, not for this gate:** B-09 — a *crouched* soldier's shot still
  leaves from standing eye height, and throws (grenade, rocket) leave from
  standing eye height in every stance, **prone included**. Note it if you
  hit it, but it is logged and does not fail E-2.8 by itself.
- **Sliders.** The movement panel has a **Prone** speed slider next to walk,
  sprint and crouch; the weapon panel has **Cone prone ×** per weapon. On
  the in-page session the server and predictor share the config, so a
  slider moves both; on the host only your prediction moves, so tune
  in-page and confirm on the host. Record any change in Tuning.

## The questions

A line each is enough; "fine" is a valid answer, "did not try" is a
required one.

### 1. Getting down and getting up (T-2.40, T-2.41)

Press `T` first. From standing: `Z`, wait, `Z`. Then standing → `C` → `Z`
→ `C` → `Space`. Then sprint and press `Z` mid-sprint.

- Does the drop to prone read as a soldier choosing to get down, or as the
  body snapping to a new pose? Same for standing back up.
- Does the camera's ease down to the prone eye feel right — speed of the
  drop, and the height it lands at? Any lurch at the end?
- Crouch ↔ prone both ways: does it read as one level lower / higher, or as
  standing up in between?
- Sprint into `Z`: does it read as diving down, or as stopping dead and
  then lying down? Is that the right thing for it to be?
- `peak` after all of the above: ______ rad at ______ fps.
- Does `Space` from prone ever jump when you meant to stand, or stand when
  you meant nothing?

### 2. Prone vs crouch vs downed — does it read? (T-2.41)

This is the gate's first question, and the one the downed-crawl removal
makes sharp: a player must never mistake a prone teammate for a downed one.

- Your own soldier, third person, prone and still, then crouched and
  still, from front, side and behind. Obviously different?
- Prone: is the rifle in hand and pointing where you aim? (Downed has no
  weapon; that is the tell. Does the tell read?)
- Get the second person to down you, and look at each other: one prone, one
  downed, side by side, then at 10 m, then at 30 m. Can you tell which is
  which without the HUD? At what range does it stop being obvious?
- Is anything visibly wrong with the prone body — a limb through the
  ground, a hand off the weapon, the rifle through the torso, feet floating?

### 3. The crawl (T-2.40, T-2.41)

Press `T`. Crawl each of the eight directions for a few seconds. Read the
`locomotion` line and `peak`.

| | forward | fwd-right | right | back-right | back | back-left | left | fwd-left |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| body reads as crawling that way? | | | | | | | | |
| classifier reads `prone`? | | | | | | | | |

- Crawl from spawn to the low wall (about 5 m). Time it roughly: ______ s.
  Walk the same distance standing for comparison: ______ s.
- **Earned or crippling?** Is 1.1 m/s slow enough that going prone is a
  real decision, and fast enough that you would ever move in it rather
  than stand, move, and drop again? If it is wrong, which way — try the
  **Prone** slider and write down the value that felt right.
- Turning while prone (mouse yaw): does the body turn with you, or skate?
- Tap and release while prone. Does the body settle cleanly?
- `peak` after a minute of crawling: ______ rad at ______ fps.

### 4. Cover (T-2.40)

- Crawl up behind the low wall and behind a crate. Are you hidden, as far
  as you can tell from your own camera? Ask the second person, from the far
  side: can they see any part of you?
- Crawl out from behind the end of the low wall and back. Does "into cover"
  and "out of cover" read, for you and for them?
- Stand up in the open: it should just stand. (There is no ceiling on
  the range to refuse the rise, so ceiling rejection is not judged here.)
- Is there anywhere on this range where prone is *better* than crouch, or
  does the range not give it a reason to exist? (That is a range answer,
  not a prone answer — say which.)

### 5. Firing from prone (T-2.42)

Use at least the carbine and the marksman. Target: the second person
standing, then crouched, at 10 m and 30 m in the open; then from prone at
the end of the low wall, shooting around it.

- Does the trigger ever refuse while prone? (It must not. Downed still
  refuses, and should.)
- Aim (`RMB`) prone: does the view and the reticle read as steady, and is
  the tighter cone noticeable? Carbine ×0.6, marksman ×0.5.
- Do hits land where the reticle says? The shot leaves from 0.4 m, not the
  standing eye — does anything about the impact point surprise you, near
  or far?
- **Usable or a curiosity?** Is there a situation on this range where you
  would choose to lie down to shoot? If the prone cone bonus is too big or
  too small, try the **Cone prone ×** slider and record it.
- Recoil is untuned for prone. Does that feel wrong — too much kick for a
  soldier on the ground — or fine?
- Grenade or rocket from prone (B-09: leaves from standing eye height). Just
  note whether it is noticeable; it does not fail this gate.

### 6. The other person, over the host

Watch the other person do sections 1 to 5 while you stand still, then swap.
Then restart the host with a poor link and watch again — the page's link
panel is greyed out against a host, so the conditions go on the host:
`LINK_LATENCY_MS=80 LINK_JITTER_MS=15 LINK_LOSS=0.05 pnpm host` (Poor), then
`LINK_LATENCY_MS=200 LINK_JITTER_MS=40 LINK_LOSS=0.2 pnpm host` (Awful).

- Their drop to prone and rise: do they read on your screen, and at about
  the right moment? Does their prone stance ever flicker back to standing
  or crouch on your screen when it did not on theirs?
- Their crawl: do the arms and legs move at a rate that matches how fast
  they are actually moving, or do they slide?
- Their gun when prone: pointing where they are aiming?
- Shoot them while they are prone: do hits register on the low body where
  you see it, and not in the air above it where a standing soldier would
  be? Any shot you were sure hit that did not, or vice versa?
- Under Poor and Awful: does the prone body ever snap, or only lag?
