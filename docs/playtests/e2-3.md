## What this is judging

T-2.25 through T-2.28 put four procedural layers on the rig, each applied
after the gait every frame and none of them moving anything authoritative
(not the hit capsule, not the traced aim, not the muzzle the tracers leave):
**aim offsets** (the spine, neck and rifle pitch to where the soldier looks,
hands re-solved onto the grips), **fire and reload** (each shot kicks the
rifle back and up; a reload dips the muzzle and takes the left hand to the
magazine well and back), **hit reactions** (the chest turns away from the
shooter, the head snaps on a head hit, scaled by damage), and **foot
placement** (each foot on what is under it, hips settled to the lower foot).
The gate asks, of each person watching the other (PLAN.md §7.5):

1. Is the rifle **pointing where they are looking**?
2. Does a **burst look like a burst**?
3. Does a **reload read as one without a HUD**?
4. Does a **hit land on the body**?
5. Do the **feet stand on the world**?

And: **tune the layers' numbers while the feel is in hand.**

## The questions

A line each is enough; "fine" is a valid answer, "did not try" is a
required one. Each of you does each section; the other watches from about
10 m, then 30 m, from the front and the side.

### 1. Aim offsets (T-2.25)

Warm up in the page first: third person, look slowly from the ground to
the sky and back while standing, then crouched, then walking.

- Your own body: does the rifle stay on the crosshair's line through the
  whole range? Does the torso lean into a steep aim, or does it bend at an
  odd joint? Do the hands stay on the rifle at the extremes?
- On the host, watch the other person look up and down, slowly then fast.
  **Is the rifle pointing where they are looking?** At 30 m, can you tell
  *roughly* where (ground at your feet, you, the sky)?
- Does their pitch arrive smoothly, or step? On a conditioned host (see
  Before you start), does it lag, snap, or wander?
- Vault the low wall looking steeply up: the aim should fade out through
  the vault and back in after. Any pop? `peak` ______ rad.
- Downed: the aim layer is off. Does the downed body read as having let go?

### 2. Fire (T-2.26)

Carbine: single shots, then short bursts, then a whole magazine held. Then
the marksman and the sidearm, single shots. Hip, then aimed.

- Your own body in third person: does each shot move the rifle and the
  chest, and does a held burst build and settle rather than jitter?
- **Does a burst look like a burst** on the other person, at 10 m and at
  30 m? Can you tell a single shot from a burst without the sound?
- Marksman vs carbine: does the heavier kick read as heavier?
- Aimed vs hip (the kick is ×`recoilAdsScale` aimed): visible difference?
- Does the kick ever put the rifle through the body or a hand off it?
- Do tracers still leave the muzzle you see, or visibly somewhere else?
  (The layer moves the picture only; the tracer's muzzle is untouched.)

### 3. Reload (T-2.26)

In the page press `R`; on the host, fire each gun **dry** and let the
server's reload run.

- Your own: does the muzzle dip and the left hand go to the magazine well
  and back? Does it end with both hands on the grips, no snap?
- **Does a reload read as one without a HUD?** Watching the other person
  (press `H` to hide your readouts): could you tell they were reloading,
  and roughly when they were done — the moment to push?
- Is 2.1 s (carbine) read as the same length on your screen as theirs?
- Reload while walking, while crouched, into a vault: anything wrong?
- On a conditioned host: does the reload start late, stutter, or restart?

### 4. Hit reactions (T-2.27)

Shoot the other person (and be shot) with the carbine: torso from the
front, from their left, from their right, from behind; then head shots;
then the marksman's torso shot. Friendly fire is on.

- **Does a hit land on the body?** A round from the front tilts the chest
  back, from behind forward, from their side turns it away from you. Does
  each read as the right way for where you shot from? (The damage is after
  range falloff, so a carbine hit past 22 m reacts less.)
- A head hit: does the head snap more than the body under it, and does it
  read as a head hit rather than a bigger body hit?
- Carbine torso (22, half size) vs marksman torso (58, full size): does the
  difference read? Is either too big, too small, too twitchy?
- A burst into them: does each round visibly land, or does it blur into
  one lean? (By design each hit restarts the reaction; it does not stack.)
- Taking hits yourself, third person: does it read, and does it ever get
  in the way of aiming? (It must not move your aim.)
- Downed: no reaction. Does a round into a downed body look right with none?

### 5. Foot placement (T-2.28)

Press `T`. Walk to the slab and stand on its west edge (about x 7.5, z 8)
so one boot is on it and one off; then the south edge; then walk slowly on
and off it in each direction. Then stand at the block's edge on the slab.

- Standing half on the slab: does one boot rest on it and the other on the
  ground, the hips lowered between them and the knee bent? Anything
  floating or sunk? Readout: `L` ______  `R` ______  `hips` ______.
- **Do the feet stand on the world** as the other person watches you do
  the same? At 10 m? At 30 m, does anyone notice?
- Stepping on and off: does it slide, or pop? `peak` after a minute of it:
  ______ rad at ______ fps (the walk's own was 0.168 at 60).
- Flat ground after the slab: do the readouts return to `L 0.00  R 0.00`?
- The block's edge: one foot hangs short of the slab (0.8 m is beyond the
  0.45 m reach). Acceptable, or does it look broken?
- If there is time: the lobby's **Kit gallery** map (or `?kit` in the page)
  has a stair to a roof. Feet on the steps, going up and down?

### 6. All at once

Five minutes of a proper exchange across the range: cover, peeking,
bursts, reloads behind cover, hits, a down and a revive (hold `E`).

- Does the body tell you what the other person is doing — aiming at you,
  firing, reloading, hit — without looking at the HUD?
- Anything that read wrong only when layers combined (a kick during a
  reload, a hit during a vault, aiming up while on the slab's edge)?
